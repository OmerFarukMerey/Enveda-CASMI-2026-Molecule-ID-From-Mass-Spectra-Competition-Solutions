## 0. Verdict

**s1v is not a submission candidate; `casmi26-sub-s1v` v1 is not proposed.** Two pre-registered gates say no:
- the T0 stop rule fires: inserting the rows at a fixed engine-2 position 4 costs 0.027 MRR on s1val's real lists;
- the T2 cost gate fails on the rows' real positions: -0.048, with 3 wins against 46 losses.

The T3 benefit gate passes on the new dB arm, where every answer is PubChem-only: +0.100, 113 wins against 29 losses. The mechanism
is real, but it is not free. It pays off only if PubChem-only answers make up more than about 32% of the hidden set [I]. Section 5
explains why that number is not usable as it stands.

## 1. What s1v does [C]

Engine 2 scores the first k = 5 rows that the PubChem channel interleaves, for molecules whose channel is open. It uses its own fitted
rankers, scores the rows jointly with each candidate window, and inserts them into its list at the position their blend gives them.
With vote-only, a row is never placed at position 0. The rows then get the second fusion vote that PubChemLite structures already get.
T1, the commit run, was healthy: its sha equals s1's and pass 2 had dev 0.0.

## 4. Mechanics [V]

- **Engine 2 rates the channel's rows highly.** Pass 2 put 395 of 1,669 T2 rows and 506 of 1,345 T3 rows at position 0. In T3 it put
  the true answer row at position 0 in 119 of the 201 molecules where that row was an interleave row. In T2 none of the rows can be the
  answer (s1val PREREG section 1), and 1,246 of 1,669 still land in positions 0-5.
- **The cost comes from the extra vote at the top.** In T2, 32 of the 46 losses move the answer from rank 1 to 3 or from 2 to 3: a
  channel row with two votes overtakes the answer.
- **On T3 the vote helps strongly.** Most wins come from molecules whose answer is an interleave row.

## 5. Reading [I]

- **Break-even.** If p is the hidden share of answers that are PubChem-only and reachable, s1v pays when 0.100 p > 0.048 (1 - p),
  that is p > 0.32.
- **Why that number is not usable.** Both sets use structures the networks were trained on, so the absolute MRR is inflated and so are
  the deltas on both sides. On the hidden set, the T3-type molecules would also have weaker channel recall. The pre-registered gates
  say not to rely on a mixture argument, and this review does not.
- **Where a gate could help.** In T3 the vote helps when the answer is the interleave row. In T2 it hurts mainly when engine 2 lifts
  a row that is not the answer above an answer at rank 1-2. A gated vote might keep most of T3 and avoid most of T2, for example only
  at engine-2 position 0, only on the aggressive channel branch, or only when engine 1's top row is weak. This is a **post-hoc idea**.
  It would need a new pre-registration and can be measured offline from these two dumps, which hold `eng0` and the pass-2 entries.
- **The dB arm is reusable.** `casmi26-val-s1v-t3` and its replay are the first real-list harness where PubChem-channel changes can
  show a benefit.

## 6. Gated s1v (PREREG section 6, fixed before any gated number) [V]

Two gates were pre-registered and replayed offline from the T2 / T3 dumps (`scratch/s1v/replay_gates.py`). A gate passes only if all
three hold:
- on T2, delta >= -0.002 and losses <= wins + 3;
- on T3, delta > 0 and the CI lower bound > 0;
- the break-even PubChem-only share p* = -d2 / (d3 - d2) is at most 0.10.

| gate | T2 delta [CI], w/l | T3 delta [CI], w/l | p* | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G0: vote only for rows pass 2 put at engine-2 position 0 (inserted at 1) | -0.0320 [-0.0462, -0.0196], 3/31 | +0.0608 [+0.0389, +0.0835], 60/16 | 0.345 | FAIL (T2, p*) |
| G1: ungated rule only in cell 44's aggressive branch (T2 4 / T3 52 molecules) | -0.0029 [-0.0075, 0.0000], 0/2 | +0.0188 [+0.0036, +0.0351], 16/5 | 0.134 | FAIL (T2 delta, p*) |

Neither gate passes, so nothing was implemented or built and no Kaggle run was made.
- **G0** keeps about 60% of the T3 gain but also about two thirds of the T2 cost. A position-0 row is exactly the kind that overtakes
  rank-1 answers.
- **G1** is nearly neutral on pool answers, but it reaches too few molecules to pay its own small cost below a 10% share.
