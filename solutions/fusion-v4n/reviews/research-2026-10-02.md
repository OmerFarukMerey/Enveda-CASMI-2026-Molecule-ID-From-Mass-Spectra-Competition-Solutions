With the public arrays the PubChem-channel prior costs 0.007 to 0.018 and hides the GLACIER gain. This matches the working
hypothesis in `EXPERIMENTS.md` (v5c 0.405 vs v4b 0.409). GLACIER `[M+H]+` only is the positive part: +0.007 to +0.010.

Why the GLACIER restriction can matter: both public runners (`gl_runner.py`, `ice_runner.py`) score spectra whose adduct is
`[M+H]+` or `[M+Na]+` in positive mode and average the entropy similarity over a molecule's covered spectra. The v27/v29
change removes the `[M+Na]+` spectra from GLACIER's average, and removes GLACIER entirely for molecules without an `[M+H]+`
spectrum. ICEBERG still scores both adducts in every public notebook.
