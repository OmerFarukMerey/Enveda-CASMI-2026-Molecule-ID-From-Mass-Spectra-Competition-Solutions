## 0. Bottom line

- **No silent answer-dropping bug of public-answer size exists in stack1's filters, caps, keys or tolerances.** Every suspect was
  located and counted; the two real keying defects found (F1K, F1) are genuine bugs but move < 0.2 public answers each, and fail the
  pre-registered criterion. **Nothing to build as a single-switch patch.**
- **The 7-8% "other database" (DB) loss is a local artefact**: all 47 / 47 DB lists (22 molecules) are non-timsTOF spectra whose
  labelled adduct / precursor puts the answer outside +-10 ppm (e.g. -1,009 ppm, +69 ppm). On Enveda timsTOF data the chain's target is
  within +-10 ppm for **0 misses in 183,191 structures** (enveda-180 + np-examples, range -1.9 to +6.0 ppm) and 290 / 290 local timsTOF
  molecules; the hidden test is all timsTOF and drops precursor-inconsistent spectra. 15 of the 29 "in no database" lists are the same
  artefact.
- **The 12% CUT loss is not a cap problem**: the dropped answers sit deep (PubChem channel row median 9, engine-2 row median 32, base
  rank median 40-45, fused position median 50); un-capping puts them at ranks >= 20 (RR <= 0.05). F2 (all 25 base rows into the fusion)
  measured +0.0006 in both arms.
- **The 8-20% PCO loss is ranking, not filtering**: the 5,000-row first pass costs <= 2 of 191 (m) / 0 of 51 (u) answers; the rest are
  ordered out of the 25 by f.z + popularity (in arm m mainly because their popularity is masked).

## 3. The two keying bugs (real, small)

- **F1K** (row 8): the forward scores are stored per SMILES string; the final re-rank (`gl_fuse.rerank_multi`) falls back to
  `score_key`, which the dicts never use. An engine-2 row whose identity is at base rank 21-60 is sent to the simulators as the base
  SMILES and read back as engine 2's SMILES: z = 0 instead of its GLACIER z. Fix = complete the dicts by metric identity (no model call).
  962 / 971 lists change order, but only 54 / 48 answer reciprocal ranks move (24/30, 24/24).
- **F1** (row 9): the '|gen' suffix keeps a pool row out of the simulators when its only same-formula base neighbours are generated,
  yet cell 34 re-ranks it among those generated isomers with z = 0. In the debugged case (mx c1 h_6b83dd20a6) the answer is the only
  pool row among 52 same-formula candidates and the only unscored one; it stays first because its fused score dominates.
- Both are fixed in `scratch/audit1017/au_replay.py` (variants F1K, F1) by substitution in the notebook's own cell source; neither is
  proposed: they move about 0.1 public answer, the gain sits in dA / dB lists (the class the harness over-rewards, and the PubChem-row
  class the replay mispredicted twice, v71b / v73a), and the pool-answer lists are flat to negative.
