# A learned verifier for joined rows: quality, two exposure-aware uses, transfer (2026-10-05)

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed or committed, no `kaggle` command was run, the visible `test.parquet` was
not read, no NIST data, no existing file was edited. Trained here: logistic regressions and 200-tree LightGBM models on
22,000 rows (seconds each, CPU). GPU: inference only, the existing runners on the Enveda panel, one process at a time
(GLACIER 0.44 GB, 704 s; ICEBERG at most 1.79 GB, 1,260 s; `gpushare` duty 0.8, no game detected). At most three CPU
processes, below-normal priority. Scripts: `scratch/verifier/` (git-ignored). Outputs: `D:\casmi-data\verifier\` (153 MB,
all new). New repo files: `prototypes/join_verifier.py`, `prototypes/test_join_verifier.py`, this file; `INDEX.md` was
not regenerated.

**Every number below was computed in this study** unless a file is named. MRR = MRR@25 on the metric key; "±" is one
standard error of a paired per-molecule difference; (wins / losses) in brackets. Setting: the mixed regime and the full
replay of `reviews/fwd-replay-2026-10-05.md` (380 hold-out molecules; `c1` 150, `c2` 380, `dA` / `dB` the 325 with a
PubChem record; N 100, join gate `lib_max` < 0.7, public ranker, fusion config of v13a, real ICEBERG / GLACIER scores
masked to what `nb/07` would have scored). **Exposure** on the final lists of `c2` / `c1`: lists whose first row entered
only through the join ("joined first"), lists with a joined row anywhere above the answer ("above"), joined rows in the
first 25 per list. v13a: `c2` 25 / 54 / 9.23, `c1` 6 / 12 / 1.77; MRR `c1` 0.8223, `c2` 0.7339, `dA` 0.5016, `dB` 0.3096
(identical to the fwd-replay review). **No leaderboard estimate is given.**

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The verifier ranks well and separates badly where it matters.** Row AUC 0.980 (answer against every other joined
   row), within-list AUC 0.973. At list level ("the best-scored joined row of this list is the answer") 0.855, and
   against the first joined row of pool-answer lists 0.90; the ranker's own gap to the best pool row gives 0.77 and
   0.85 on the same lists. Logistic regression and LightGBM are the same within the spread of the repeats.
2. **The forward models add nothing at list level.** Without ICEBERG / GLACIER features: list AUC 0.849, against
   pool-answer lists 0.899 (with them 0.855 / 0.897). They help inside a list (within-list AUC 0.960 → 0.973; the
   best-scored joined row is the answer in 97 → 103 of 154 lists). The confidence-gate probability adds nothing
   either (0.857 without it).
3. **PubChem popularity of the row is a leak and was removed.** With it the list AUC is 0.917 and 129 of 154 answers
   are the best-scored row: the hold-out answers are famous pool structures (median popularity 4.2 against 1.1 for
   joined decoys); a hidden PubChem-only answer is not. No reported model uses it.
4. **(a) Demotion fails on the answer-removed side, at every setting.** 16 settings of "rows with p below t go behind
   the k-th pool row or are dropped", 10 of "only the list's first row": exposure falls (`c2` joined first 25 → 21 to
   5) and `dB` loses 0.014 to 0.12, always beyond one standard error; the mildest is −0.0137 ± 0.0053 (9 / 17) for
   25 → 20.6 lists. `c2` gains at most +0.007 ± 0.004, never beyond two standard errors. The reason is recall near
   the top: 29% of the joined answers have an out-of-fold p below 0.1.
5. **(b) Promotion at a high-precision threshold is exposure-neutral and small.** The rows above the threshold are
   mostly already first in the base list, so only the fusion credit for that one row moves anything: `dB`
   +0.0077 ± 0.0036 to +0.0097 ± 0.0042 (5 to 7 wins, 1 to 3 losses), `dA` +0.002 to +0.003, `c2` and `c1` unchanged,
   joined first 25.0 to 25.4. That is at the edge of the stated test (two standard errors, six molecules), not a result.
   Lower thresholds gain more on `dB` and raise the exposure (precision 0.7: 27.4 lists, `c2` −0.0021 ± 0.0012).
6. **On the fine-tuned selection (v22a lists) one family passes the test by the letter against v13a**: demote p < 0.03
   behind the 10th pool row, promote with credit at precision 0.8: `dB` +0.0256 ± 0.0119 (51 / 38), `dA`
   +0.0102 ± 0.0095, `c2` −0.0031 ± 0.0043, `c1` +0.0011 ± 0.0007; `c2` joined first 23.4, in the first 25 8.53, `c1`
   3.4 (three verifier variants: +0.022 to +0.026 at 2.1 to 2.2 standard errors). It is the best of about 110
   settings, the `dB` gain is v22a's own (+0.030) carried through, and lists with a joined row above the answer rise
   54 → 56. Not proposed.
7. **Transfer.** Second hold set (441 joined lists, 231 joined answers, no forward scores): the ranking transfers
   (list AUC 0.87, as in CV inside that set), the high-precision end does not: at p ≥ 0.7 precision 0.75 with two
   pool-answer lists promoted (regime CV: 0.91, none). Enveda timsTOF panel (v1 engine and both forward models run
   here, 161 joined lists, 126 joined answers): list AUC 0.89, precision 1.00 at p ≥ 0.7 (20 lists), promotion at the
   regime's threshold `dB` +0.026 ± 0.009 (9 / 0) with `c2` untouched; demotion is the same trade as in the regime
   (`dB` −0.007 to −0.05, `c2` +0.004 to +0.031). The panel replay has no engine-2 stage.
8. **Recommendation: no.** A verifier does not buy lower exposure without giving back the answer-removed gain, and
   its one exposure-neutral use is worth under 0.01 on `dB` locally. The prototype exists (promotion only by default,
   demotion off) so that the numbers can be reproduced; it is not wired in and no config was added.

first 60 cannot reach the output and have no forward scores; three of 157 joined answers of v13a are there.

Rows without a forward score are in the 74 molecules the runners do not cover and in formula groups of one. Training
rows: `c1` + `c2` + `dB` (21,925 rows, 154 positives for v13a). `dA` rows are scored by the fold model of their
molecule.

**Features** (`vf_lib.py`; 57 in the set "core"), all from one molecule's base entry, PubChem list, forward-score
dicts and query spectra:

- ranker: position, score, list z-score, gap to the list's first row, to the best pool row (raw and in list standard
  deviations), to the best joined row, to the next joined row; rank among joined rows; pool rows above; joined rows
  in the first 60 / 25; pool rows in the first 60; list spread; best pool score; first row is joined; margins of the
  first two pool rows and of the first two joined rows;
- formula: same formula as the list's first row / as the first pool row; size of the row's formula group, pool rows
  in it (count, any, above the row);
- selection model: rank, log rank, f.z z-score among the first 100, gap to the next row and to the first row, margin
  of the first two rows, **all in units of the spread of the first 100 f.z** (the f.z scale differs between models:
  about 18 for the fold-safe model, 570 for A+B in `hardneg\sel\np_A+B_lists.json`); a second selection model's rank
  as an optional set ("sel2");
- ICEBERG and GLACIER, each inside the row's same-formula group of the first 60: score, rank, rank fraction, margin
  to the best other row, z-score, gap to the best pool row and to the best other joined row, "is the best", "has a
  score"; plus the sum of the two z-scores, the smaller of the two pool gaps, "best in both";
- list level: `lib_max`, number of spectra, number of adducts, an [M+H]+ spectrum exists, negative-mode spectra,
  `conf_gate.p_present` of the run without injection.

Not used: the label, the type, anything keyed to the molecule; PubChem popularity (bottom line 3); engine row
features such as `best_tan` (set "ext", reported only as a sensitivity: the regime inflates them,
`reviews/join-rank-2026-10-05.md`). The confidence probability needs a second engine pass in the notebook; set
"noconf" drops it and is what the prototype uses.

## 3. Two uses in the full replay, against v13a (task 3)

`replay_vf.py`. The verifier acts on the base entry before stage A of `fusion_core` (after `nb/07` has the forward
scores). Each variant is replayed once per CV repeat with that repeat's out-of-fold scores; reciprocal ranks are
averaged over the five repeats per molecule before the paired difference, exposure counts are means over repeats.

- **demote(t, k)**: joined rows with p < t that stand above the k-th non-joined row move to just behind it; "drop":
  removed from the base entry and from the PubChem list of the slot merge.
- **top-demote(t, k)**: only while the list's first row is a joined row with p < t.
- **promote(T)**: the best-scored joined row, if at or above the nested threshold for precision T, goes to the first
  place; "credit": it also gets `FUSE_INJ_CREDIT` 1.0 in the engine-2 fusion (pool id −2 for this row only).

## 4. Why: the score of the rows that matter

`curve.py`, repeat-averaged out-of-fold p, logistic core, v13a lists. Of 262 answer-removed base lists 70 have the
answer first and 88 a joined decoy first; 43 `c2` / `c1` base lists have a joined decoy first; 138 joined rows stand
above a pool answer ("harmful") in 37 lists.

| p below | joined answers (154) | answers that are first in their list (70) | decoys first in a `c2` / `c1` list (43) | decoys first in a `dB` list (88) | harmful rows (138) | pool lists with every harmful row below (37) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.01 | 10 | 0 | 2 | 5 | 87 | 6 |
| 0.03 | 21 | 1 | 15 | 14 | 111 | 15 |
| 0.1 | 45 | 5 | 25 | 40 | 121 | 21 |
| 0.3 | 84 | 13 | 38 | 68 | 132 | 31 |
| 0.5 | 105 | 26 | 42 | 76 | 137 | 36 |
| 0.7 | 117 | 35 | 43 | 81 | 138 | 37 |

AUC of the answers that are first against the decoys that are first on a pool list: 0.912 (LightGBM 0.926; ranker gap
to the best pool row alone 0.837; score alone 0.798). Joined answers against harmful rows: 0.888 (ranker gap 0.750).
Median p: answers 0.21, answers that are first 0.70, decoys first on a pool list 0.09.

- **Demotion breaks on the answers that are not first.** A threshold that clears the harmful rows of half the
  exposed pool lists (0.1: 21 of 37) also demotes 45 of 154 joined answers; they stand at positions 2 to 10 with
  small margins, where the model cannot tell them from decoys, and they fall behind pool rows. Restricting demotion
  to the first row avoids that and still loses (`dB` −0.017 at 0.1): the 5 demoted answers cost more than the
  exposed lists return, because removing the joined decoy from the top of a pool list rarely puts the answer there.
- **Promotion breaks on headroom, not on precision.** At or above 0.7 there are 35 of the 70 first answers and no pool-list
  decoy; those rows are already first. The answers that would gain from a lift (84 with p below 0.3) are the ones
  the verifier does not recognise.

## 6. The prototype (task 5)

The test of task 3 is met by the letter only at the edge (section 3), so nothing was wired in. Because one family
did meet it, the module asked for under that condition exists, with the conservative use as its default:

- `solutions/fusion-v4n/prototypes/join_verifier.py`: numpy only. `row_features(...)` (the 56 features of the set
  "noconf": no second engine pass), `MODEL` (fixed constants of the logistic fit on the 21,925 v13a rows; the fit on
  the v22a rows is in `D:\casmi-data\verifier\model_v22a_noconf.json` and can be passed as `M`), `p_answer`,
  `demote`, `promote`, `apply`. Defaults: demotion off, promotion at p ≥ 0.70.
- `prototypes/test_join_verifier.py`: 7 tests without data (feature shapes and identities, no joined rows, missing
  forward scores, missing PubChem list, invariance to the f.z scale, "a demoted row never moves up and pool rows
  keep their order", one promoted row tagged −2, `apply` does not mutate). All pass.
- `scratch/verifier/parity.py`: on the real lists (522 of v13a, 521 of v22a; 21,098 and 21,151 joined rows) the
  module's features equal the study's exactly, p differs by at most 1.0e-5 (constants rounded to 6 digits), and
  `apply` returns the order of the study's functions in every list.

**Where it would hook in.** After `nb/07_rescore.py` has the ICEBERG / GLACIER dicts and before
`fusion_core.build_submission(BASE, ...)`: for each molecule whose `BASE[mid]` holds a pool id −2,
`BASE[mid] = join_verifier.apply(BASE[mid], row_features(...))`, and `FUSE_INJ_CREDIT = 1.0` in the fusion config.
`apply` leaves pool id −2 on the promoted row only and re-tags every other joined row −1, so the existing credit code
of `fusion_core` reaches that row alone; `popularity_reorder` treats −1 and −2 alike. `nb/06_base_lists.py` would have
to dump, per molecule, four numbers it already has in `sub` (spectra, distinct adducts, an [M+H]+ spectrum, negative-
mode spectra) and nothing else: the PubChem list's keys and f.z are in `PC_DEEP[mid]`, the entry is `BASE[mid]`. No
change to `fusion_core.py` is needed. The other agent's uncommitted `JOIN_FWD_A` flag in `fusion_core.py` was left
alone and is off in every replay here.

## 7. Limits, and what failed or could not be verified

- **The models the notebook runs are not the models the verifier was fitted on.** Engine and PubChem selection use
  the fold-safe FPNet locally and the public A+B models on Kaggle; ranker scores, margins and the f.z shape of joined
  rows may differ there, and A+B cannot be used locally (they have seen every molecule). The f.z features are scale-
  free; nothing else was done about this. A fixed-coefficient verifier on Kaggle is unverified.
- **Leaks, unchanged from the earlier reviews**: the hold-out answers are known structures with library relatives
  (the ranker's score for a joined answer is optimistic) and the forward models have very likely seen them. The
  popularity leak was found and removed; another of the same kind may remain unnoticed.
- **Selection on the evaluation lists.** Demotion thresholds and k, and the fixed promotion thresholds, are grids
  read on the lists they are evaluated on; only the precision-target thresholds are nested. About 55 settings per
  verifier variant on v13a and 55 on v22a were looked at; a best-of-110 at 2.2 standard errors is not evidence.
- **Small counts.** 154 joined answers, 43 exposed pool lists, 20 joined `c1` lists; the promotion results are 5 to 9
  molecules. Differences under 0.01 on `dB` are not resolved.
- **The regime does not predict the leaderboard** for changes in the number or rank of joined rows
  (`reviews/calib-2026-10-05.md`: no composite orders the eleven submissions). Exposure-neutral here means neutral
  on 280 local pool lists.
- **Not built or not run**: forward scores for the second hold set; engine-2 lists for either disjoint set (no full
  replay there); `c1` lists for the panel; the v22a full replay for every LightGBM / "noconf" setting (only the
  demote / promote families of section 3); the calibration study's "answer lost first" count; any Kaggle-side check.
- **Candidate masks.** Variants that drop rows were replayed with the forward scores of the original candidate set (a
  superset of what `nb/07` would score after the drop); re-ordering variants are exact.
- **Failed or corrected on the way**: the first models used popularity (found through the importances, removed, all
  numbers re-run); the selection margins were first in raw f.z units (re-run in list units before any reported
  number); `curve.py` crashed once on a NaN in a baseline column (fixed); three "wait" shell commands hit the tool's
  time limit, the jobs themselves ran to the end. No runner failed; ICEBERG and GLACIER scored every panel
  candidate.
