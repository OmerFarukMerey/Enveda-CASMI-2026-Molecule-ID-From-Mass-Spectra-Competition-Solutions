## 5. Reading and next step [I]

- **What v2 is.** A real improvement over v1 on fresh NP isomers: +0.055 overall, +0.07 on unseen scaffolds. It is level with or ahead of
  GLACIER / ICEBERG once their MassSpecGym memorisation is removed. The lever is the graph channel: without it the same data gives v1's
  number (section 6).
- **What it is not.** It is not a better isomer order than the pipeline's own list. The list carries fingerprint-retrieval evidence (FPNet
  similarity, popularity, engine 2) that a spectrum-annotation model does not replace. heliosli's +0.007 therefore probably needs a model
  that complements the list rather than one that only beats GLACIER. Their exact recipe is private.
- **Next, if anyone continues.**
  - Measure an engine-1 / FPNet order on P (FPNet is honest there: P is absent from train). This decides whether the panel's list
    advantage is real or a COCONUT / MassSpecGym leak.
  - Train the reranker on lists whose order is not leaked, e.g. clean-instrument-style lists for held-out train molecules. That gives a
    residual over the list without copying engine 1's 97% leak.
  - Mix in drug-like groups so enveda-180 does not regress.

## 8. Addendum 1: does v2 add to an honest list order? (pre-registered in PREREG.md addendum 1 before computing) [V `casmi-work/isoxe2/fp/`]

**Honest list order L.**
- **What it is.** The fingerprint-retrieval order of the clean instrument's retrained FPNet, `cl_s0` + `cl_s1`. They were trained on
  train.parquet only, with hold + np + E held out, so P is unseen.
- **Score.** Selected-bit fingerprint · logits per spectrum, averaged over spectra and seeds. Candidate bits equal the pool table's on
  60 / 60 checked rows.
- **Not used.** The public fold-safe weights are not on disk, and the other nets' training sets cannot be shown to exclude P.
- **What it leaves out.** This is the fingerprint part of the list only: no popularity prior, engine-1 ranker, engine 2 or library.
- **Caveat.** P's decoys were chosen by ECFP similarity to the answer. That is adversarial to any fingerprint order, so L is pessimistic on P.

**P (164) and P_GL (128)**, isomer MRR and Δ against L:

|---|---|---|---|
