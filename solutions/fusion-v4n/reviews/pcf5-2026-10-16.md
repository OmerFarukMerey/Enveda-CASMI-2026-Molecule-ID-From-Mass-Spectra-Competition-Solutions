## 2. The rules

**PCF5** (`pcf5_row`): leader = `pc[0]`. If its metric identity already sits at rank ≤ 5, nothing changes; otherwise every token with
that identity is dropped and the leader goes to rank 5 (appended to a list shorter than 5), cut to 25. Segments `promoted` and `no_pc`,
and placeholder rows, are never touched. Pos 5 was chosen offline from the displacement profile on 41 gate-closed held-out lists
(insert at rank 3: −0.80 RR, rank 4: −0.05, rank 5: 0), never on the LB.

**EVR** (`evr_row`), promoted lists only: P = the promoted `pc[0]`, O = the S-order top from `pc_lists.json`. When P ≠ O, O goes to
rank `max(5, rank(P) + 1)` unless it already sits there or higher. Per-molecule consistency skips: `not_permutation` (cell 22 should
only permute), `prefusion_mismatch` (PREFUSION rank 1 must be P), `no_orig`.

Failure handling (both): a commit run (`IS_RERUN False`) raises; a scoring rerun restores the cell's input bytes and prints
`PCF5_FAILED` / `EVR_FAILED`; past 8.5 h since `T0` the cell skips.
