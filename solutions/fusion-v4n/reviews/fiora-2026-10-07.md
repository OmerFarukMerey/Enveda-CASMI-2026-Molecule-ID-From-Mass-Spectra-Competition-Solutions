# FIORA as a forward model for the gap (negative-mode molecules), 2026-10-07

Local work only. Nothing was committed, pushed, submitted or uploaded; no `kaggle` command that writes; the visible
`test.parquet` was not read; no NIST data; no training. **Result: the conditions for wiring were not met, so the solution
is unchanged** (`git status` clean apart from this file). A complete default-off wiring was written, tested and then taken
out again; it is kept as a patch under `scratch/fiora/prepared/` (section 6).

**Every number below was computed in this study** unless a file is named. MRR = mean reciprocal rank; "±" = one standard
error over molecules (paired where a difference is given); (wins / losses). **No leaderboard estimate is given.**

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Compliance: no contradiction with the desk research.** The Kaggle package `ahmedberatozer/casmi26-fiora-os-v1` is
   byte-identical to upstream `BAMeScience/fiora` at tag v1.0.0 (commit `21dc75bf`): all 51 source and model files have
   the git blob hash of the upstream tree. Licence file: MIT. The model's parameter file says `"training_library":
   "MSnLib v7"`; the package says the NIST / MS-DIAL default model is not bundled. Open point, unchanged: no separate
   licence for the weights (they sit in the MIT repository).
2. **FIORA runs locally** from the package source with two of its wheels (`torch_geometric`, `treelib`) and stand-ins for
   its plotting imports: 48 to 51 predictions per second on the shared GPU, 1.9 GB allocated at peak, no failed graph in
   46,990 candidates.
3. **In-group isomer ranking, FIORA alone, on molecules it has not seen:**
   - Enveda-180 hold fold, timsTOF `[M-H]-`, 186 molecules: MRR **0.811 ± 0.023**, top-1 0.714 (chance 0.215 / 0.083). The
     MetFrag-parsimony score on the same spectra: 0.689 (FIORA +0.122 ± 0.024); on all of the molecule's spectra: 0.808
     (equal). Clear, and large. These are drug-like structures with pool isomers as decoys.
   - **Natural-product panel**, timsTOF `[M-H]-`, 15 molecules not in MSnLib: MRR **0.160 ± 0.068**, top-1 1 of 15 (chance
     0.053 / 0.010): +0.106 ± 0.068 over chance, 1.6 standard errors. MetFrag: 0.055, at chance.
   - **The 19 panel molecules the variant would touch** (an `[M-H]-` spectrum, no `[M+H]+` / `[M+Na]+`; 16 of them are in
     MSnLib, so FIORA has seen them): MRR **0.069 ± 0.025 against chance 0.061. At chance.** MetFrag there: 0.043 ± 0.008,
     below chance.
   - Against the models we trust, same molecules, same candidates, panel `[M+H]+` (210 molecules): FIORA 0.138, ICEBERG
     0.446, GLACIER 0.466, both 0.526 (chance 0.066). ICEBERG and GLACIER have very probably seen 225 of the panel's 243
     molecules; on the 13 that are in none of their source libraries: FIORA 0.045 (chance 0.048), ICEBERG 0.227, GLACIER 0.248.
4. **Replay against v33b: no form passes.** Four forms were fixed in advance (FIORA added to the MetFrag gap term, or in
   its place; weight 0.5 or 1.0). Composite on the panel with cross-fitted engine 2 / on the mixed regime: added 0.5
   −0.0079 ± 0.0032 / −0.0060 ± 0.0032; added 1.0 −0.0174 / −0.0149; **replaces 0.5 +0.0005 ± 0.0013 / −0.0034 ± 0.0024**;
   replaces 1.0 −0.0121 / −0.0132. The best form is neutral on the panel and loses `c1` −0.0085 ± 0.0048 (0 / 5) and `dB`
   −0.0043 ± 0.0025 in the mixed regime, where exposure also rises (`c1` 5 / 0 → 6 / 1, `c2` 23 / 5 → 24 / 6). Every list of a
   molecule with ICEBERG / GLACIER scores is identical to v33b's in every variant (checked: 0 lists changed).
5. **Decision (rule fixed before the runs): wire nothing.** Failed: Step 3 (a loss beyond one standard error on a type, for
   every form; exposure up in the mixed regime). Step 2 is met on Enveda-180 and **not** established on natural products,
   the class of the hidden test: at chance on the gap molecules.
6. **Offline install on Kaggle: not verified** (no Kaggle run was made). What the prepared runner needs and what is missing
   is listed in section 6.

## 3. Step 1: running it

Runner: `scratch/fiora/prepared/fiora_runner.py` (own process, own site directory, resumable, budget, checkpoint after
every molecule). It uses FIORA's own classes for everything that touches the model (`Metabolite`, the three feature
encoders with the settings of `scripts/fiora-predict`, `FioraModel.load_from_state_dict`, `SimulationFramework.simulate_spectrum`).

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Adducts predicted | `[M-H]-` and `[M+H]+` (the two trained ones; `[M]+` / `[M]-` unused) |
| Collision energy | the query's `collision_energy_ev`, in eV, inside the trained range 1 < CE ≤ 100. A spectrum listing several energies (the panel has many merged spectra such as 20 + 50 eV) gets the sum of the predictions at each energy, each scaled to its maximum. A spectrum with no usable value would get 20 + 40 + 60 eV (did not occur) |
| Instrument token | `HCD`, the only one the checkpoint has. **Mismatch**: trained on Orbitrap HCD (MSnLib), queries are timsTOF (QTOF-type CID). Nothing can be set to correct for it |
| Similarity, pipeline's kind (**primary**, fixed before the runs) | weighted spectral entropy similarity, 0.01 Da or 20 ppm, as `ice_runner.py` scores ICEBERG; query cleaned as there (≤ precursor + 2, floor 0.002, 256 peaks); peaks above precursor − 0.5 Da removed on both sides (`ent_np`). Molecule score = mean over its spectra |
| Similarity, the authors' | FIORA's `spectral_cosine` with square-root intensities at its default 0.05 Da, with the precursor (`sqc`) and without (`sqc_np`: the paper's choice for candidate ranking) |
| Speed, memory | 48.4 and 50.9 predictions per second (two runs of 97,049 and 35,431 predictions; GPU shared with a second runner; one prediction = one candidate at one adduct and energy), 1.85 GB allocated and 2.93 GB reserved at peak. The T4 rate is unknown |
| Failures | 0 graph failures and 0 prediction errors in 46,990 candidates (panel groups 23,514, Enveda-180 groups 15,700, gap candidates 2,361 + 5,322, smoke 93) |
| RDKit | 2026.03.3 (the venv's). FIORA's `requirements.txt` pins 2024.9.6; whether predictions differ is unknown and no key is computed in the runner |

## 7. What this replay can and cannot tell, and the arithmetic

**Can.** That the term is confined to the gap (0 untouched lists change). That more forward weight on local gap molecules
costs in every stratum where the answer is in the base list. That at equal weight FIORA is no better than the term it
would replace, on 18 to 49 molecules. That exposure does not fall.

**Cannot.**
- The sign on the hidden test. Local replays had the leaderboard's sign in 5 to 7 of 11 known contrasts
  (`reviews/uregime-2026-10-07.md`), and they have understated the forward stage every time it was tested (weights 0.6 and
  0.5, v34a): the same harness says that switching the stage-A ICEBERG / GLACIER terms off gains +0.010 ± 0.003 (that
  review), and the leaderboard answered −0.011. A local loss of
  −0.003 at 1.4 standard errors carried by 23 to 48 moving lists is inside what that record cannot tell from zero.
- Who has seen what cuts both ways here. FIORA has seen 16 of the 19 touched panel molecules and 14 of the 49 in the
  mixed regime, which flatters it; the base lists have seen the molecules too, which penalises any re-rank. Chain U
  removes the second leak and gives the same result.
- The mixed regime's touched molecules are mostly Orbitrap and QTOF library compounds (4 of 49 are timsTOF); the hidden
  test is timsTOF natural products. The panel is the closer set and has 18 touched molecules: one answer is 0.06 inside
  the segment.
- The reason to prefer Step 2 over the replay is that it has a physical meaning and unseen molecules. It is Step 2 that
  says no for natural products: FIORA at chance on the gap molecules is not a statement about the base list.

## 8. Limits, and what failed or could not be verified

- **Not verified**: the Kaggle offline install and the T4 speed (no Kaggle run); the image's Python packages listed in
  section 6; whether FIORA's predictions depend on the RDKit version; the MSnLib v7 structure list (the `pluskal_ms2`
  rows of `train.parquet` stand in for it); a licence for the weights other than the repository's MIT file.
- **Panel, unseen negative-mode natural products: 15 molecules** (4 of them in the gap). The statement "FIORA is weak on
  natural products" rests mostly on molecules it has seen (107 in negative mode, 179 in positive mode), where a model
  should if anything look better; on unseen ones the numbers agree but the errors are wide.
- Enveda-180 groups hold no joined PubChem rows, the panel's do; the two sets are not on the same decoy difficulty.
- ICEBERG / GLACIER scores in table D are the cached pipeline scores (mean over `[M+H]+` and `[M+Na]+` spectra), FIORA's
  are `[M+H]+` only. No ICEBERG / GLACIER scores exist for the Enveda-180 hold molecules, so FIORA was compared with them
  on the panel only, where they are leaked.
- The replay scored every candidate of a gap molecule (up to 214); the prepared stage caps at 150. Formate and chloride
  spectra were not given to FIORA in the replay (`[M-H]-` only), as planned. One collision-energy rule, one similarity
  as primary; no tuning on any set.
- The forms were fixed in advance, but "rep 0.5 is the least harmful" is a pick among four.
- One seed of the out-of-fold ranker, one draw of the class-2 tie emulation, composite weights equal by decree: as in
  the libprotect review.
- **Failed or corrected on the way**: the GPU breach described in section 1 (runner fixed: cache emptied above 1.5 GB
  reserved, at most 8 conditions per forward pass); three runs were stopped and resumed from their checkpoints because
  of it; a shell here-document broke a backslash in the first verify script (rewritten); FIORA's plotting import needed a
  stand-in for the parent package as well; the first version of the new test asserted formula slots after the engine
  fusion, which does not hold (restricted to lists without the fusion). No process of mine is left running.
