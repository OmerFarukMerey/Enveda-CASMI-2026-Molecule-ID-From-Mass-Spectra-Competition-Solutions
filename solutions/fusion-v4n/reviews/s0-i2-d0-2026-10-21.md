## 1. Why no tuning bench [V scout and spec reviews]

A leak-free replay of q1's late stages (fold-safe networks, a 250-molecule Enveda panel, every input of cells 24-35 dumped) was specified
and adversarially reviewed. It was not built: each leaderboard sign it could be gated against rests on about one public molecule, so even a
perfect bench passes its own sign gate only 9-16% of the time (pre-registered simulation), and it can only judge gate-scope changes (PubChem
promotion and placement), which are themselves worth about one public molecule. Cost would have been 2-3 days of code and about 4.5 T4-hours.

The scouts also found: no public notebook above 0.456 on 10-20; natural-product catalogues exhausted for engine 2 (COCONUT / LOTUS / NPAtlas
add about 240 structures); ahmedberatozer's bioext PubChem block in engine 2 lost 0.004-0.009 on v15; no time budget binds on q1 (budgets
are cliffs, cell 29 asserts status `ok`); rank 1 carries about 0.377 of 0.434 locally.
