# MS2DeepScore triangulation on s1 (2026-10-24)

Marks: **[V]** run or computed here (file named); **[I]** inference. Rules fixed before any score: `scratch/ms2ds/PREREG.md`.
Resume: `scratch/ms2ds/STATE.md`. Nothing submitted, pushed or uploaded.

## Verdict

**Both pre-registered checks fail, so s1d is not built and is not a submission candidate.** The term carries real
signal on its own: it ranks the answer well above chance among hard decoys. But inside s1's final windows it disagrees
with s1 far more often than it helps, and the gain on molecules MS2DeepScore never saw is not significant (n = 42).

## What was built [V]

- **Model and code.** The model is MS2DeepScore 2.0 dual-mode (Zenodo 17826815, CC BY 4.0, md5 checked). The weights
  are read with `torch.load(weights_only=True)`. `solutions/fusion-v4n/clean/ms2ds_core.py` reimplements the encoder
  in torch, with a sparse first layer. It matches the ms2deepscore 2.7.2 package to **max |Δ| 2.2e-6** on 600 train
  spectra and 32 synthetic probes (`scratch/ms2ds/verify_pkg.py`; `golden.json` holds the package's own probe
  embeddings). The package was installed with `--no-deps --target` into `casmi-work/ext/ms2ds/site` and used only for
  this check. Its optional imports (tensorboard, deprecated/wrapt, pubchempy, lxml) were stubbed. The repo .venv was
  not touched.
- **Train embedding.** All **2,539,608** train spectra were embedded on the CPU (4 threads, below-normal priority) in
  **~10 min** (`embed_train.py`). The local GPU was not used.
- **Neighbour search.** Search runs over 274,186 identities: train_structs metric keys, with the query's own key
  excluded. One batch of 175 molecules against all of train takes 12-14 s on the CPU (`tri_eval.py`).

## DEV-A: choosing the rule [V `casmi-work/ms2ds/deva.json`]

- **Set.** 300 enveda-180 keys were drawn, none of them in MS2DeepScore's training libraries (gnps / massbank / mona /
  pluskal_ms2) and none in the 400 evaluation molecules. 236 were kept; 64 had fewer than 9 same-formula decoys.
- **Candidates.** Each molecule's answer is ranked against up to 24 same-formula decoys, the most Morgan-similar to it.
- **Result.** The best of the 24 configurations is **AGG max, K 50, no anchors, FORM wdot**: MRR **0.518**, top-1 33%,
  against 0.169 for a random order. Random anchors and K 200 hurt in every form.

## DEV-B (weight) and evaluation [V `casmi-work/ms2ds/replay_ms2ds.json`, log `replay_ms2ds.log`]

The s1 replay reproduces the final lists on **400 / 400** molecules, using `scratch/s1val/replay.py` imported unmodified.

holds for 200 of the 400 molecules.

**Inside s1's windows** (`diag_window.py`, run after the checks; it changes nothing). 199 of the 200 gated molecules
have the answer in the window. There the term alone has MRR **0.364** (random order 0.153), while s1 has 0.877. The
term ranks the answer better than s1 in 7 windows and worse in 152.

The median top-neighbour predicted Tanimoto is 0.84, even with the molecule's own key removed. The answer's close
analogues are in train, but so are those of s1's other candidates. A weak re-ranker against a strong base only costs.

**Caveat.** This harness's base has seen these molecules: s1 scores 0.88 here, against 0.465 on the LB. So the
hidden-test trade-off could be less unfavourable than it looks here. Nothing here shows that it is.

## Runtime and shipping (estimate, not built) [I]

On a T4 the term would cost about **10-20 min**:
- Reading train.parquet's peak lists: 1-2 min.
- Sparse embedding of 2.54 M spectra on the GPU: a few minutes (10 min on 4 local CPU threads).
- Batched search: well under 1 min.
- RDKFingerprints of the K = 50 neighbours of about 1,000 gated molecules: about 1-2 min on 4 CPUs.
- Peak RAM: about 2.5 GB fp16 embeddings plus one chunk.

Shipping needs no pip install. The image's torch, numpy and rdkit suffice. A private dataset would hold:
- the model file (416 MB);
- `golden.json`;
- `ms2ds_core.py`;
- the wheels, for provenance only;
- a README with CC BY 4.0 attribution.

`np.bitwise_count` needs numpy ≥ 2, so the patch would need a popcount fallback.
