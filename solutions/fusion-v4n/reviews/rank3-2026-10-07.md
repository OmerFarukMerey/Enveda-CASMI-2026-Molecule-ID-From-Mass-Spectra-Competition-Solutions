## 0. Bottom line

1. **A third ranker does add to the local three-way average, and so does a second copy of the one we have.** Three-way
   minus two-way on the 1,574 held-out lists below the gate: +0.010 to +0.018 for every candidate of production-like
   quality. **The control, the production recipe with another seed as "third ranker", gives +0.012 ± 0.003** (185 / 75);
   the plain two-way average at weights 1 : 2 gives +0.012 ± 0.002. The three-way gain is the shift of weight from the
   public ranker (1/2 → 1/3) to our family, not diversity.
2. **Against that control nothing is beyond two standard errors on both halves.** Best: the ranker fitted to the
   fold-safe + `ft_hn` bank's features +0.007 ± 0.003 (on the 1,370 lists that bank has; half A +0.003 ± 0.003 against its
   same-list control, half B +0.008 ± 0.004), MLP (three seeds) +0.005 ± 0.003 (half B 0.000 ± 0.004), extra-trees and
   hard-list LightGBM +0.004 ± 0.002, XGBoost and CatBoost +0.001 ± 0.002. Weak-but-different rankers (linear, analog-only,
   fingerprint-only, fragmentation-only) lose 0.009 to 0.095.
3. **In the weight-neutral design** (public ranker keeps its half, our half becomes production + candidate) every
   candidate is between −0.004 and +0.001 ± 0.002 to 0.003. Ensembling inside our half adds nothing.
4. **The weight axis is where this measurement is known to be wrong.** Locally more of our ranker is better without a
   turning point: public alone 0.664, average 0.760, 0.3 : 0.7 +0.013 ± 0.002, ours alone 0.782 (+0.022 ± 0.004). The

   local gain is not evidence; `fsranker2`'s full replay already found 0.3 and 0.7 inside the noise of 0.5.
5. **Pre-stated rule: no candidate is chosen on half A, so none is reported as passing on half B.** The rule's side
   condition (no loss beyond one standard error on lists whose answer starts first) cannot be met by any candidate,
   including the reseed control: such lists can only lose (section 3 says what that does to the start-rank split).
6. **Question 6: no.** A ranker on a second fold-safe bank would correlate about 0.8 to 0.86 with the production ranker
   (section 5), the diversity of CatBoost, the MLP or the no-list view, none of which adds beyond the control; the

## 3. The pre-stated selection (step 3) and why it selects nothing

Rule, written into `k3_eval.py` before any three-way number existed: halves A / B of the held-out molecules by a hash of
the generic Murcko scaffold (843 / 731 lists; first-place lists 588 / 463, others 255 / 268); on A choose the candidate
with the largest three-way gain on lists whose answer does not start first, among those whose mean difference on the
first-place lists is not below −1 SE; it passes when on B the gain is above 2 SE on all lists and on the not-first lists,
with no loss beyond 1 SE on the first-place lists and on `c1b`. Controls are not eligible.

**Outcome: no candidate is eligible on A; the choice is "none".** Every candidate, the control included, loses on the
first-place lists beyond one standard error (control: A −0.005 ± 0.002, 0 / 6; B −0.009 ± 0.003, 0 / 8).

I should have seen this when writing the rule: **a list whose answer starts first cannot gain, and one whose answer does
not start first has most of its room upward**, so any change of the order, a random one too, shows "gain where it
starts lower, loss where it starts first". The start-rank split of a difference against the *same* reference that
defines the strata is regression to the mean and cannot separate a good third ranker from noise. The symmetric
accounting is the first-places column of section 2 (lost / gained) and the comparison with the control.

**What the leaderboard allows us to infer, and what it does not.** The three known points (0.437, 0.442, 0.434) say the
optimum is strictly between 0 and 1 and that the two ends are 0.003 apart, less than one answer between place 1 and 2

measured here moves that: **0.5 stays; 0.7 is the only neighbour without a local argument against it, and there is none
for it beyond a measurement that got 1.0 wrong.** A possible reason for the bias, not tested here: what follows the
base list in the notebook (forward models, engine 2) repairs many of the lists our ranker repairs, so a base-list gain
is passed on by about a third (`fsranker2`), while a first place lost in the base list is not always won back.

## 5. Question 6: would a second fold-safe fingerprint bank trained by us give a more diverse ranker?

Measured rank correlations (per-list Spearman over the first 60 of the two-way average, mean of 1,574 lists; bank rows
from `k3_bank.py`, lists below the gate):

| what differs | Spearman with the production ranker |
|---|---|
| seed only (four-seed mean against a fifth seed; single seed against single seed 0.92) | 0.95 |
| training data: + 2,000 molecules / half of the molecules | 0.88 / 0.84 to 0.85 |
| learner, same inputs: XGBoost 0.88, pointwise 0.87, extra-trees 0.86, CatBoost 0.78, MLP 0.74, pairwise logistic 0.71 | 0.71 to 0.88 |
| target: hard lists 0.87, same-formula rows 0.83 | 0.83 to 0.87 |

| public ranker, same bank, same inputs (the pair of v33b) | 0.45 |

The bank's own effect, same ranker (the public one) on the same molecule's list: fold-safe against fold-safe + `ft_hn`
0.96 to 0.98 (`f.z` alone 0.93 to 0.98); public FPNets against fold-safe 0.54 to 0.57 on joined lists (where 18% of the
first 60 keys differ, because the bank picks the joined rows) and 0.69 to 0.76 on plain lists (`f.z` alone 0.71 to 0.80).
That last pair is a seen bank against an unseen one; two unseen banks of one recipe are closer than that.

Estimate. Our ranker leans little on the bank (gain share of `fp` + `model_views` 0.21, `fsranker2`; without any
fingerprint input its own MRR is −0.011 ± 0.006 and its order correlates 0.78). A ranker fitted to a second, wholly
different fold-safe bank must land between 0.78 (the bank's information removed altogether) and 0.86 (half of it
swapped): **about 0.8, the diversity of CatBoost, the no-list view or the MLP.** Those add +0.001 to +0.005 ± 0.003 over
the reseed control overall and 0.000 to +0.004 ± 0.004 on half B, and nothing in the weight-neutral design. The public
ranker's 0.45 comes from being fitted in another regime (features of memorised molecules), which no unseen bank
reproduces. The one hint the other way: `t_d` is +0.005 ± 0.003 above its same-list control, but with an own MRR
+0.006 ± 0.004 higher, i.e. because `ft_hn` improves the bank; a second bank of the same recipe on other folds would be
different, not better.

Cost, for the user: about 6 GPU hours per model (`fsranker2`: 5.0 h unthrottled, 6.2 h at duty 0.8, 19.9 h beside a
game); the new bank must hold out `hold` and `np` as well or all 13,092 training lists are seen by it; every list must
be re-run through the engine with the new bank on the CPU (the 2,000 molecules of `fsranker3` took 3.6 h on six
workers; 5,202 molecules about 9 h); and the notebook would run a third engine pass below the gate (not timed).
**Answer: no; do not train it for this purpose.** Nothing was trained.

## 6. What this does and does not tell

- **It tells**: at ranker level, on unseen molecules, no differently built ranker fitted on our rows carries
  information the production ranker lacks in a form the rank average can use; the apparent gain of any three-way
  average is the weight shift; a second bank would not change that.
- **It does not tell** whether the leaderboard would reward that weight shift (the weight axis is where this
  measurement has the wrong sign, section 4), nor anything after the base list: no replay was run, so there is no
  per-type (`c1`, `c2`, `dA`, `dB`) difference, no exposure after the fusion. The replay's own record is 5 to 7 signs of
  11 (`uregime`).
- **Not tested**: a third ranker fitted in the *seen* regime (a second "public-like" ranker: public FPNets' features on
  train molecules), which is the kind of partner the leaderboard rewarded; it would be leaked locally and could only be
  judged by a submission. Kendall's tau (Spearman only). More than one seed for any candidate but the MLP; the reseeding
  noise of a single LightGBM fit (0.003 on `c2J`, `fsranker2`) is not in the standard errors. Hyper-parameters of the
  MLP, XGBoost and CatBoost were set once and not tuned. `t_d` covers 1,370 of the 1,574 lists and is fitted on the old
  lists only.
- **Asymmetries**: stated at the top; in addition the held-out lists are the old ones, and the candidates fitted on the
  n2000 set have 2,000 more training molecules than the production reference (the n2000 controls show what that is
  worth: +0.002 to +0.003 ± 0.002).
- **Failed or corrected on the way**: the first launch of the workers from the Bash tool died when the tool call ended
  (window-close event); they were restarted from PowerShell and survived, the MLP from its first fold. CatBoost on one
  thread did not finish a fold in 17 minutes and was restarted with four threads and 300 iterations. A kill command
  ended its own shell, so the edit after it was applied in a second call. The full evaluation exceeded a 115 s limit
  once and was rerun without one. The first-place condition of the rule was unsatisfiable by construction (section 3).
  The two caches `Xr.npy` and `X_d.npy` (2 GB) were deleted at the end because `D:` has 8 GB free; they are rebuilt in
  under a minute.
