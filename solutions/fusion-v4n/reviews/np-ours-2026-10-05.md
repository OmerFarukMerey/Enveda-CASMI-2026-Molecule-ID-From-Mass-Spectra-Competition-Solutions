# Our own NP-tuned FPNet for engine 2 (2026-10-05)

Offline. Nothing was submitted, uploaded, pushed or committed; the only `kaggle` calls were read-only (two dataset
cards, two file lists, three listings); nothing was downloaded. The visible `test.parquet` was not read. Training data:
`train.parquet` only. Trainer: `train/train_eng_fpnet.py`; scripts of the evaluation: `scratch/np_ours/`
(git-ignored); outputs: `D:\casmi-data\np_ours\`.

**Marks.** **[V]** = read or computed here. **[C]** = somebody's claim. "±" = one standard error of a paired
per-molecule (per-structure) difference, (wins / losses) in brackets. **No leaderboard estimate is given.**

## 0. Bottom line

1. **A fine-tune of the public `fp_single_s2` on the Enveda NP standards works on standards it has not seen** [V]:
   cross-fitted over five folds (each model trained on 200 of the 250 structures of `enveda-np-examples`, tested on
   the other 50, which the base has not seen either), rank of the answer in its pool window: the model alone
   0.478 → 0.613 (+0.136 ± 0.018, 114 / 23); with engine 2's exact logits, ½ [½ (s2 + ours) + merged],
   0.490 → 0.538 (**+0.048 ± 0.010**, 82 / 11). These are the only timsTOF natural products with labels.
2. **On the local replay it is indistinguishable from no extra model, and it is not as good as goooman's model on
   engine 2's `c2` list** [V]: engine 2's own list `c2` 0.6343 against 0.6339 (public) and 0.6421 (goooman); ours
   minus goooman −0.0077 ± 0.0039 (4 / 22); `c1` 0.8355 against 0.8257 and 0.8336 (ours minus goooman
   +0.0019 ± 0.0047). Full replay of v13a, ours minus public: `c1` +0.0044 ± 0.0035, `c2` −0.0017 ± 0.0014,
   `dA` −0.0003 ± 0.0006, `dB` +0.0002 ± 0.0002. **Exposure unchanged: 25 and 6.**
3. The two results do not contradict each other. None of the 380 replay molecules is an NP standard or a timsTOF
   natural product; there our model is a near copy of `s2` (cosine of the logits 0.997) and adds nothing, while
   goooman's is an independent network (cosine to the public mix 0.965) and adds ensemble diversity. Where the
   fine-tune acts (item 1) the replay cannot look.
4. **The criterion of the brief (at least as good as goooman on engine 2's own list, exposure not raised) is met on
   `c1` and on exposure and NOT met on `c2`.** The dataset folder was therefore **not** created. The flag value
   (`ENG_EXTRA_FPNET='np-ours'`), the preset `configs/v25b-v13a-np-ours.json` and the build rule exist and are
   tested, default off; section 8 has the two commands that make the folder if the main session decides to use
   the model on the strength of item 1.
5. Oversampling all Enveda timsTOF spectra 4× (second arm) does nothing for the standards (+0.006 ± 0.006 in the
   engine's mix) and nothing in the replay.
6. Provenance of the base is **as good as, and no better than, what every submission of ours already rests on**
   (section 2): `fp_single_s2.pt` is engine 2's own model. Its card is empty.
7. A model trained **from scratch by us** on `train.parquet` (20,000 steps, 0.30 GPU h; no public checkpoint in it)
   is weaker alone (0.68 to 0.84 on seen libraries against 0.78 to 0.92), adds nothing in the replay
   (engine 2's `c2` list −0.0017 ± 0.0022, `c1` +0.0022 ± 0.0021; exposure 25 / 6) and is not a replacement for
   goooman's model either (section 6).

## 2. The base we chose and its provenance

**`prvsiyan/casmi26-fp-models-v2/fp_single_s2.pt`**, sha256 `c19aa29016ed4d7a…`, 144,110,255 bytes, licence field
CC0-1.0, published 2026-09-16.

- Why: it is the per-spectrum model engine 2 already runs, so a fine-tune keeps the logit scale the engine's rankers
  were fitted on, and it adds **no artefact our submissions do not already depend on** (v4b to v13a all load it).
- What is and is not known. The dataset card is empty [V]. That it was trained on `train.parquet` is a claim of the
  public lineage (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES, megayak's notebook), supported by what the weights do [V]: they rank
  structures of the training libraries at 0.77 to 0.92 and the one library megayak says was held out at 0.48.
  **Nothing here can show the absence of NIST data in its training**; the same is true of every submission we have
  made. By the landscape rule ("unknown counts as unusable") this is a gap of the whole engine-2 lineage, not a
  new one.
- The alternative with a written statement is megayak's from-scratch pair (README above). It was not used: the
  files are not on this machine and a download needs the user's approval; it is also weaker on seen structures
  (0.52 to 0.62 against 0.76 to 0.82 [C]). `train_eng_fpnet.py --init <ours_fpnet_single_16k.pt>` is the same
  command.
- A model with no public checkpoint in it at all: section 6.

Fine-tune data: `train.parquet` only (spectra and structures). Negatives: rows of engine 2's own candidate pool
(COCONUT and ChEBI / LIPID MAPS structure lists of prvsiyan's public datasets, training structures). No NIST.

## 3. The trainer and the start-up checks

`train/train_eng_fpnet.py` (new; engine 2's network is not the v1 class of `train_fpnet.py`: 6,930 bits, an
instrument embedding, no element head). Model class and peak preparation are imported from `nb/embed/eng/pv_fp.py`;
sharing, yield (exit 75), resume and logging as in `train_fpnet.py` (`gpushare.py`, `guard_run.py`).

- Inputs per spectrum exactly as `casmi_engine.compute_channels` builds them (spectrum cache of `train.parquet`:
  peaks up to precursor + 2, base peak 1; `parse_ce`; `instr_family`). Rows without a collision energy
  are not trained on (2,191,185 of 2,539,608 rows remain: collision energy present, answer in the pool, peaks): at inference the engine feeds NaN for them
  (`reviews/np-eng2-2026-10-05.md`, section 3).
- Loss: cross-entropy over the answer and 63 random rows of its ±10 ppm pool window (tautomer twins excluded) on
  **f.z / T**, T fitted once on the base checkpoint and then fixed. `--w_bce 0` in all fine-tunes (the base is not
  calibrated as bit probabilities, BCE 0.33; a BCE term would pull the scale).
- Folds by structure (metric key): the fold's NP standards are removed from training in every library. The 380
  replay molecules are removed too (`--hold_keys`), so the replay below is not helped by extra memorisation.
- Evaluation = the engine's use of the model: mean logits over the (at most 8) spectra of a structure in one
  library, rank of the answer by f.z in its pool window.

**Checks before the real runs** [V] (`BASE` line of every log; `smoke_np4`):

## 7. What this does and does not support

- **Supported** [V]: the recipe transfers to Enveda timsTOF natural-product standards it has not seen
  (+0.048 ± 0.010 in engine 2's fingerprint logits, n 250, cross-fitted, base unseen too). It does not raise
  exposure and does not move the local replay.
- **Not supported**: that it is as good as goooman's model where the replay measures (engine 2's `c2` list). The
  leaderboard evidence (np1 0.413 on 0.409, one submission, inside the noise band) belongs to goooman's model, not
  to ours; ours has none.
- **Not measured**: the engine-2 ranker and the fusion on NP standards (the fingerprint score is 6 + 6 of the
  ranker's features; the replay passes about a quarter of an engine-2 gain on). A class-2 replay of the 250
  standards through engine 2 with fold models would measure it: about an hour of CPU for the channels, not run.
- **Open**: the provenance statement for `fp_single_s2.pt` (section 2), which every submission shares.
- The soup that would ship has seen all 250 standards; if hidden-test molecules are among them (class-1-like for
  the library channel anyway) it helps more than item 1 says, otherwise item 1 is the estimate.

  about 0.75 h of the 2 h budget. Largest GPU memory of a run 2.7 GB. Three data threads, below-normal priority;
  list building on the CPU with three threads, one process at a time next to the trainer.
- The session was interrupted once (all background jobs killed during the pilot); `p_tims4_f0` was resumed from its
  step-500 state by `guard_run.py`, the second pilot fold was not run (the pilot table is fold 0 only, n 50).
- One seed per fold; lr, steps and the ×100 were chosen on fold 0, which is one of the five folds of the pooled
  result (fold 0 alone: +0.233; the pooled +0.136 includes it; folds 1 to 4 alone are therefore lower than fold 0).
- The control (no boost) exists for fold 0 only: +0.046 ± 0.029 on unseen standards, so about a third of the
  fold-0 gain could be "more training of any kind"; the dose response (×4 → ×20 → ×100) says the rest is the standards.
- `gpushare` reported "no game" throughout; the yield path was not exercised by this trainer (the code is the one
  of `train_fpnet.py`).
- megayak's FPNets and prvsiyan's other model datasets were not downloaded or measured.
- Not run on Kaggle; the checkpoint was loaded with the engine's class on CPU and GPU locally.
