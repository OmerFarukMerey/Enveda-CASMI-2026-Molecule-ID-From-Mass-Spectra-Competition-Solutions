## 1. The one addition: lock the baseline leader [V code, I sign]

- **What changes.** At offset 3 and w 0.25, B0 lets baseline #2 take rank 1 when it is neural #1 and the leader is neural #18 or worse.
  B1 keeps the baseline leader there. On those molecules ranks 1 and 2 swap back and nothing else moves. Everywhere else B1 = B0 byte for byte.
  A random check on 300,000 lists (ties included) found 0 exceptions to this.
- **The edit.** Two constants in the author's configuration cell. `rrf_order(s, 0.0, 3)` returns the baseline order, so
  `leader_safe_order` gives `[baseline #1] + rrf_order(s, 0.50, 3)`. There is no new code, module, dataset or runtime. The fallback
  chain (CUSTOM → V1 → V0 → 0.466 file → baseline) has the same shape as B0's.
- **Why the sign should be ≥ 0.** Every LB point where the neural model reaches rank 1 more often lost: w 0.5 at offset 3 −0.021, w 0.75
  −0.047, offset 10 −0.020. Every locked variant scored 0.467: offset 0.5, offset 1 (n1o), offset 1.5. n1 with flips scored 0.466. The
  one visible flip (m_068354) overrides a leader that is engine-1 #1, engine-2 #1 and pre-fusion #1, with library_max 1.0 and the best
  ICE score.
- **Expectation (set before the run).** Public 0.468 with 85-90 % probability (no flip touches a public answer); otherwise ±0.004.
  Private +0.0003 to +0.0005 expected, range −0.002 to +0.004. Exposure: about 0.2-0.9 % of molecules.

## 4. Result

B1 submitted 2026-10-26 14:50 UTC (sub 57669910): **0.468**, the same as romanrozen's V2 and +0.001 over n1o (team best before).
This is the case section 1 called most likely (85-90 %): no public answer sits on a molecule where the lock changes rank 1. On the public
part the lock is therefore 0.000; its private effect stays unmeasured (exposure about 0.2-0.9 % of molecules). For the final selection
B1 and V2 are interchangeable on the evidence; B1 is the one we own and has no rank-1 change the LB ever rewarded.

Follow-up evidence (guhongbin's sweep on the same code, read on 10-27 from the score order,
[`update-2026-10-27.md`](../../../docs/landscape/update-2026-10-27.md)): with ranks 2-25 at offset 1 instead of 3, w_mid 0.35 and 0.50
fall to the 0.466 block and w_mid 1.00 to 0.464 or below. Offset 3 for ranks 2-25 is part of the gain; a higher w_mid at offset 3 is unscored.
