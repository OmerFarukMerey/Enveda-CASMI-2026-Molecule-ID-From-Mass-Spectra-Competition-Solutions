## 0. Bottom line

1. **A fitted prior beats the hand-made one in-group, and the whole gain is a catalogue-membership flag** [M]. On
   23,328 natural-product answers of other libraries (scaffold-grouped 5-fold CV, full pool formula group): current
   prior 0.472, conditional logit on all features 0.486 (+0.0142 ± 0.0012), less famous half 0.316 → 0.348
   (+0.0315 ± 0.0015). Without any COCONUT-derived feature the same model is +0.0006 ± 0.0010; inside the universe of
   COCONUT rows only (membership constant) it is +0.0008 ± 0.0011. The largest coefficient is "has a COCONUT record"
   (+0.84 standardised). The answer set is defined by a natural-product catalogue bit, so that flag is partly
   tautological, and it is exactly the kind of flag pitfall 7 warns about.
2. **Refitting the counts themselves does not help** [M]: a fitted combination of substances, PubMed, patents is
   −0.0054 ± 0.0011 against the plain sum (it drops PubMed, the term the replay of `popmu` found useful).
3. **In the full replay every fitted variant loses** [M]: six variants, all negative on the panel (composite −0.0055
   to −0.0086, 2 to 3 SE) and five of six negative on the mixed regime's natural products; `c2` is −0.010 to −0.020 on
   the panel for all six and −0.011 to −0.016 on the mixed regime for the five logit variants. Against v44a they are further behind (panel −0.009 to −0.012). Exposure does not rise.
4. **The label design in the brief contains a trap** [M]: with negatives = same-formula isomers that are not in
   train, every negative is a COCONUT-only row, and the model learns to recognise train rows by their missing
   COCONUT annotation (Enveda-180 answers against non-train isomers: 0.184 → 0.58 to 0.77; on the full group, where
   the other train isomers are present, the same model is −0.051 against the current prior).
5. **Circularity** [M]: giving every train structure one or two substance records fewer removes 2% and 4% of the
   current prior's signal above chance on the whole set, 12% and 20% on the less famous half, and **45% and 78% in
   the bottom quartile of substance counts**. For low-count answers, which is what classes 2 and 3 look like, the
   measurable signal is of the size a library's own deposit could produce.
6. **Nothing is wired. v44a stays the only popularity candidate.**

## 2. Model and in-group validation

**Groups.** Universe P of the availability review: the answer plus every pool row of its formula, groups of two or
more. Answers: **OTH-NP** = train structures in no Enveda library with a natural-product catalogue bit, all 23,328
(median group 30; median 54 substance records, lower quartile 20, 10th percentile 9); **Enveda panel** 239 (median
497 records); **Enveda-180** without a catalogue bit, 20,000 sampled; **other-library, not NP**, 20,000 sampled.

**Models** (`p2_model.py`). Conditional logit (softmax inside the group, weak ridge) on fixed feature sets: F1
counts; F2 + COCONUT annotation; F3 + structure; F4 + in-group context; NS = F4 without any substance-count
feature; ST = structure only; added after the first tables: F4x = F4 without the three membership flags, CS = F4
without any COCONUT-derived feature. LightGBM lambdarank (7 leaves, 300 trees, negatives capped at 100 per group) on
F4. CV: 5 folds by the answer's generic Murcko scaffold; panel, Enveda-180 and not-NP are scored by the fit on all of
OTH-NP (the panel is never in the fit).

**Two label designs.**
- *As briefed* (`main`): negatives = same-formula isomers that are not train structures. Evaluated on the full group
  the fitted arms **lose**: F1 +0.0007 ± 0.0007, F2 −0.0567, F4 −0.0507 ± 0.0014, GBM −0.0351 ± 0.0016. Evaluated the
  way they were trained (answer against non-train isomers) they win: 0.600 → 0.621 (F4), 0.653 (GBM), and Enveda-180
  jumps from 0.184 to 0.583 (F4) and 0.772 (F2). Cause: the pool is train ∪ COCONUT, so every non-train row is a
  COCONUT row and "little COCONUT annotation" identifies a train row. The design measures train membership.
- *All isomers* (`mainA`, a deviation recorded in `PREREG.md` after seeing the above): negatives = every other pool
  row of the formula, which is the list the pipeline ranks. Held-out answers remain negatives in the training folds'
  groups; this is the situation of a hidden answer that sits in our pool as a COCONUT row. **All tables below are
  this design.**

## 3. Leak and circularity

Every labelled answer is a compound of a public spectral library. Four checks.

**(a) Is the fitted gain "is in a catalogue"? Yes** [M]. CS (no COCONUT-derived feature): +0.0006 ± 0.0010. Study
`cocoA` (universe restricted to rows with a COCONUT record; 22,517 answers; current prior 0.477): logit F4
+0.0008 ± 0.0011, CS +0.0001 ± 0.0010, LightGBM +0.0014 ± 0.0015; less famous half +0.0141 ± 0.0013, more famous
half −0.0126 ± 0.0018 (LightGBM +0.051 and −0.048; panel −0.095 ± 0.019). With membership held constant a fitted
prior only moves weight from famous to less famous answers at no net gain.

**(b) Spectral-library depositors inside the substance count.** The local arrays hold one count per structure; the
depositors cannot be separated without `SID-Map.gz` (3.3 GB, not fetched). MoNA (1.32 M substances), MassBank
Europe, HMDB, Metabolomics Workbench and NIST are depositors (`scratch/avail/web/sourcetable.csv`); GNPS is not in
that table. Bound by counterfactual: every train structure gets one or two substance records fewer (floor 0), all
features and baselines recomputed (`minus1A`, `minus2A`).

| Set | chance | current prior | minus 1 record | minus 2 records | share of the signal above chance removed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| OTH-NP | 0.219 | 0.472 | 0.466 | 0.461 | 2% / 4% |
| less famous half | 0.222 | 0.316 | 0.305 | 0.297 | 12% / 20% |
| bottom quartile of substance count | 0.233 | 0.273 | 0.255 | 0.242 | **45% / 78%** |

The fitted logit's gain over the prior is unchanged (+0.0143, +0.0149): it does not come from the count. NS (no
substance-count feature at all) equals the current prior (+0.0005 ± 0.0015): PubMed, patents and COCONUT annotation
reach the same level without it.

**(c) Answers by library** [M]. In MoNA or MassBank (PubChem depositors), 10,232: current prior 0.529, AUC 0.879;
only in libraries that do not deposit (`gnps`, `pluskal_ms2`, `riken`, `msdial`, `spectraverse`, `drug_plus`, `masaryk`), 13,096:
0.427, AUC 0.786 (chance 0.214 and 0.223). The difference is confounded with fame (MoNA and MassBank hold more
common compounds; MoNA also mirrors other libraries) and is not an estimate of the deposit effect; the bound is (b).
The prior works on answers of non-depositing libraries, so the signal as a whole is not a deposit artefact.

**(d) Enveda-only answers.** Not possible on natural products: 0 of the 250 panel structures are only in Enveda
libraries (each is in 2 to 8 other libraries; `extra_db\ref\train_libs.parquet`). Enveda-180 is Enveda-only and
synthetic: counts are below chance there (0.134 against 0.169) and the fitted prior lowers it further (0.126). That
shows only that an Enveda library by itself creates no count enrichment; it says nothing about Enveda's natural
products.

Plain statement. On famous answers the prior is not a library artefact. On answers with few records it largely
could be. The fitted model's extra over the hand-made prior is a membership flag whose value on the hidden test
rests on one assumption: that hidden answers are COCONUT structures more often than their pool isomers are. Nothing
local can test that, since the labelled set was selected by that very flag.

## 6. Plain reading

- "Which isomer do people measure" can be learned, and what is learned is: the one with the most substance records,
  as the hand-made prior already says; beyond that, the one that is in COCONUT. The second is a curated-membership
  flag on a set selected by that flag.
- The hand-made sum is close to the best linear use of the counts. The fitted counts-only model is slightly worse
  in-group, and `popmu` found the substance-only form worse in the replay.
- A fitted prior shifts weight from famous to less famous answers. In-group that looks like a gain on the less
  famous half; in the replay it costs first places on both sets, the Enveda panel first.
- For answers that look like classes 2 and 3 no count-based prior is above chance, and in the bottom quartile most
  of the measurable signal is within what a library's own deposit can explain.
- The in-group measure, clean as it is, did not predict the replay's sign for this change. It measures the prior
  alone; the pipeline needs what the prior adds to a spectral score.
