**Subformula tokens fail, and not because of formula errors.** With the TRUE formula both formula designs are below every
reference seed on natural products (NP-catalogue 0.370 / 0.392 against 0.402 to 0.408, panel 0.342 / 0.368 against 0.397 to
0.426); the pipeline formula costs a further 0.017 to 0.018. Telling the network the formula removes the rows of other
formulas, so the fair yardstick is `iso top-1`: 0.372 / 0.394 against 0.428 for the m/z reference. The formula tokens make the
network **worse at separating isomers**. Plausible reasons (not tested separately): given the precursor formula, the subformula
of a peak is a function of its m/z, so the tokens add no information, they only change the inductive bias; they discard the
exact mass inside the tolerance; 56% of annotated peaks are ambiguous and the smallest-error pick is noise; and the nets fit the
training chemistry faster (training accuracy 0.860 / 0.876 against 0.849) while `b_both` gains on enveda-180 (hold timsTOF 0.705)
and loses on natural products, the specialisation pattern of the ownfp study.

**Formula-free changes help at 16,000 steps.** Pairwise mass differences and multi-spectrum attention each lift NP-catalogue by
+0.013 to +0.025 over the reference mean (two seeds each), enveda-180 by +0.020 to +0.031, hold all by up to +0.020; together
+0.024 / +0.027 (NP-catalogue), +0.026 / +0.036 (hold all). The **panel stays level** in every run (0.400 to 0.424 against
0.397 to 0.426; paired against the best seed −0.026 to −0.003, SE 0.02): nothing here measurably moves the Enveda natural
products against a same-length reference.

## 7. What a hold-out gain can and cannot promise

The hold-out ranks held-out library structures among pool rows of a ±10 ppm window. The hidden test differs: about 45% of its
answers are in none of our lists (`reviews/budget-2026-10-10.md`), the ranker on top of the network turned a level network swap
into a level chain (ownfp P4), and local validation had the leaderboard's sign in 5 to 7 of 11 contrasts this month. A hold-out
gain of +0.02 to +0.04 top-1 on natural-product-catalogue structures is a necessary condition for a leaderboard gain from the
network, not evidence of one.
