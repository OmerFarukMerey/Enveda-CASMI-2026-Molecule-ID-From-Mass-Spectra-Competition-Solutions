# Top-1-locked re-ranking of ranks 2-25 by our own scorers (2026-10-25)

Marks: **[V]** = computed here; **[I]** = inference. Rules: `scratch/top1lock/PREREG.md`, written before any scorer was run on s1's
final lists. Code: `scratch/top1lock/score.py`, `eval_lock.py`, `diag_full.py`, `diag_fp.py`. Outputs: `C:/Users/hemre/casmi-work/top1lock/`
(`eval.json|md`, `eval_rows.parquet`, one pickle per scorer, `seen.json`). Local CPU only. No Kaggle call, no notebook, no git.

## 1. Question and setup

Why this test: `docs/landscape/update-2026-10-25.md` traces the public 0.467 to n1 with RRF offset 1. That offset locks the baseline
rank 1, and every earlier add-on of ours that could change rank 1 lost or was flat on the LB. Here we test whether one of OUR scorers can
earn the same kind of gain by re-ordering only ranks 2-25.

- Base: s1's final lists on the 400 s1val molecules, taken from the dump's `submission.csv`. The base answer ranks equal `replay.py`'s
  `rank_s1` for 400 of 400 molecules [V].
- Rule: row 1 is fixed. Rows 2..n are sorted by fused = 1/(1 + b) + 0.25/(1 + r), where b is the base rank and r is the scorer's rank
  among the movable rows (ties averaged; unscored rows get the neutral rank). There was no sweep. Top-1 changes were asserted to be 0.
- With k = 1 and w = 0.25, only the base rank-3 row can displace the base rank-2 row. Where the answers sit in the base lists: rank 1: 321;
  rank 2: 45; ranks 3-5: 27; ranks 6-25: 7.
- Scorer checks [V]:
  - isoxe on the CPU matches the earlier GPU scores: max |diff| 8e-4 on 1,075 shared rows.
  - Our candidate fingerprint bits equal the network's pool table on 200 of 200 rows.
  - MS2DeepScore uses the DEV-A rule (max / K 50 / wdot) with the molecule's own key removed from the references.
  - Coverage: GLACIER scores 87% of the movable rows and ICEBERG 84%; every other scorer covers 100%.

## 3. Reading

1. **The lock itself works as designed** [V]. No top-1 changed, and only 0-2 of the 45 rank-2 answers were lost per scorer. So a
   top-1-locked rule cannot do large harm on this set. Every |Δ| is ≤ 0.0015 (about 0.6 answers out of 400).
2. **Our scorers order ranks 2-25 worse than s1 does** [V]. Their own MRR among the movable rows is 0.36-0.59, against 0.75 for the
   base order. RRF at w 0.25 therefore mixes in a weaker ranking, and wins and losses roughly cancel. Most scorers lose slightly, and
   answers deep in the list (ranks 6-25) almost always move down.
   - Over the full 25 rows (`diag_full.py`), the scorers are informative on their own: GLACIER 0.60, ICEBERG 0.59, isoxe 0.46,
     MS2DeepScore 0.38, MetFrag-lite 0.31, against random 0.15. They are just not better than s1's own order, which already contains
     the fingerprint net, GLACIER and ICEBERG.
3. **No scorer is close to passing.** No CI on the lib_max < 0.9 subset is above 0. Two subsets are significantly negative:
   MS2DeepScore on its unseen molecules (0 wins / 7 losses) and FP (fp_cos) on all 400.
4. **FP: the pre-registered score form was a mistake on our side** [V]. This network's logits are small (mean |z| 0.15-0.9), so `fp_cos`
   ranks candidates at random (0.157 vs 0.153). The post-hoc f.z form ranks well (0.525 over the full list) and gives the best
   all-400 Δ of the set, +0.0008, but it still fails P2 and P3. This post-hoc result does not change any verdict.
5. **What the harness cannot resolve** [I]. On 400 molecules, the CI half-width is about 0.002, while the public 0.466 → 0.467 step was
   +0.001. A pass here would need a scorer whose within-list MRR is clearly above 0.75. None of ours reaches that; the best is 0.59.

## 4. Recommendation

- Build no notebook for any of these scorers as a top-1-locked re-ranker. All fail the pre-registered rule, and their within-list
  ordering is weaker than s1's.
- The lock is still the right shape for any future add-on: it is cheap and safe. But the scorer must first beat the base order inside
  ranks 2-25. Use this screen before any further test: "own MRR on the movable rows > the base order's 0.746 on the s1val dump". That
  costs minutes and would have rejected all eight scorers here.
- The only top-1-locked gain with LB evidence is sanpier's neural model in n1o, which is a public copy (still unsubmitted; it needs the
  user's approval). Our own path to a gain here needs a stronger ranker of ranks 2-25 (for example a model trained on what separates
  s1's rank-2 and rank-3 rows), not a re-use of the existing scorers.
