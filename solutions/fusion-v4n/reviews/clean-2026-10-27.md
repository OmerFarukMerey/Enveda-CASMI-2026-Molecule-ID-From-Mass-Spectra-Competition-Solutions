## 3. Reading (post hoc unless marked) [I]

- **Leak.** The engine-1 leak is gone on enveda-180: top-1 is 0.33 when the answer is listed. On the panel it is 0.77, and the panel MRR stays at 0.85. Two causes remain:
  - the panel answers stay in the pool through COCONUT, as on the hidden test;
  - 236 of the 250 panel structures are in MassSpecGym, which ICEBERG / GLACIER were trained on. That leak cannot be removed (residual, INVENTORY F-1).
- **Class mix decides the s1v pairs.** The two halves disagree:
  - Panel half: Ga −0.010, which matches the LB's −0.008.
  - Enveda-180 half: +0.022. Its class-`all` answers are almost all PubChem-only. They leave the pool but 97% are in the PubChem tier, so 194 of the 600 answers are PubChem-reachable and almost none are truly novel.
  - So H has far more PubChem-reachable answers than the hidden test seems to have. This matches s1v's earlier break-even estimate: Ga pays only above a PubChem-only share of about 12%.
- **What would make it usable.** Not pre-registered and not tested:
  - reweight the classes to the hidden-test budget;
  - replace the enveda-180 `all` arm with structures absent from PubChem.
  Either change needs a new pre-registration and a fresh LB pair to check it.
- **What it is good for now.** Nothing that "passes" here is evidence for the LB, so it cannot rank changes. Within a stated subset it is a leak-free harm check.
