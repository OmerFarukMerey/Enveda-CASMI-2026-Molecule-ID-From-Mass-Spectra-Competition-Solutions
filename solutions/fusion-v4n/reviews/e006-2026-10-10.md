### 2.2 Why not the literal notebook, and what was run instead
e006's base lists come from `fpnet_full1` and the public ranker's feature models, its PubChem lists from FPNet A + B:
all trained on every training structure, so on any local hold-out the literal notebook ranks the answer first almost
always (rules-and-pitfalls 10: 93.6%). Our own local lists avoid this by putting the fold-safe network under every
stage. **e006u** does the same and nothing else:

### 4.1 Design
Option `E006_FUSE` (`'rrf'` | `'fill'` | `'agree'`, default `None`), with `E006_RRF_K` (3), `E006_RRF_W` (1), `E006_FILL_POS` (6),
`E006_SHARE_ENG` (true), `E006_DEADLINE_S` (27,600 = 7 h 40 min).
- `tools/build_notebook.py`: when the option is set, the 30 code cells of `reference/e006/` are embedded unchanged as
  `e006/cells.json` (+ the notice). Without it nothing of the snapshot is in the notebook. No dataset is added: all
  of e006's inputs that it reads are already ours.
- `nb/09_e006.py` (new last cell; with the flag off it defines `E006_STATS = None` and nothing else): after cell 08
  has written our `submission.csv` (kept as `submission_ours.csv`), `nb/embed/e006_stage.py` turns the cells into one
  script and runs it as a subprocess in `/kaggle/working/e006` (own process group, log to `e006/e006.log`). Three
  text patches, each asserted to match the source exactly: (1) `/kaggle/working` → `/kaggle/working/e006` (21
  places); (2) the engine-2 call goes through a wrapper that copies our `eng_lists.json` instead of running the
  engine again; (3) `OURS_E006_FULL` for full commit runs. `display` is a no-op. A test checks that reversing the
  three patches gives back the cells byte for byte.
- **Shared: engine 2 only.** Same embedded code, same arguments; the gate `engine_shareable` hands the lists over
  only when our `ENG_*` knobs are the reference's and our engine-2 stage did not fall back, otherwise the second
  pipeline runs its own. **Not shared, and why:** the PubChem channel (theirs carries the popularity prior and the
  200-row union, another list), the v1 engine pass (no join, popularity 0.15, their top 60), ICEBERG (their candidate
  set is defined by their own lists; scores are per candidate, so one union run would be possible, but it needs a
  change inside their forward cell and a restriction of the score dictionaries afterwards: not done, cost below),
  GLACIER (theirs scores [M+H]+ spectra only: different scores for molecules with both adducts).
- Fusion on the official metric's key, computed from the SMILES by `e006_stage.metric_key` (a test compares it with
  the snapshot's copy of the metric function) in a worker process with 4 workers. A molecule without a second list
  keeps our row byte for byte. `fusion_core.validate` on the result; `submission_e006.csv` kept; the second
  pipeline's large folders removed.
- **Failure policy:** any exception, a non-zero return code or the deadline (7 h 40 min of notebook time) → "E006 STAGE FAILED -> our own table
  kept", an entry in `FAILS` (so `STRICT` raises on a commit run), and `submission.csv` stays v59a's. The stage
  cannot make the submission worse than v59a except by running the notebook out of time, which the deadline guards.
