## 5. Honesty and limits

- **The panel is leaked for most public models** (`reviews/panel2-2026-10-05.md`, section 2): the public ranker was
  trained with these molecules, ICEBERG / GLACIER have very probably seen 245 of 250 structures, engine 2's FPNets
  were trained on them, the answers are famous (popularity). What that weakens:
  - every **level** in section 2 (pool answers are too easy: `c2` 0.84 here);
  - every **gain from weakening a re-rank term on `c1` / `c2`**: where a leaked ranker already has the answer first,
    any added term can only cost. This applies to the `c1` / `c2` side of the MetFrag result and of hypothesis A, and it
    is the pattern that made forward weights 0.5 look better than 0.75 locally while the leaderboard preferred 0.75.
    The MetFrag result also gains on `dA` / `dB` in the `pub` arm and the mixed regime, where that argument does not
    apply; hypothesis A does not;
  - the **forward models' cost in the cs segment**: the leak favours the answer's forward score, so the cost on pool
    answers is more likely understated than overstated, and the gain on answer-removed types overstated;
  - **exposure**: 2 to 8 exposed `c2` lists on the panel carry no veto (panel2 review); "exposure unchanged" here
    means only that no lever was seen to raise it.
- **The segments were found and tested on the same molecules.** Hypothesis A was formed from the stage table of
  section 2.3 after all three sets had been tabulated; its in-segment numbers are therefore optimistic. The MetFrag
  weight was in the task's list beforehand and has an earlier independent sighting (param-search, mixed regime), but
  the panel result is one 34-molecule segment at 2.1 standard errors.
- **Small segments**: 16 gap molecules, 34 uncovered, 38 cs on the panel; one molecule is 0.004 of a type's MRR and
  0.03 to 0.06 inside a segment. No segment-level number here has a standard error under 0.006.
- **Segment shares of the hidden test are unknown** (section 2.4). If the hidden test has fewer molecules without an
  [M+H]+ / [M+Na]+ spectrum than the panel's 13.6%, the preset touches fewer.
- **The class mix is unknown**; nothing was collapsed into one number except in the null, whose mixes are
  assumptions of earlier reviews.
- **Local stand-ins, as in the panel2 review**: the `pub` arm runs FPNet A, not the production `fpnet_full1`;
  the fusion's PubChem list is the arm's own selection; RDKit 2026.03.3 in the forward runners (`--any-rdkit`); no
  time budgets emulated; local forward scores are not bit-identical to Kaggle's (r 0.997 to 0.998 on one run,
  `reviews/fwd-replay-2026-10-05.md`). The surrogate path itself was never compared with a Kaggle run.
- **Stage attribution** uses one fixed order of stages; "decided by the ranker" means the answer was listed and not
  first in the joint v1 list, not that a better ranker exists (ranker work is closed, `EXPERIMENTS.md`).
