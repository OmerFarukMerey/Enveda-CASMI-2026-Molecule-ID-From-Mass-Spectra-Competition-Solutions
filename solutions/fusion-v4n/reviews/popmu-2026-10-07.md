## 2. Where the prior acts and where it is lost

### 2.1 Fixable and breakable (v33b's final lists, first 25)

Popularity of a row = pool value by metric key, else the tier value, else 0. "More popular" is strict.
- **Fixable**: the answer is at place 2 to 25 and every row above it is less popular. A strong enough prior that reaches
  all rows would put it first.
- **Not fixable**: the first row is more popular than the answer. More prior entrenches the error.
- **Breakable**: the answer is first and a same-formula row in the list is more popular.

  on other libraries' natural products. A stronger prior is a bet that the hidden answers look like the panel's.
- **Where the prior is lost.** After stage A, in the forward re-rank and the engine fusion (41 first places lost, 31 of
  them to a less popular isomer). A second application after the fusion gets them back and loses more on answers outside
  our pool, which cannot receive the prior. The form that would not have that cost (prior only among rows that have a
  pool key, other rows keep their slots, as stage A does) was **not** in the fixed list and was not run.
- **What the less famous half says.** On answers below the fame of an ordinary library natural product, more prior is
  flat at 0.35 and negative from 0.5 on, in every form.

  twelve. Its expected local size (+0.002 pooled) is below one leaderboard answer (0.0038).
