### 1.3 What changes in e006's behaviour [V, `i2_report.py`, counts over the 12 list files of I0]

- **At `lib_max` ≥ 0.7: nothing.** I0's final list equals e006u's on 1,178 of 1,178 lists (our first pass is his

  ranker's (as replayed; the block is his own code); (ii) joined rows carry the generator's `|gen` tag in his
  formula groups, so they are sent to the simulators only among themselves and through the engine-2 union: 4 to
  176 of 6,690 to 23,612 GLACIER candidates per list type have no cached [M+H]+ score locally and keep the fused
  order (as in graft Eb; **not scored**, so that I0 stays comparable with Ea2 / Eb); (iii) his slots skip
  popularity rows that are already joined rows of the first 25, so later popularity rows move up; (iv) ICEBERG
  receives 9% more candidates (215,217 against 197,379 over all list files); (v) commit runs join every molecule
  (our smoke rule), so the 12 visible lists hold joined rows that a scoring run would not add to library hits.
