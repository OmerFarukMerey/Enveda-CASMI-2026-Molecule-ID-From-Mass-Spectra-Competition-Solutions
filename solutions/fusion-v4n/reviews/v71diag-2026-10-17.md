## 0. Bottom line

1. **Defect found (pre-registered D1, promotion inflation by the rescaling)** [V]. At Kaggle's A+B scale the v71 gate rescaling
   (`r_m = SD(old f.z) / SD(new f.z)` over the pool window) does not preserve the rel distribution: on the 359 below-gate molecules of our
   diag67 validation set, `rel > 150` fires **29× with stack1's channel, 101× with v71 (seed 0) and 75× with the 2-seed mean**
   (all wrong: every answer there is a pool structure). v71 at 150 fires as often as stack1 at **rel > 23 (s0) / rel > 59 (2-seed mean, = v71b)**. The public sweep
   already priced a milder version of this (stack1 at rel > 100 fires 54 of 359): on the same base, rel 100 scored 0.444 against 0.451 at rel 150 [C two-notebooks 3].
   Rule D1 (> 1.5× the old firing share) is met with either seed setting.
2. **Not the cause**: the selector's order (locally positive in every cell), an order × gate interaction (H1 not supported: I = +6 [−4, +16]
   masked, +10 [−2, +22] real), r_m outliers in small windows (0 (s0) / 1 (mean) of 400 at Kaggle scale and 1 of 630 locally outside
   [median/3, 3 × median]), the 2-seed mean (logit SD 0.697 vs 0.708; its inflation is smaller, not larger) [V].
3. **Why every local replay said +0.007 / +0.009**: e006u rescales into the fold-safe FPNet's units, where the rescaling is calibrated
   (rel-clause promotions 315 old vs 311 new); and e006u's answer mix rewards extra promotions anyway (stack1 itself at the inflated
   firing rate scores +0.0005 / +0.0126 locally). The harness cannot see this cost; the leaderboard did [V, I].
4. **Correction (pre-registered)**: keep the new network's PubChem order, take the gate quantities (`fz_top`, `best_pool_fz`) from stack1's
   own channel. Local, vs stack1: **+0.0086 ± 0.0034 (120 / 100) masked, +0.0130 ± 0.0033 (76 / 59) real**, positive in both arms; at
   Kaggle scale its rel-clause and slot decisions are stack1's by construction. Built as **`scratch/v71diag/nb/casmi26-sub-v73a/`**
   (switch off = stack1's output). It removes the identified loss mechanism only: the order change itself has never been scored on the
   leaderboard, and local replays have matched the leaderboard's sign only about half the time. **Not fit to submit before a commit run
   shows `V73_PATCH ... rel_or_slot_differ 0` and no `V73_FALLBACK`.**

## 5. Verdict and the correction

**Defect: D1, promotion inflation by the per-molecule rescaling at A+B scale** (3.5× s0, 2.6× mean, rule > 1.5×). Not D2 (r_m explosion),
not D3 (seed mean: firing 75 vs 101, composite mean/mean vs new/new within 1 SE), not H1.

Correction (the one pre-registered for D1): **new order, old gate** = cell 150 new/old:R, +0.0086 ± 0.0034 / +0.0130 ± 0.0033, positive in
both arms. Stricter alternative (S-clause inputs from stack1 too, cell new/old): +0.0086 ± 0.0034 / +0.0078 ± 0.0030 (built as v73b).

**Notebook** [V `scratch/v71diag/build_v73.py`]: base `scratch/nb1016/omer/casmi26-om-stack1.ipynb` (the team's own stack1 v1, Apache 2.0
notice; AST-identical to the eason notebook). 36 of 37 cells byte-identical; inserted: A (switch `V73 = {'pc_net': True, 'gate': 'old'}`,
dataset lookup), B (build_v71's network cell verbatim: logits in a subprocess, self-test against `selftest.npz`), C appended to the PubChem
cell (build_v71's runner patch verbatim, merge self-test `_v73_selftest`, per molecule the new list / S / top_pop with stack1's `fz_top` /
`best_pool_fz`; fallback to stack1's entry when not listed, no logits, either list empty: `V73_FALLBACK` lines; whole-patch fallback on any
exception), D last (`V73_PATCH {stage: report, ...}`: fallbacks, changed lists / first rows, rel-clause / slot / S-clause counts under both
channels, `rel_or_slot_differ` must be 0; raises at the very end on the commit run only, never on the scoring rerun). With `pc_net` False PC

(v71b reconstruction, single seed; local reference only). Kernel metadata: private, stack1's 14 inputs + `huseyinemreaksoy/casmi26-ours-msnet`
(existing private dataset, seed 0 checkpoint; no upload needed). Tests: `solutions/fusion-v4n/tests/test_v73_gate.py` (4 pass;
`test_pcsel_v71.py` still passes). Runtime: the PubChem channel runs twice, as in v71b, which completed its scoring run [I].

## 6. Not verified

- v71b's own code and logs (teammate's private notebook): seed averaging (logits assumed), its exact r rule; its scoring-run firing counts.
- The Kaggle-scale firing counts come from a leaked validation set of pool answers only (cost side); the hidden test's honest A+B scale is
  not observable. The rescaled-rel shift is present with and without the answer rows and in the both-leaked subset, so the direction is
  robust; its size on the hidden test is not known.
- v73a has not been run on Kaggle (no commit run, no validation run). The local gain of the order is the same kind of evidence that
  predicted +0.007 for v71b.
- CPU forward scores for the mean channel's rows (ICEBERG / GLACIER runners with `--device cpu`) may differ slightly from GPU scores.
