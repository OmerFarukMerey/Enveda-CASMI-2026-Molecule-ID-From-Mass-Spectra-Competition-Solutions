## 3. (c) What the channel does in pub-p2 and the e006u replay [V]

**pub-p2, cell by cell** (`casmi-work/pcsel/p2cells/`): cell 14 writes `probe_core2` + the v17 probe and runs `pc_runner.py` (4 workers):
z = FPNet A+B logits (0.5·(mean single + mean merged)), `best_pool_fz` = max f.z over the pool window (±10, fallback 30 ppm); per molecule pass
1 ECFP4-block f.z top 5,000 ∪ 200 most documented, pass 2 full f.z, `fz_top` = best non-pool raw f.z, S = z(f.z) + 0.25·pop, walk in S
order skipping pool keys, 25 rows (`pc`, `pc_fz` = S, `S`, `top_pop`). Cell 21 sends the 25 rows to ICEBERG / GLACIER (lib_max < 0.9) and
re-orders them by z(S) + z(ICE) + z(GL) in formula groups. Cell 23: lib_max ≥ 0.9 untouched; else `rel = fz_top − best_pool_fz` (raw units),
slots [2,4,6,8,10] if rel > 600 else [4,8,12,16,20]; **promotion** of `pc[0]` to rank 1 if (S > 6 and top_pop ≥ 5) or rel > 250. Cell 25
fuses with engine 2 (RRF) and re-ranks by GLACIER; cell 28 fills tails from the PubChem lists; cells 29 / 33 vote and overlay. Only cells
21 / 23 / 28 read the channel.
