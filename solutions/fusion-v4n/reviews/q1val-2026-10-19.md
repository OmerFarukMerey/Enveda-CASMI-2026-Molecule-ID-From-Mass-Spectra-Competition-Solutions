## 1. Why a new harness, and what it can and cannot see

The local replay mispredicted three PubChem-channel / engine-2 ordering changes (v71b, v73a, q1p: local +0.003 / +0.006, LB -0.004)
[C `docs/experiments.md` rows 67, 72, 84]. It replays a stack1-like surrogate chain without PubChemLite and gets its q1p gain entirely
from dA lists, where the answer is artificially taken out of the pool [C row 84]. The new harness replays q1's own last steps on the
lists q1 itself produced at full scale on Kaggle.

What it cannot see, fixed in the PREREG before the run [V `pool_meta` has all 275,810 train structures; the PubChem channel searches
only structures outside the pool]: every validation answer is a **library** answer (class `lib`: its other spectra stay in the
reference library) or a **pool** answer (class `all`: purged from the library, still in the pool). PubChem-only, engine-2-library-only
and no-database answers cannot be produced without changing q1's code, and the hidden test has them. A rule that promotes
PubChem-channel rows (q1p) can therefore show only its **cost** here, never its benefit, so a q1p "PASS" is expected and is a
necessary, not a sufficient, calibration. **Leakage**: q1's networks (FPNet / ranker of engine 1, engine 2's fingerprint models and
rankers, the PubChem channel's FPNet, ICEBERG / GLACIER) were trained on train, i.e. on these molecules (diag67: 99.5% of answers in
the top 25 on a similar set [C]). Absolute MRR is meaningless; only differences between ordering rules on the same lists are read.

Validation set [V dry run on the real train.parquet, 31 s]: 400 molecules, all timsTOF, 1,324 spectra, 63 without [M+H]+ (visible
test: 34 of 400):

| library | class `all` (purged) | class `lib` (library kept) |
|---|---|---|
| enveda-np-examples (Enveda NP panel, "closest to test") | 125 | 125 |
| enveda-180 (same timsTOF, drug-like) | 75 | 75 |

The replay also classifies each answer by where it was retrieved (engine-1 top 60, engine-2 export, engine-2 top 80, PubChem list,
none) and by `lib_max` band; those counts come with the run.

## 5. Can it be trusted for the next ideas?

Not yet: no result. What is already clear [I]: even with a PASS it can be trusted only for rules that reorder library / pool
candidates (shields, fusion weights inside q1's lists, E2R2-like inserts, simulator re-rank windows), and for the cost side of any

test) needs answers outside the pool, which this validation mode cannot create.
