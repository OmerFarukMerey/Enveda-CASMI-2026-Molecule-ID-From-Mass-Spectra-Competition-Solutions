- **Which of the three is best locally: none, by a margin that means anything.** Pooled composite v33b 0.6620, v41a 0.6612,

  standard errors, with small feature drift and no growth of exposure on the mixed regime. (b) The library protection is
  the half that costs locally (−0.002, `dA` on the mixed regime beyond two standard errors), with the true bank as with the
  stand-in. (c) So if the fork's 0.426 against 0.421 is real, the local replay points at `ens` as the more likely source,
  weakly; and **v33b + `ens` without the protection is the variant the local numbers like best** (pooled +0.0018 ± 0.0012
  against v33b). No config file exists for it and none was written.
- **Pattern check.** The pattern that preceded the v34a loss was a local gain carried by lists whose answer already starts
  first. `ens` does not have it: the rank-1 stratum is +0.001 and −0.001 with six wins and five losses in each set, and

  protection's loss, in the stratum the U-regime review found closest to the leaderboard. Neither is beyond two standard
  errors.

  `ens` (joined row first 2 → 4 of 452 pool-answer lists; with FPNet A alone it was 4 and 1). On the mixed regime the count
  falls from 6 to 5 of 530. Two lists; the standing exposure rule asks for no growth of 5.
- **Standing caveat.** `fpnet_full1` was trained on everything: it has seen every molecule of both sets and, unlike A, the
