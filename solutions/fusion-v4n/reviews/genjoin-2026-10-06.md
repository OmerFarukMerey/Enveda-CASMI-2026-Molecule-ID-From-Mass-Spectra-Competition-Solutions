# The generator join, measured honestly (2026-10-06)

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed or committed; no `kaggle` command was run; no model was trained; the visible
`test.parquet` was not read; no NIST data. **No tracked file was changed except this review** (`INDEX.md` and
`EXPERIMENTS.md` untouched: run `python tools/gen_index.py` when this is committed). CPU: at most four processes of mine
at below-normal priority (one stretch of about three minutes had seven, see section 8). GPU: inference only through
the Kaggle packages' runners, one runner at a time, at most 1.8 GB, `gpushare` duty 0.8 and 0.25 while League of
Legends ran. Scripts: `scratch/genjoin/` (git-ignored). Outputs: `D:\casmi-data\genjoin\` (1.1 GB, all new; every other
cache was read, not written).

**Every number below was computed in this study** unless a file is named. MRR = MRR@25 on the metric key; "±" = one
standard error of a paired per-molecule difference against v24a in the same replay; (wins / losses) in brackets.
v24a = the v13a lists (PubChem join N 100 below `lib_max` 0.7, public ranker) with `ICE_LAM` = `GL_LAM` = 0.75. **No
leaderboard estimate is given.** The regimes are leaked to the public models as the earlier reviews describe: the
ranker, engine 2's fingerprint models and both forward models were trained on all of `train.parquet`; only the v1
engine's FPNet is fold-safe here.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **No variant passes the stated rule. Nothing was wired, no preset was written.** The condition that fails for
   every variant is the first one: the gain on answers that are in no database is not beyond two standard errors on
   two of the three sets including the fair one. The best reading per set: mixed regime +0.047 ± 0.033 (1.4 SE, 55
   molecules), Enveda panel with the answer taken out of pool and PubChem list +0.024 ± 0.012 (2.1 SE, M 10 only;
   +0.013 ± 0.011 once relatives above Tanimoto 0.8 are purged), record-less timsTOF molecules +0.023 ± 0.012
   (2.0 SE, five molecules gain). The second condition fails as well for every variant that keeps a gain: answers
   that the PubChem join supplies lose 0.010 to 0.014 (−1.6 to −2.5 SE; mixed regime and panel agree).
2. **Reach is real and survives the purge better than "divide by three"**, but it depends entirely on the chemistry
   having close library relatives. Answer among the first 30 products, answer-only purge → relatives above 0.8
   purged → above 0.7: Enveda panel natural products 54.8 → 44.8 → 38.0%; record-less natural-product-library
   structures 33.0 → 24.7 → 13.0%; **record-less timsTOF molecules (enveda-180) 6.8 → 6.1 → 4.4%**. The curve by the
   nearest remaining library structure (section 2) is the result: under 10% when nothing above 0.6 remains, 15 to
   40% at 0.6 to 0.7, 30 to 60% above 0.7.
3. **Being generated is worth far less than reach suggests, for three measured reasons.** (a) The v1 engine's own
   generator already supplies part of these answers (panel: the baseline without any generated-join row scores
   0.164 on answers that are in no candidate source). (b) A generated answer is placed at 0.36 to 0.41 on the panel
   and 0.57 to 0.67 in the mixed regime, not at 1. (c) The engine-2 fusion pushes generated rows down exactly as it
   does joined PubChem rows: on the panel the v1 side scores 0.240 before the fusion and 0.164 after it.
4. **The three protections do what they were meant to do, and (iii) removes the lever.** (i) alone costs `c2`
   0.010 ± 0.006 (v1 side 0.020 ± 0.007) and adds 4 to 5 exposed lists per 380. (ii) (`conf_gate.p_present` below
   0.5) removes the `c2` cost (+0.003 ± 0.003, 59 of 271 lists still touched) and keeps the whole no-record gain,
   but not the cost on PubChem-supplied answers, which have low `p_present` too. (iii) (support from two parents)
   hands over 0.1 to 2.4 rows per list instead of 5 to 18, has no cost and no exposure, and no gain either
   (+0.002 to +0.005 mixed, +0.000 to +0.004 panel, +0.014 ± 0.010 fair).
5. **Run time is not the obstacle**: 2.0 to 2.8 s per joined molecule on one loaded desktop thread (section 6).
6. What would change the verdict is not a parameter of this join: a ranker that places generated rows (the same
   missing piece as for deep PubChem rows), and knowing whether hidden class-3 answers have relatives above 0.7 in
   the public libraries. Neither can be measured here.

**The set the task asks for does not exist.** `train.parquet` holds no Enveda timsTOF natural product without a
PubChem record: all 250 enveda-np-examples structures have one, and none of the 4,870 record-less enveda-180
structures carries a natural-product catalogue flag (counted from `train_structs.parquet` and
`casmi26-pubchem-popularity-prior/pool_lsid.npy`, `pool_np.npy`). So "fair" is read from two imperfect sets: (c) has
the right instrument and truly no record but the wrong chemistry (amide-coupling library compounds, for which the
generator's natural-product rule table has few moves); (b) with a graded purge has the right instrument and
chemistry but its answers are famous compounds with records, emulated as record-less.

**Graded purge.** For a purge level τ every library spectrum of every training structure with Morgan-2 (2,048 bit)
Tanimoto above τ to the answer is masked in the analog search and in the whole v1 engine run (library features
included), next to the answer's own spectra. All libraries, not only the answer's own source (the same-source
variant is implemented in `gj_reach.py --same_lib` and was not run). Pool rows of relatives stay (they are candidates
only when isomeric). Engine 2 was **not** re-run under the purge: its lists are the answer-only ones at every level.

**Class-3 share of the hidden test.** `docs/competition/data.md` lists the three novelty classes under "hidden mix"
and gives no shares. Nothing else in the documentation states it.

## 3. The replay (task 2)

`gj_run.py` runs the v1 engine as `nb/06` would with the recipe of next-levers section 7:
`gen_join.parents(E.analogs(...), ..., 20)` and `gen_join.records(..., exclude = window keys ∪ PubChem record keys, M,
pc_join.candidate_record)`, the records appended behind the 100 PubChem records, `pc_join` order 'first', public
ranker. Generated rows only where `pc_join.library_max` < 0.7 (protection (i), as for PubChem rows). (ii): the
generated rows are used only when `conf_gate.p_present` of the list without injection is below 0.5 (the PubChem
join stays as in v24a). (iii): only products derived from at least two distinct parents. `gj_replay.py` runs
`fusion_core.build_submission` with the forward scores masked to the candidates `nb/07` would score. Forward scores
for the new candidates: mixed 1,392 of 1,392, panel 873 of 873, fair 14,160 of 14,160 scored by both models; a last
batch of 310 candidates (fair 95, panel 44, mixed 171; the multi-parent variants, the panel's record types and the
purge-0.7 cells) was **not scored** because other processes held more than 8 GB of GPU memory when it was due: those
candidates have no forward score in the replay (at most 0.1% of the candidates of any cell above the 0.7 purge
cells; 2 to 4% in the mixed 0.7 cells). The six pre-specified variants are the first six columns; (i)+(iii)
without (ii) is added for reading.

### 3.4 What the later stages do with a generated-join row (read from `fusion_core.py`, confirmed by the counts)

- **It is handed on exactly like a joined PubChem row** (pool id −2; the SMILES is the product's). Every flag that
  acts on joined rows (`JOIN_FWD_A`, `FUSE_INJ_CREDIT`) would act on it; both are off in v24a.
- **Popularity** (`popularity_reorder`): a row with pool id below 0 has no popularity term, keeps its slot, and the
  pool rows are re-sorted around it. No PubChem record means nothing here either way.
- **Stage A**: its formula gets the `|gen` tag, so it sits in a same-formula group of generated and joined rows
  only. ICEBERG and GLACIER score it (coverage above) but can move it only against other joined / generated rows of
  its formula, never past a pool row. The counts agree: rows in the first 25 fall a little from the ranker list to
  the v1 side (4.00 → 3.19 mixed) through the slot merge of stage B, the number of lists it tops does not (33 → 34);
  on the fair set the forward terms lift a generated answer among its generated neighbours (0.58 → 0.77 to 0.83).
- **Engine-2 fusion: yes, it pushes them down as it does joined PubChem rows.** Engine 2 cannot list a generated
  structure, so the row keeps only the v1 share of the reciprocal-rank weight; the post-fusion re-rank (tag gone)
  returns part. Lists topped by a generated row: mixed `c2` 6 → 2, `dB` 34 → 31, panel `dBn` 24 → 19. A generated
  answer: 0.744 → 0.666 (mixed `dB`, M 10), 0.736 → 0.578 (no-record at purge 0.8), 0.418 → 0.409 (panel); in `dA`,
  where engine 2 holds the answer, it rises (0.744 → 0.910). The whole type shows it most plainly: panel `dBn` under
  v24a is 0.240 on the v1 side and 0.164 after the fusion.

## 4. Why the panel gains so little from so much reach

Panel `dBn`, M 10, (i): the answer is a generated-join row in 75 of the 164 lists below the gate (46%), yet the type
gains +0.024 after the fusion and loses 0.005 on the v1 side. The baseline already holds part of these answers: the
v1 engine's own generator (6 parents, cap 150; `generate=True` in every run here as in the notebook) produces them
as pool-id −1 rows (panel2 review: it re-creates 40% of the removed answers), which is why v24a scores 0.164 with the
answer in no candidate source. With `pc_join` order 'first' the generated-join row replaces that row, sits last in
the candidate order (the worst order-coded rank features, next-levers section 5), and comes with about six more generated decoys. So
on the regime with the most reach the join mostly re-labels answers the engine had, at a slightly worse rank on the
v1 side (26 lists up, 26 down), and the net gain appears only after the fusion (25 up, 21 down). How many of the 75
answers the engine's own generator already held was not counted separately.
