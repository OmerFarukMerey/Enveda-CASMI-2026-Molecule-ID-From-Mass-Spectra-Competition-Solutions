## 0. Why the neural model, and the warning

The 0.466 -> 0.477 gains of the public line all run through Saner Turhaner's spectrum-candidate model (n1 fusion 0.25, leader-safe
ranks 2-25 at 0.50 then 0.75, and the fragment check's neural gates). That model is undertrained: 16 x 500 steps of batch 96, about a
third of one pass over 2.43 M spectra, loss still falling. But Ahmed Berat Özer's 10x longer run of the same recipe **lost on the LB**
(busyaprime e3 / e4: 0.453 / 0.461 against 0.472 with sanpier's model) [C]. So "train it longer" alone is not a ticket.

## 1. Offline: the long model is the better scorer [V]

External CC0 spectra (senanuretin's GNPS2 and MassBank 2026.03 snapshots) minus near-copies of train spectra (cosine >= 0.90 to a
train spectrum of the same structure): XS = 171 train structures with new spectra, XU = 24 structures absent from train. Each
structure against 255 decoys (same formula first, then nearest mass), scored as the notebook's `neural_scores.py` scores a list.

| model | XS MRR@25 / top-1 | XU MRR@25 / top-1 | LB in the 0.75 consensus |
|---|---|---|---|
| sanpier `final_model.pt` (S0) | 0.237 / 0.094 | 0.204 / 0.042 | 0.472 (no fragment check) [C] |
| Ahmed Berat Özer long (AL) | **0.423 / 0.263** | **0.517 / 0.333** | 0.461 [C] |
| S0 + sanpier's best-validation checkpoint (E2) | 0.249 / 0.094 | 0.228 / 0.083 | not run |
| S0 + our 4 new seeds of the recipe (N3) | 0.254 / 0.111 | 0.227 / 0.083 | not run |

Stand-alone retrieval does not predict the fused score. Working explanation [I]: the pipeline's own networks are trained on all of
train (dmitriigluzdov's fold-safe FPNets rank 97% of seen molecules first, 68% of a held-out fold); a neural model that memorises
the same structures adds correlated, not independent, evidence, and the rank-1 rules amplify shared mistakes.

Seen-vs-unseen decoy bias (mean within-set z of training-structure decoys minus unseen decoys, same formula): -0.06 to -0.09 SD
(short) and -0.08 to -0.11 SD (long). Small; not the 0.34 SD heliosli reports for its re-ranker.
