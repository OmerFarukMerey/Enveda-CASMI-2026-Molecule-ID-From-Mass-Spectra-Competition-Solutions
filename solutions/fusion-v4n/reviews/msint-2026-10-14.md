# Integrating a multi-spectrum fingerprint network: engine map, insertion points, adapter, DEV test (2026-10-14)

Marks: **[V]** = run, read or computed in this study (file named); **[C]** = cited from a review; **[I]** = inference.
No leaderboard estimate is given. Nothing was submitted, pushed, committed or downloaded; no notebook, engine, study
file or checkpoint was modified. GPU: one timing run of 0.4 GB for under 5 s next to the specenc trainer (section 3).
CPU: one process at a time, 3 threads, below-normal priority. Pre-registration of the DEV test written before any
benchmark output: `scratch/msint/PREREG.md`.

## 0. Status in one paragraph

**As an extra ranker column (insertion point d) the multi-spectrum network adds nothing measurable on DEV; as a
stand-alone similarity it is clearly better, which is what the PubChem selection (insertion point c) uses.**
Pre-registered test: `cd_pairmulti`'s f.z as four extra inputs of our ranker, refitted out of fold on DEV: primary gain
**+0.0016 ± 0.0033** (rule: beyond 2 SE) → **not met, FINAL not evaluated**. The same with `base_s0`: −0.0034 ± 0.0026
(the level of joint1's null control, −0.0030 ± 0.0020 [C]); paired cd − base +0.0051 ± 0.0031. Stand-alone, the
network ranks the same lists much better (MRR on c2J / dropJ 0.319 / 0.316 against `base_s0` 0.271 / 0.262 and the

## 1. Where the fingerprint network enters (read end to end) [V]

Code: the v1 engine is the public package of `casmi26-v4b-models` (`code/casmi`, `code/fe_v4`); locally the full copy
is `D:\casmi-data\ranker_study\ds\v4b\code\` (the copy under `input/datasets/.../casmi26-v4b-models/code/casmi` holds
only `__init__.py`, `chem.py`, `fpnet.py`). ENG = `casmi/engine.py` of that copy; S = `solutions/fusion-v4n`.

**Network call and batching (v1 engine, `Engine.run`).**
- Input: a list of spectrum dicts `(mz, it, mode ±1, adduct, prec, ce, ce_n)` (ENG:238), built by `S/nb/06_base_lists.py:22-28`
  from the raw `test.parquet` rows (raw peaks; `ce` = mean of `collision_energy_ev`, `ce_n` = its length or 1).
  Only the first 16 spectra are used (ENG:243, `max_query_spectra` 16 at ENG:38).
- **Two kinds of items, two bank calls per molecule** (ENG:336-361): one *single* item per spectrum
  (`fpnet.prep_peaks`: 0 < m/z ≤ prec + 2, floor 0.001, top 160, sqrt intensity; ENG:339-345), and one *merged* item per
  polarity over ALL that polarity's spectra whatever their adducts (`merge_spectra` 0.005 Da on base-peak-normalised
  spectra, median precursor, mean known CE, most frequent adduct with a hash-seed tie, `n_merged` = summed CE counts
  capped at 8; ENG:346-359). `zs = bank.logits_el(items_s)`, `zm = bank.logits_el(items_m)` (ENG:360-361).
  `ModelBank.logits_el` averages the logits of the bank's models per item (`casmi/fpnet.py:187-211`, mean at 206-207).
- **Aggregation = means of logits, no max, no best spectrum, not per adduct** (ENG:362-366):
  `z_single = zs.mean(0)`, `z_merged = zm.mean(0)` (= mean of the positive and negative merged items when both exist),
  `z_avg = 0.5·(z_single + z_merged)`; candidate scores `fz_s = cfp·z_single`, `fz_m = cfp·z_merged`,
  `fz_avg = cfp·z_avg` with `cfp` the candidate's 10,226 selected bits (0/1). Sigmoid only for the secondary features
  (ENG:367-374). Element head averaged the same way (ENG:363).
- Checkpoint: `fpnet_full1.pt` by default (`FP_BANK 'full1'`, `S/nb/05_v1_engine.py:21-33`; `nb/00_config.py:8`).
  Our `clean/SPEC.md:116-133` describes the same (correction: the merged item joins all spectra of a polarity up to
  the 16-spectrum cap, not "up to eight"; 8 caps only `n_merged`).

**What consumes it.** Not a molecule fingerprint vector downstream: per-candidate f.z values and features of them.
- Engine columns depending on the network (ENG:369-393, 450-462): `fp_ll`, `fp_ll_gap`, `fp_cos`, `fp_tan`; `fz`,
  `fz_z`, `fz_rank`, `fz_gap`, `fz_norm`, `fz_top`, `fz_softmax`, `fz_grp_ent`, `fz_grp_gap`, **`fz_single`,
  `fz_single_gap`, `fz_merged`, `fz_merged_gap`** (the per-spectrum view and the merged view separately); `el_c`,
  `el_n`, `el_o`, `el_sum`, `el_rank` (element head); crosses `lib_x_fz`, `ap_x_fz`, `agree_lib`, `agree_ap`, `fr_x_fz`;
  `gen_rank_fz`. The network also gates feature computation: fragment features only for the top 600 by `fz_avg`
  (ENG:41, 420-422), fe_v4 `frag2` top 600 and `fragnet` top 400 by `fz`, their "lead" = the best-`fz` candidate.
- fe_v4 `model_views` (A / B / D networks of the v4b manifest, `fe_v4/v1engine.py:78, 87-94`): `fzABD_gap`,
  `fzABD_rank`, `fzD_*`, `rank_spread`, `rank_worst` (per-model, per-spectrum rank statistics).
- The public ranker `ranker_v4b.pkl` (160 inputs = 94 engine + 66 family; `nb/05_v1_engine.py:39-47`). Its fingerprint
  margins carry about 60% of its gain [C `fsranker-2026-10-06.md` §2(ii)].
- Our rankers: `casmi26-ours-ranker-fs` 165 inputs (94 `x__`, families, 5 `j__` incl. `j__fz_rel_pool`;
  `nb/embed/ranker_infer.py:94-146`).

**Our second pass** (`RANKER 'fs' / 'fs_avg'`): `nb/05_v1_engine.py:54-63` loads the fold-safe network
(`RANKER_FS_NET`, default the public `fpnet_fold0.pt`) as `FS_BANK` and as the `model_views` bank, and our ranker
(`RANKER_FS_DS`). `nb/06_base_lists.py:97-124`: below `lib_max` 0.7 the engine is re-run inside
`fs_pass.swapped(E, V, FS_BANK, FS_VIEWS)` (`nb/embed/fs_pass.py:34-53`: monkeypatch of `E.bank` and `V.banks`, one
model fills the A, B, D slots), scored by our ranker (`fs_pass.fs_score`, 56-61) and rank-averaged with the public
ranker's list at weight 0.5 (`fs_pass.rank_average` 94-112). The network enters through the same `bank.logits_el`
calls as above.

**PubChem channel** (ours `nb/04_pubchem.py:12-16` → `nb/embed/pc/pc_runner.py`; e006 cell 13 → the same runner):
FPNet A + B of `casmi26-v3-models` in one ModelBank (`pc_runner.py:24-27`), all spectra of the molecule (first 16),
`probe_core2.molecule_logits` = `0.5·(mean single + mean merged-per-polarity)` (`nb/embed/pc/probe_core2.py:30-59`),
then a **similarity ranking only**: pass 1 partial f.z on the ECFP4 block (top 5,000), pass 2 full f.z, dedupe, skip pool
keys (`probe_core2.py:105-145`); e006 adds popularity (`S = z(f.z) + 0.25·pop`, `probe_one_pop` 168-238). No trained model.

**e006's chain (0.443 / the 0.444 family, `reference/e006/…ipynb`; pub-p2 and v70a have the same cells, shifted)**
- Engine 1: `fpnet_full1` bank (cell 16:11-14) + fe A / B / D (`V1FE`, 16:15) → public ranker `ranker_v4b.pkl`
  (16:20-27); base lists cell 18.
- PubChem: A + B as above (cell 13), then gates in **raw f.z units**: `rel = fz_top − best_pool_fz`, slots aggressive
  when `rel > REL_TH`, promotion when `S > 6 and pop ≥ 5` or `rel > 250` (cell 22:25, 57-63).
- Engine 2 (prvsiyan): `fp_single_s2` + `fp_merged_m1` (6,930 bits, `casmi26-fp-models-v2`), `pv_fp.molecule_logits`
  = mean of (mean over spectra of the single nets) and (one merged pseudo-spectrum) (`nb/embed/eng/pv_fp.py:148-182`),
  used only by `pv.rank_features` and an sklearn HGB ranker (`casmi_engine.py:352, 391-431`).
- After that no trained ranker: ICEBERG / GLACIER z-rerank, RRF fusion with engine 2, the window-40 overlay.

## 2. The interface question: one fingerprint per molecule (or per polarity) at the aggregation point

The multi-spectrum network takes exactly what the engine's **merged item** is: all spectra of one polarity, 1 to 8 in
training (`clean/specenc.py` `build_item`, `multi=True`; `train_fpnet.groups_of` and `specdata.group_of` cap groups at 8).
So the slot exists: **replace `zm` (ENG:361) by the multi-spectrum network's logits per polarity**. It is not a
drop-in for the bank, because `ModelBank.logits_el(items)` receives finished per-item tensors without spectrum
boundaries; a shim must take the molecule's spectrum dicts instead (the adapter of section 3 does).

What changes meaning if only `zm` is replaced: `fz_merged`, `fz_merged_gap` (now the new network), and every `z_avg`
feature (`fz*`, `fp_*`, crosses, the top-600 / top-400 gates, `gen_rank_fz`) becomes half old network, half new.
If `z_single` is also replaced (the new network on one spectrum = 1-spectrum groups, which it saw in about 70% of
training items, `merge_p` 0.3), then `fz_single*` are the new network too and the per-spectrum view no longer differs
in kind from the merged one. Per-spectrum agreement features exist only in `model_views` (rank statistics over the
A / B / D models' views, `fams/model_views.py:34-66`); they are untouched unless the fe banks are swapped too.
A ranker fitted on the old network's values meets a different scale and margin distribution (section 4c).

## 3. The adapter (`clean/msadapt.py`, `tests/test_msadapt.py`) [V]

- `MolSpectra(spectra)` takes the engine's spectrum dicts or raw rows (`from_rows(test_rows_of_one_molecule)`) and
  rebuilds the training cache's representation: `chemcore.clean_peaks` (0 < m/z ≤ prec + 2, floor 0.001, top 512,
  base peak 1), CE = mean of the finite values (NaN if none), `ce_n` = their count, `specdata.ADDUCT_IX` (59 = other),
  mode. `SpecFP(ckpt)` loads any `clean/specenc.py` checkpoint (also the reference layout, e.g. `base_s0`) and builds
  the items with **the trained code itself** (`specenc.build_item`, `collate`, the model's own pair features), so
  tokenisation, per-spectrum peak budget `max(8, 160 // n)`, pairwise differences, slots, CE and adduct per peak are not
  re-implemented. Grouping: per polarity, more than 8 spectra cut into ceil(n/8) near-equal chunks; molecule logits =
  mean over polarities of the spectrum-count-weighted chunk means (the engine's merged-view convention).
  `scores(z, bits)` = f.z and the engine's `fp_cos`; `unpack` = the pool tables' bit order.
- **Unit tests** (7, synthetic spectra, no competition data; all pass [V]): raw rows == what `specdata.build_cache`
  writes for training (a synthetic `train.parquet` through the real cache builder: peaks, precursor, adduct index,
  mode, CE mean and count, including NaN / empty CE lists, peaks above the precursor, non-positive m/z, an unknown
  adduct); engine dicts == rows; items == `build_item`; grouping and chunking; batch invariance and the aggregation;
  scores and unpack; a reference-layout checkpoint.
- **Train / inference parity on real data** (`scratch/msint/parity.py`): 300 seeded `hold all` query groups of the
  study's evaluation, re-read from **raw `train.parquet` rows**, through the adapter on CPU, against the cache items and
  the study's stored GPU ranks:

  | Network | items identical | rank of the true structure identical | top-1 adapter / stored |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `cd_pairmulti` (16k, multi + pair) | **300 / 300** | **300 / 300** | 0.683 / 0.683 |
  | `base_s0` (reference, merged item) | 299 / 300 | 298 / 300 (max difference 1 rank) | 0.670 / 0.670 |

  The one `base_s0` difference: the training cache's top-512 cap breaks ties at the cut differently from
  `chemcore.clean_peaks` (spectrum with 2,319 peaks, 73 tied at the cut); none of six candidate tie rules reproduces
  the cache on 300 capped spectra (`casmi-work/msint/dbg4.py`), so the cache was built by other code. 17,235 of
  2,539,608 training spectra (0.68%) reach the cap. It cannot change a multi-spectrum item (its per-spectrum budget of at
  most 160 peaks is taken from the most intense peaks, above any tie at the 512 cut); it can change a merged item when a
  tied low peak merges with a neighbour (the case seen). The engine itself has no 512 cap (`prep_peaks` on raw peaks).
- **Time per molecule** (benchmark molecules, 3.6 spectra on average, items built included):

  | | `cd_pairmulti` | `base_s0` |
  |---|---|---|
  | CPU, 3 threads, float32 (5,350 molecules) | 70.7 ms | 66.1 ms |
  | GPU (RTX 4070 Ti SUPER, float16, 300 molecules, batch 32) | 1.45 ms (peak 0.38 GB) | 1.04 ms (peak 0.36 GB) |

  GPU run: 9.5 GB in use before (the trainer + desktop), 2 GB cap set, under 5 s; the trainer kept running at duty 0.8
  (`casmi-work/msint/gpu_time.json`). For 2,000 hidden molecules on a T4 that is seconds [I]; on Kaggle's CPU about
  2.5 min at this rate [I, CPU speed not measured there].

### 4d. What these local tests can and cannot tell

They say the network orders held-out library molecules' candidates better on its own (4a alone, 4b), and that our
ranker, given the fold-safe network's features already, extracts little more from it (4a). They cannot say how e006's
chain or the leaderboard reacts: diag67 measured a list change that validation favoured by +0.045 lose 0.03 on the
leaderboard [C]; the benchmark's lists come from the v1 engine with the public fold-safe network, not from e006's chain;
the hidden answers that no channel lists (about 45% [C specenc PREREG §5]) are untouched by any re-ordering.

## 5. Plan for a full-size Kaggle validation (described, not launched)

**Which insertion point.** Not (d): it failed its pre-registered DEV rule, and in the 0.444 family our ranker's list
reaches e006's chain only through `E006_BASE`, the v67 route that lost on the leaderboard [C diag67]. Not (b): the public
ranker cannot be refitted and the scale shift of 4c hits its strongest inputs. **(c), the PubChem channel's selection**,
is the only point where the measured advantage (stand-alone order, 4a / 4b) is what the code uses and no fitted model
