# Extra candidate databases (2026-10-04): are we missing catalogue structures?

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed, uploaded or committed, no Kaggle notebook was run, no existing file was
edited, the GPU was not used. Scripts: `scratch/extra_db/` (git-ignored, log in `scratch/extra_db/LOG.md`). Downloads
and outputs: `D:\casmi-data\extra_db\` (2.5 GB). Keys: first pass by the standard InChIKey first block (RDKit
2026.03.3) of the pipeline's standardised structure (`casmi.chem.standardize_smiles`: largest fragment, uncharged
where possible, no stereo); second pass by the metric key (`casmi.chem.score_key`) for every structure that looked new.
"Eligible" = neutral, C H N O P S F Cl Br I only, 150 to 1,250 Da (the PubChem tier's own filter; the hidden test is
157 to 1,159 Da).

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The pool's COCONUT part is today's COCONUT.** Of the 452,385 eligible structures in the COCONUT release of
   October 2026, **21** are not pool rows under the metric key. The pool holds 97% of the connectivities COCONUT
   added after its December 2025 release, so it was built from a 2026 release (COCONUT's content has been stable
   since March 2026).
2. **The PubChem tier is a complete PubChem snapshot inside its filter**, and the filter is: stereo removed, one
   fragment, no isotopes, C H N O P S and halogens only, 150 to 1,250 Da, **net charge zero**. 105,875,489 rows,
   89,944,373 distinct connectivities.
3. **No other catalogue holds a population we lack.** Six catalogues, 455,794 distinct eligible structures in
   total: **3,146** are not pool rows (2,320 of them are PubChem-tier rows already), **826** are in none of our
   three sources. 2,847 of the 3,146 come from NP-MRD.
4. **Proxy:** 249 of the 250 `enveda-np-examples` structures are COCONUT structures (in the pool), 245 are in the
   PubChem tier; the one that COCONUT lacks is in no other catalogue. The structures the catalogues would add sit
   at 0.6 per 10 ppm window (mean; median 0) against 56 pool rows and 6,084 PubChem rows.
5. **Recommendation: no extra tier is justified by the evidence.** A 3,146-structure tier was built anyway because
   it costs under one extra candidate per molecule (`D:\casmi-data\extra_db\np_extra_tier`, 0.5 MB, section 5); it
   can be tried as a free rider on another submission, not as a submission of its own. The missing half of the
   hidden answers is not in a catalogue we forgot: it is either in PubChem among several thousand window rows, or
   in no database.

| PubChem tier `ahmedberatozer/casmi26-pubchem-tier` | 105,875,489 rows, 150.00 to 1,250.00 Da, 6.7 GB. Card: "CID-SMILES + CID-Mass, stereo stripped, organic CHNOPS+halogens, 150-1250 Da", uploaded 2026-09-23 | card, arrays |

Checks (`s05_tier_probe.py`, `s09_tier_holes.py`, `s10_coconut_release.py`, `s08_charged.py`):

- **Tier content**, 1,000,000 sampled rows: no multi-fragment SMILES, no stereo marks, no isotope labels, no element
  outside C H N O P S F Cl Br I. 4.6% of rows carry charge marks; they are charge-separated neutrals (nitro groups,
  zwitterions such as glycerophosphocholine).
- **Net-charged structures are excluded from the tier**: berberine, palmatine, cyanidin and choline are absent;
  caffeine, quercetin, rutin, paclitaxel, silibinin and amoxicillin are present. Deuterated caffeine maps onto the
  caffeine row (isotopes are not separate rows). Selenium and boron compounds are absent.
- **Completeness inside the filter.** 18,152 eligible COCONUT structures (4.0%) have no tier row. Of a sample of
  80 asked from PubChem by InChIKey, 73 have no PubChem record and 7 have one (old CIDs, so not a matter of snapshot
  date). So about 9% of the "COCONUT but not tier" structures, roughly 1,600, are tier holes; 16,744 of the 18,152
  are pool rows anyway. NPAtlas structures first published in 2021, 2022 and 2023 are tier rows for 100%, 100% and
  98.6% (1,219 / 1,355 / 71 structures): the snapshot is recent.
- **COCONUT release behind the pool.** 100.0% of the pool's COCONUT-only rows are in the October 2026 release.
  Against older releases: of the connectivities present in October 2026 and absent from September 2025 (37,440)
  the pool holds 96.7%; absent from December 2025 (29,579), 97.0%. The lite files of March to October 2026 have
  the same size within 0.01%. So: a 2026 release, not the 2024 one.
- **The pool's COCONUT part also drops net-charged structures.** Of 4,933 net-charged organic COCONUT structures
  between 150 and 1,250 Da (quaternary alkaloids, anthocyanidins), 236 are in the pool (as training structures)
  and 248 in the tier. This is the one real hole found. It probably does not matter for the hidden test: the
  training file labels such structures `[M]+` (or wrongly `[M+H]+`) in massbank, riken, pluskal and gnps, the hidden
  test has no `[M]+` among its ten adducts, and neither `enveda-np-examples` nor `enveda-180` (the two libraries
  made by the host's pipeline) contains a single net-charged structure (509 in the other libraries).

- The test "remove the structure from the pool and ask which catalogue would return it" has a trivial answer:
  COCONUT returns 99.6% of the np-examples, and COCONUT is the pool.

## 5. The extra tier (built, not applied)

`D:\casmi-data\extra_db\np_extra_tier\` (0.5 MB), built by `scratch/extra_db/s11_build_tier.py`, read by
`scratch/extra_db/np_extra.py` (self-test passes: 300 window look-ups, exclusion, cap).

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `npx_mass.npy` | float64, 3,146 exact masses, ascending (the layout of `pc_mass.npy`) |
| `npx_off.npy` | int64, 3,147 offsets into the buffer (`pc_off.npy`) |
| `npx_smiles.npy` | uint8 ASCII buffer of standardised stereo-free SMILES (`pc_smiles.npy`) |
| `npx_keys.npy` | S14 metric keys, row-aligned: no tautomer canonicalisation at run time |

The files are named `npx_*`, not `pc_*`, on purpose: `nb/01_setup.py` locates the PubChem tier with
`find('pc_smiles.npy')` and would otherwise pick up the wrong directory. Rows: NP-MRD 2,847, NPASS 166, LOTUS 145,
NPAtlas 116, CMAUP 64, COCONUT 21 (a row can have several sources); 3,093 between 157 and 1,159 Da; none is a pool
row (200 sampled rows re-keyed against `pool_meta.parquet`). Provenance and licences: section 2. A CC0 / CC BY only
variant is `np_extra_tier_open` (COCONUT + LOTUS, 154 rows). The tier inherits CC BY-NC from NP-MRD and NPAtlas and
contains unlicensed NPASS / CMAUP rows (230 rows have no other source); rebuild with
`python s11_build_tier.py <dir> coconut,lotus,npatlas,npmrd` to leave them out.

## 6. What this does not show

- The hidden answers cannot be seen. "Half of the answers are in none of our candidate sets" is consistent with
  what was counted here (the catalogues are in the pool, PubChem is in the tier) only if those answers are
  PubChem structures the channel fails to list, or structures in no database. This study cannot tell the two apart.
- HMDB, FooDB, Super Natural 3 and KNApSAcK were counted only through their COCONUT imports.
- Tier membership rests on PubChem's keys for the tier rows, not on re-keying 106 million rows.
- The net-charged hole (section 1) was judged from the training file's labelling, not tested.
