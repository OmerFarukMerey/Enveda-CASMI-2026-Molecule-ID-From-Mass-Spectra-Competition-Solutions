## 1. What was replayed

`scratch/combo/cm_replay.py`, built on `scratch/fwd/replay.py` and the out-of-fold scores of
`D:\casmi-data\verifier\oof_v13a.parquet` (imported / read, not edited).

- **JOIN_FWD_A**: the flag of `nb/embed/fusion_core.py` as it is in the working tree.
- **Promotion**: as in the verifier review, before stage A: the best-scored joined row of a list goes to the first
  place if its out-of-fold p is at or above the threshold chosen inside the cross-validation for list-level precision
  0.8, and that row alone gets `FUSE_INJ_CREDIT` 1.0. Replayed once per CV repeat (5) with that repeat's scores and
  threshold; reciprocal ranks and exposure are averaged over the repeats per molecule, so exposure counts can be
  fractions. Two verifier variants: the logistic model without the confidence-gate probability ("noconf": the one the
  prototype holds and the only one a notebook could run without a second engine pass) and the logistic "core" model
  (the verifier review's headline row).
- **Both together.** The pool id of a joined row is −2; JOIN_FWD_A and the fusion credit both find rows by it. Here the
  joined rows that are not promoted are tagged −3 and stage A reads "joined" as pool id ≤ −2 (one token of
  `fusion_core`, changed in a copy in memory, not on disk), so JOIN_FWD_A acts on every joined row including the
  promoted one and the credit reaches the promoted row only. With JOIN_FWD_A off the −3 tag gives the lists of the
  verifier review's −1 tag in every repeat (same MRR and exposure, "wiring check" in section 4).
- **Forward weights**: `ICE_LAM` = `GL_LAM` = 0.75 or 0.5 (stage A and, through the `None` defaults of
  `POST_ICE_LAM` / `POST_GL_LAM`, the post-fusion re-rank), as in the param-search scan.
- **Exposure**, on the final list of `c2` (380 lists) and `c1` (150): **joined first** = the first row entered only
  through the join; **answer lost first** = that, and the answer is the first row that is not joined; **in 25** =
  joined rows in the first 25 per list; **above** = lists with a joined row above the answer.
- **MetFrag cache.** The param-search review completed the regime's MetFrag cache (its v13a: 0.8223 / 0.7338 / 0.4978

## 3. Null: is any of this distinguishable from picking the best of five families?

`scratch/combo/cm_null.py`, the sign-flip null of the param-search review on this candidate set. Six candidates (the
five families, family (d) at both weights). Per molecule the summed effect under a mix is the sum over its types of
weight × difference / type size, so the sum over the 380 molecules is the mix of the per-type mean differences. One
random sign per molecule, the same for every candidate and type (20,000 draws): correlations between candidates stay,
every true difference becomes zero. "p alone" = one-sided, that candidate only. "p best of six" = share of draws in
which the best of the six null sums reaches the candidate's observed sum (in brackets: the same on the scale sum / SE,
which does not favour candidates that move many molecules). Mixes were fixed in the script before it ran: equal
(0.25 each: the summed effect proper), LB-like (`c1` 0.15, `c2` 0.36, `dA` 0, `dB` 0.22; the assumption of the
param-search review), and `c1` + `c2` only (0.5 / 0.5; no answer-removed term).

With −1 tags JOIN_FWD_A sees a joined row only in the lists that have a promoted row, and there it sees that row
alone: the promoted row gets the reduced forward weight while the other joined rows of its `|gen` group keep the full
one, and it loses the place the promotion gave it. A wiring needs (i) a tag for "joined, no credit" that is not −1
(−3 here; `popularity_reorder` only tests `pid < 0`), (ii) `p <= -2` instead of `p == -2` in the `jrow` line of stage A,
(iii) the credit left on `p == -2`. With both flags off no list holds a −3 and nothing changes.

## 5. Honesty and limits

- **The 380 molecules are leaked for the public models.** ICEBERG and GLACIER have very likely seen every answer and
  engine-2's fingerprint models have memorised them. What that weakens here:
  - every `dA` / `dB` number: the forward scores favour the joined answer more than they will on the hidden test, so
    the `dB` cost of JOIN_FWD_A and of lower weights is more likely overstated, and the promotion's `dB` gain (a row
    the verifier recognises partly through forward margins, then credited) more likely overstated too. The statement
    "the two cancel on `dB`" is therefore a statement about this regime only;
  - the exposure counts: pool answers are helped by the same leak, so 25, 22 and 20 are more likely understated,
    and their order between variants may not carry over;
  - the verifier's threshold: its precision at the top was 0.67 to 0.75 on the second hold set against 0.82 to 0.91
    in the regime's CV (verifier review, section 5); a lower precision on the hidden test puts decoys first with
    credit on pool-answer lists, which no row here measures.
- **Out-of-fold, not the notebook's model.** The promotion rows use fold models and nested thresholds. A fixed model
  fitted on all regime molecules with one threshold was not replayed (it would be in sample here), and a
  fixed-coefficient verifier with the public A+B selection model on Kaggle is unverified, as in the verifier review.
- **The pass / fail test is thin where it decides.** (d) at 0.75 is inside the `dB` limit by 0.005; the "core"
  promotion row fails on one `c2` list in one of five repeats; exposure has no error bar. The test has never been
  checked against a submission that scored above v13a.
- **The null answers one question**: whether the best of these six could arise with no true difference, given their
  observed spreads. It does not test exposure, and a candidate that reshuffles many lists with a net of zero looks
  the same to it as noise. Three mixes were looked at; only the equal mix is "the summed effect", the other two are
  assumptions of earlier reviews.
- **Selection**: the six candidates were given, not searched; the two verifier variants and two MetFrag caches are
  sensitivity runs. The five component rows are context and were not ranked.
- **Local stand-ins unchanged**: fold-safe FPNet for the public A+B models in the v1 engine and the PubChem list;
  local forward scores (r 0.997 to 0.998 against one Kaggle dump); 74 molecules without forward coverage contribute
  nothing to any row.
- **Not done**: step 3's module, test, preset and notebook build (by the task's own condition); the v22a lists; the
  LightGBM verifier; the "answer lost first" count for the verifier review's other variants; any Kaggle-side check.
- **Failed**: nothing. Both replays (100 s each) and the null ran once, in the foreground, without a restart.
