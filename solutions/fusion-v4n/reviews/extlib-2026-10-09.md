# Open MS/MS spectra (MassBank, GNPS2, MultiMS2) as extra library spectra (2026-10-09)

**The dataset adds nothing measurable to the pipeline as it stands.** It is licence-clean as far as its card and
file show, but 93% of its GNPS and MassBank rows are rows `train.parquet` already has, it holds 1,068 structures
train lacks (820 of them combinatorial amide products, 189 natural-product pool rows), and with the evaluation
molecules' own structures removed neither engine finds a closer analog in it. The test stopped after the model-free
step. No flag, no preset, no dataset folder, no notebook.

Offline except for one approved download (`sergiomalv/casmi-open-msms-spectra`, 202 MB zip, unpacked 217 MB, zip
removed by the CLI) and three read-only calls: `kaggle datasets files`, `kaggle datasets metadata` (the card),
`kaggle kernels list` (twice, "Not found"), plus one read of the Zenodo API record 17417089 (metadata only, no file).
Nothing was submitted, pushed, committed or uploaded; the visible `test.parquet` was not read; no model was trained;
no GPU. CPU: at most 6 threads of mine at below-normal priority. Scripts: `scratch/extlib/` (git-ignored). Outputs:
`D:\casmi-data\extlib\` (275 MB, new); download in `D:\casmi-data\input\datasets\sergiomalv\casmi-open-msms-spectra\`
(208 MB). Other caches were read, not written. **No file of the solution tree was changed except this review**
(`INDEX.md` not regenerated, `EXPERIMENTS.md` not edited, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` not edited because no preset uses
the data). Free space on D: 4.7 GB at the end (5.2 GB at the start).

**Every number below was computed in this study** unless a file is named. Keys: `casmi.chem.score_key` (the metric
key, RDKit 2026.03.3 asserted in every worker). **No leaderboard estimate is given.**

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Provenance.** Three sources, per-row licence column: MassBank Europe 2026.03 (58,948 spectra; CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA
   4.0, CC0, DL-DE BY 2.0), GNPS2 community libraries, non-propagated export of 2026-10-07 (344,179; CC0 as
   distributed by GNPS), MultiMS2 (18,544; CC0, Zenodo 17417089, ETH Zurich). No NC licence, no library with NIST in
   its name, no MoNA / HMDB / ReSpect mirror in the file. Usable subset after my own exclusions (ten small libraries
   of unclear origin, 7,683 rows): **413,988 spectra, 57,518 structures.** The build notebook is not public, so the
   card's filter is verified only by what is in the file (section 1).
2. **New against train: little.** 60,377 metric keys, 59,284 in train. **1,093 are not (1,068 in the usable
   subset)**: 820 from two GNPS "alkylamines" libraries (multiplexed-synthesis amides, 729 of them in no database of
   ours), 136 MassBank, 51 MultiMS2. **189 of the 1,068 are pool rows, all COCONUT**; 142 are PubChem-only; 737 are
   in neither. Spectra: 317,626 of 336,578 GNPS rows and 53,744 of 58,866 MassBank rows match a train row of the same
   structure and adduct in their five strongest peaks and peak count. **MultiMS2 is the only new measurement: 18,152
   ZenoTOF CID spectra (20 / 40 / 60 eV) of 2,470 structures train already has**, plus 330 spectra of 51 new ones.
3. **Leak.** 465 of the 630 evaluation structures are in the dataset (232 of 250 panel, 233 of 380 mixed); their
   10,877 rows were removed before anything was built.
4. **Model-free result, own structure removed everywhere.** Added the way a flag would add it (v1 engine: a new
   library slot; engine 2: rows appended to its library): the best analog similarity rises by 0.02 or more for 7 of
   250 (v1) and 0 of 250 (engine 2) panel molecules, 12 and 6 of 380 mixed; **the Tanimoto of the answer to the
   nearest of the first six analogs rises by 0.1 or more for 5 / 0 (panel) and 4 / 4 (mixed) molecules and falls by
   as much for 4 / 1 and 2 / 2.** Below `lib_max` 0.7: 3 up / 3 down (v1), 0 / 0 (engine 2) on the panel. No
   structure the dataset adds is nearer to a panel answer than its nearest train structure (0 of 250; 4 of 380
   mixed, none by 0.1).
5. **`lib_max` gate.** With the added rows of pool structures in the library kernel, **0 of 250 and 0 of 380
   molecules cross 0.7** in the answer-removed state; `lib_max` rises at all for 2 and 5 molecules. So the join and
   the fold-safe pass would see the same molecules.
6. **Steps 3b, 3c (engine runs) and 3d (replay) were not run**: 3a and the kernel part of 3c show nothing to carry.
   Run-time cost on Kaggle was therefore not measured.
7. **Same outcome as the Enveda-180 Zenodo study, for the opposite reason.** There the new structures were far from
   natural products; here the chemistry is right (GNPS natural-product libraries, MSnLib) but it is the same data
   train was built from.
8. **Side observation, not external data:** giving engine 2 a second set of representatives raises the best analog
   similarity by 0.02 or more for 25 of 250 panel molecules, and 23 of those 25 rows are rows train already has. That
   is engine 2's one-representative-per-structure rule, testable from `train.parquet` alone (section 4.3).

the same licence, attribution and a note of changes. Attaching the public dataset to a private notebook and reading
it at run time shares nothing, so nothing has to be published. A derived library file kept as a private Kaggle
dataset inside the team is likewise not shared with the public. If such a file were ever made public (for example
because the hosts require external artefacts to be public, or as a prize deliverable), it would have to be released
under CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution to MassBank (per record), GNPS, MultiMS2 and the dataset's author; the licence
does not reach our code or models by the text of the licence, but whether model weights trained on BY-SA data are
"adapted material" is unsettled, which matters only if the spectra were used for training, not as a look-up library.
DL-DE BY 2.0 (BAFG rows) needs the source note "(c) Bundesanstalt fuer Gewaesserkunde". Not legal advice. **No

## 3. Leak handling (task 3)

The evaluation molecules are train molecules and train's public libraries are in the dataset. **465 of the 630
evaluation metric keys occur in the usable subset (232 of the 250 panel structures, 233 of the 380 mixed-regime
structures): 10,877 rows (GNPS 6,646, MassBank 3,501, MultiMS2 730).** All of them were removed, for both sets at
once, before representatives were chosen, in every measurement below; train rows with the answer's key are removed
per molecule as in `scratch/zen180/s6_analog.py` (the `c2` / `dA` / `dB` state). 403,066 added rows remain (46
without a cleaned peak dropped too).

A leak the key rule cannot close, seen once: for three mixed-regime molecules (all stratum `s_nopc`) an added row of
a *different* structure with the same mass matches the query with similarity 1.0: the same spectrum under another
label. The three structures are not pool rows, so the v1 engine would not see them; engine 2 would, if rows were
appended to its library (new structures become candidates there). This is label conflict in the public libraries
(`docs/competition/rules-and-pitfalls.md`, item 8), and a reason not to trust a local engine-2 gain from appended
rows without checking for it.

## 4. Does it add signal (task 3a, and the kernel part of 3c)

### 4.1 Method

`s5_analog.py`, `s6_report.py` → `analog_{panel,mixed}/`, `s6_{panel,mixed}.parquet`, `s6_report.json`. The
engines' searches re-implemented with their own kernels and representative rules, as in the Enveda-180 study; not
runs of the engines.

- Queries: the 250 panel molecules (`D:\casmi-data\panel2\regime`, all 250; the brief's 226 were not singled out) and the 380 of the
  mixed regime; target mass = the answer's mass.
- Train: the v1 spectrum cache (2,539,608 spectra); v1 representatives 445,686 (structure, polarity, library),
  engine 2 representatives 275,810 (structure; timsTOF first, then most raw peaks).
- **v1, "new library"**: the added rows as three further libraries, one representative per structure, polarity and
  source (82,752); analog similarity = `casmi.spectra.search_shift`, merged query per polarity, ±200 Da.
- **Engine 2, "appended"**: what a flag that appends the rows to the library file would do: one representative per
  structure, so an added row replaces the train representative only if it has more raw peaks and train's is not
  timsTOF; new structures get one. **"Separate set"** (hypothetical, needs a code change): the added representatives
  (57,040) next to train's, best of both. Similarity = `pv.search_shift_pre` per query spectrum.
- **Library kernel**: `casmi.spectra.search` per query spectrum, same polarity, ±10 ppm, the maximum = `lib_max`;
  train rows of the answer's key removed; added rows counted when their structure is a pool row (only those can
  reach a candidate). Check against the harness: my train `lib_max` and the stored `c2_lib_max` of the mixed regime
  agree on the side of 0.7 for 369 of 380 molecules (r 0.92, mean absolute difference 0.031; the harness uses the
  measured target mass and its own batch purge).
- Structure: Morgan radius 2, 2,048 bits; "first six" = the six best analog structures (the v1 generator's parents).

## 5. Steps not run, and limits

- **3b (engine 2 with the rows appended), 3c beyond the kernel (v1 engine features and rankers), 3d (full replay),
  run time on Kaggle: not run.** The stop rule of the brief applies after 3a. No `ENG_EXTRA_LIB` flag was written;
  for the record, engine 2 reads its library in `casmi_engine.load_library(train_path)` and builds pool and index
  from it at run time (`nb/embed/eng/casmi_engine.py`), so appended rows in train's schema would enter there, and
  their structures would become candidates as well; the v1 engine needs rows in `train_structs.parquet` and
  `train_fp_sel.npy` for new structures (`reviews/enveda180z-2026-10-06.md`, section 5).
- **What the local sets can and cannot say.** They are library compounds whose public spectra are the dataset; after
  the leak rule the only measurable question is whether new analogs appear, and they do not. The hidden answers are
  "low-observation" natural products. For a hidden class-1 answer the dataset holds, train holds it too in 98% of
  cases (56,450 of 57,518 structures); a hidden answer among the 189 new COCONUT structures would become a library
  hit, which no local set can measure and which, at 189 structures, has a small prior. A hidden answer among the
  2,470 MultiMS2 structures is in train already; whether its ZenoTOF spectrum matches a timsTOF query better than
  train's rows was not measured (no local molecule is in that position after the leak rule).
- Not measured: `c1` state; the engines themselves; MultiMS2 or the 17,114 + 3,289 non-duplicate rows as *training*
  rows for the fingerprint networks (a different use, and those networks are public artefacts we do not retrain).
- Not verified: the dataset's build code; per-record MassBank licences; absence of NIST-derived annotations inside
  single GNPS records; the Zenodo source record 17250693 that MultiMS2 is derived from (not opened); the duplicate
  rule rests on five peaks and a peak count, not on a peak-by-peak comparison.
