## 0. Bottom line

1. **The hash-seed dependence is real, confirmed on Kaggle, and small.** The only hash-order tie-break that reaches the result is
   the merged-adduct label of the public v1 engine. Over 200 real hash seeds the full replay of v33b moves by a standard deviation
   of 0.0000 to 0.0009 per type (range at most 0.0034); v24a by 0.0000 to 0.0018 (range at most 0.0049). On a mixed 400-molecule
   draw the standard deviation is 0.0003 (v33b) and 0.0004 (v24a). **It does not explain ±0.004 between submissions.**
2. **The non-monotone 0.431 / 0.437 / 0.430 / 0.433 is the size of one or two lists on the public set**, which holds about 130
   molecules (`docs/competition/overview.md`: 33% of about 400): one answer moving between place 1 and 2 is 0.0038.
3. **No tie rule is better than another.** Upstream's rule (`train`), first occurrence, the logit average and the expected rank all
   lie within two standard errors of the hash-seed expectation on every type of both sets; `train` and `first` keep the exposure. `train` is wired
   as the deterministic choice because the same engine pass already uses it for the three `model_views` models.
4. **Wired default-off**: `ADDUCT_TIE` (`'train'` | `'first'` | `'mean'`) and `HASHSEED`; preset
   `configs/v36a-v33b-determ.json` = v33b + `ADDUCT_TIE='train'`, `HASHSEED=0`. Default-on and result-neutral: a log line with the
   kernel's hash facts, and a `FAILS` entry when ICEBERG or GLACIER stops at its time budget.
5. **Other sources**: forward-model scores of the same candidate differ between two Kaggle sessions when the batch neighbours differ
   (largest difference 0.007 / 0.009); injected as noise this moves a type by a standard deviation of 0.000 to 0.0024 (an upper
   bound for a rerun of the same config). Engine 2, the PubChem channel and the frag term were bit-identical in two Kaggle sessions.
   LightGBM threads: no effect. The budgets did not bind in the two diagnostic runs. One rare float-level effect was found in the
   library search (1 of 1,776 engine passes).

## 5. Fix and tie rule (task 3)

**(a) Determinism: what is and is not possible.** The kernel's string hashing is fixed when the kernel process starts.
`os.environ['PYTHONHASHSEED']` set in a cell changes nothing for the kernel itself and Kaggle offers no way to set it before the
start; it does reach every subprocess started later. Therefore:
- `HASHSEED` (int) is exported in `nb/01` before the first subprocess (engine 2, PubChem channel, ICEBERG, GLACIER). Their outputs
  were already seed-independent; this removes the question.
- The v1 engine runs inside the kernel, so its tie is broken by an explicit key instead: `nb/embed/determ.py` puts a module-level
  `max` into `casmi.engine` that answers exactly the call `max(set(adducts), key=adducts.count)` when the adducts tie and hands every
  other call to the builtin. The public package is not edited; `install` refuses a module that does not hold the expression once.
- Not removed: forward-model batch effects, row 13, rows 8 and 9.

**(b) Which rule.** Difference to the expectation over the 200 hash seeds, paired per molecule (wins / losses); exposure = lists with
a joined row first (`c1`, `c2` are the pool-answer types). `train` = upstream's `merged_adduct`; `first` = first in the molecule's
spectra; `mean` = merged-item logits averaged over the tied adducts (one pass); `erank` = rank average of the lists of all choices
(one pass per choice).

## 7. What this means for reading the leaderboard (task 5)

- **The pipeline's own run-to-run variation is far below the differences being read.** Hash seed: standard deviation 0.0003 to 0.0005
  on a mixed draw, at most 0.002 on one type; forward-model batches: at most 0.0024 on one type as an upper bound. Two runs of one
  config are expected to differ by 0 to 2 first places.
- **The grain of the public set is the larger effect.** About 130 molecules: one answer between place 1 and 2 is 0.0038, between
  2 and 3 is 0.0013. The four forward-weight submissions differ by one or two such moves. A change that really moves two lists in
  opposite directions reads as zero.
- **How large must a difference be.** Below about 0.004 (one list) a difference says nothing. 0.004 to 0.008 is one or two lists and
  is evidence only together with a paired local difference of the same sign beyond two standard errors. Around 0.01 and more (three
  lists or more) is beyond anything the notebook does by itself. This is the grain of the public set, not a measured leaderboard
  error; the private set (about 270 molecules) has a finer grain.
- **Submitting the same config twice: not recommended.** The expected outcome is the same score or one list apart (at most about
  0.004). It would be informative only if the two differed by 0.008 or more, which nothing measured here makes likely. One such pair
  already exists: our default config scored 0.399, equal to the public reference notebook (`docs/experiments.md`, row 4).
- v36a is a hygiene change with an expected effect of zero; it is not a candidate for a submission of its own. Carry the two keys
  in the next config that is submitted for another reason.
