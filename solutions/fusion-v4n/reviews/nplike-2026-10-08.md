# A natural-product-likeness prior, independent of fame (2026-10-08)

**First line: nothing survives.** A structure-only NP-likeness signal carries no information among pool rows on any
stratum, carries a real but weak one among joined PubChem rows (AUC 0.66 to 0.78 against 0.97 for the ranker's own
score), and in the full replay every cell of the pre-stated grid fails the rule: the joined-row term loses `dB` on the
mixed regime's natural products at every weight, the pool-row term loses `c2` on the Enveda panel, and as a replacement
for a reduced popularity prior it recovers 0.0006 to 0.0024 of the 0.004 to 0.015 the reduction loses. **No preset, no dataset
folder, no notebook build.**

Reading.
- **Among pool rows the signal is absent**, most clearly where it was wanted: the least famous tercile of the mixed
  natural products, where the substance count fails too (+0.020 ± 0.022), has `npscore` −0.011 ± 0.026 and the own
  models −0.027 to +0.015. The pool is train plus COCONUT; the isomers of a natural product in it are natural products.
  `npscore` is +0.044 ± 0.017 on the panel as a whole; nothing else clears two standard errors.
- **Among joined rows the signal is real**: every NP-likeness form is positive on every natural-product stratum,
  `own_lr_c` +0.220 ± 0.054 on the least famous mixed tercile, and about zero on non-natural products, which is the
  right pattern. It is small next to what the list already has: AUC 0.66 to 0.78 against 0.97 to 0.98 for the base score.
- **The leak is small in this measure**: closed against leaky differs by at most 0.039 (logistic, mixed t1: 0.220
  against 0.259) and by under 0.01 on the panel. A linear model on 4,096 hashed bits does not memorise answers.
- **What the leak-closing does not close.** In the local `dA` / `dB` lists the answer is a COCONUT structure placed
  among real PubChem-only rows, i.e. a member of the positive class among members of the negative class. The model was
  never shown that structure or its scaffold, but it was fitted to exactly this contrast. A hidden answer that reaches
  the list as a joined row is by construction **not** in our COCONUT. The only local lists of that kind are natural
  products without a COCONUT record: 2 panel and 4 mixed molecules, 4 `dA` lists. No evidence.
- The descriptors are at chance (AUC 0.45 to 0.61); the sugar flag fires too rarely to matter; "more ring systems" is
  slightly against the answer.
- `pop` on joined rows (+0.54 to +0.83) is the artefact the fame reviews describe: a library compound among rows with
  a median of 2 records. It says nothing about a hidden joined answer.

## 3. The flag

`fusion_core.np_reorder` generalises `popularity_reorder`: pool rows `f = z + POP_MU pop + NP_MU s`, re-sorted among
the pool slots; joined rows (pool id −2) `f = z + NP_JOIN_MU s`, re-sorted among the joined rows' own slots; generator
products keep their slots; tags as before. `s` comes from `build_submission(..., np_score=dict or callable)`. With both
weights 0, or no scorer, the old code path runs untouched. `tests/test_np_like.py`: off equals the reference bit for
bit (400 random lists × 3 list shapes × 3 `POP_MU`), zero weights equal `popularity_reorder`, each term stays inside
its own slots, a failing or partial scorer falls back. Run here together with `test_config_keys`,
`test_fusion_equivalence`, `test_popularity`, `test_pop_group`, `test_join_fwd_a`, `test_fwd_a_scale`, `test_frag_gap`:
all pass. In the replay, the reference with the edited `fusion_core.py` equals the poplow study's stored v33b rows
(largest reciprocal-rank difference 0 on 904 and 1,180 lists; the same for `POP_MU` 0.15 and 0.05).
The notebook has no scorer: `nb/08_submit.py` asserts both weights are 0.

## 5. What local evidence can and cannot say here

- Local replays had the leaderboard's sign in 5 to 7 of 11 contrasts (`reviews/uregime-2026-10-07.md`,
  `reviews/fame-2026-10-08.md`), and had the wrong sign for the last two popularity submissions (v44a, v46c;
  `reviews/poplow-2026-10-08.md`). A local loss of 0.001 to 0.003 is therefore not a prediction of a leaderboard loss,
  and a local gain would not have been one of a gain.
- The local answers are library compounds that sit in COCONUT: 224 of 226 panel answers and 257 of 380 mixed answers
  (256 of the 260 natural products) have a COCONUT record. For pool answers this makes the test fair in one respect
  (their pool isomers are mostly COCONUT rows too) and the result is "no signal". For joined answers it makes the
  test favourable to the idea (the answer is drawn from the model's positive class, its decoys from the negative
  class), and even so the replay loses.
- The part that resembles the hidden set is the leak-closed evaluation on the obscure strata: mixed natural products,
  least famous tercile (13 records at the median). There: pool rows, nothing (−0.027 to +0.015, 74 lists); joined
  rows, +0.09 to +0.22 in-group (38 lists) and −0.002 to −0.013 composite in the replay. The stratum that would test
  the idea directly, natural-product answers outside COCONUT reaching the list as joined rows, has 4 lists.
- What this cannot rule out: that hidden joined answers are separated from PubChem decoys by NP-likeness better than
  the local library compounds are. Nothing local measures it, and nothing local supports it.
