# Coverage: where the missing 45% are, and what a wider or better-used source can recover (2026-10-10)

Offline analysis plus a small live measurement. Nothing was submitted, pushed, committed or uploaded; no dataset was
downloaded; no solution code was changed; no GPU; `scratch/joint1/` was not touched; FINAL was not used (DEV only). The
visible `test.parquet` was opened for one column (`adduct`), as the task allows. CPU: one process of mine at below-normal
priority. Network: **236 PUG REST requests answered** (1 test, 134 connectivity look-ups, 40 tautomer look-ups, 1
property batch, 30 × 2 formula requests; 3 more attempts failed at DNS and never left the machine; budget 300), 12
read-only Kaggle CLI calls (6 `datasets list -s`, 5 `datasets metadata`, 1 `datasets files`). Only counts, keys and
CIDs were stored. Scripts: `scratch/coverage2/` (`pug.py`, `c1_local.py` … `c6_mix.py`; git-ignored). Outputs:
`D:\casmi-data\coverage2\` (9.3 MB). `INDEX.md` not regenerated, `EXPERIMENTS.md` not edited.

Marks: **[M]** measured here; **[C]** cited from a named review or file; **[A]** assumption; **[F]** formula with its
assumption named. The hidden mix is unknown; the public part is about 124 molecules (one answer = 0.008). No
leaderboard estimate is given.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Our candidate sources are complete against the public databases.** The PubChem tier is not a subset by
   annotation, depositor or natural-product filter: it is all of PubChem inside its filter (one covalent unit, net
   charge zero, C H N O P S F Cl Br I, 150 to 1,250 Da, stereo removed). Of 4,946 eligible CIDs drawn from PubChem for
   30 training formulas, **4,944 (99.96%) are tier rows** [M]. The pool's COCONUT part is the October 2026 release
   minus 21 structures [C `extra-db`]. A wider source has nothing to add.
2. **Train's Enveda-origin structures are almost all in PubChem** [M]: `enveda-np-examples` 250 of 250 (249 COCONUT
   rows; 0 nowhere), `enveda-180` 97.3% (the 2.7% without a tier row are in PubChem neither by connectivity, 0 of 40,
   nor by tautomer, 0 of 10). DEV: 15.8% natural class N, and those are **nowhere**: 0 of 71 sampled are in PubChem, 0
   in six natural-product catalogues. "In PubChem but outside our tier" is empty in the hidden mass range (0 of 77
   asked; the only out-of-tier PubChem records are below 150 Da or above 1,250 Da).
3. **Which explanation of the missing 45% the evidence supports.** (ii) "in PubChem, not in our tier": **rejected**
   (points 1, 2). (iii) "in the tier but lost by window, shortlist, cut, gate or fusion": **bounded at roughly 1 to 9
   of the 45 points**; the window loses nothing on timsTOF, and to carry the gap alone it needs a hidden class C whose
   reach into our 25 rows is 0.04 to 0.39 against 0.61 to 0.73 locally, while local reach does not fall for answers
   without library relatives. (i) "in no database": **the residual, about 30 to 43 points**, consistent with the data
   page's class 3 ("not in PubChem") and with the leader's 0.76. It is an inference: no local set has such a share
   (0%, 2.7%, 15.8%) and no local measurement can separate "nowhere" from "a tier row our selector never finds".
4. **Funnel of the PubChem channel** (1,215 DEV record answers) [M, reproduces `budget`]: 8.5% lost by the shortlist,
   7.3% beyond selection rank 200, 4.4% at 101 to 200, **79.8% in the first 100**; gate closed for 27.8%. After the
   join: 92.8% of joined answers reach the ranker's first 25, 46% its first place. In the fused chain 6 to 8% of the
   class-C answers that are candidates fall outside the final 25; the engine-2 fusion removes 2 and 5 answers from
   the 25 but **22 of 95 and 41 of 82 first places**.
5. **Plan** (section 5): the only block of size is "nowhere", and only generation addresses it; the two
   database-side changes (full-window selection at N ≤ 100, candidate-level protection instead of the gate) are worth
   about 1 to 5% and 0.4 to 1.5% of the 45 points. **No download is needed for any of them.** Not worth doing: a
   larger tier, more catalogues, a wider join.

## 4. Options to recover coverage

**(a) A larger PubChem tier: no gain** [M]. The tier is PubChem inside its filter. What lies outside: net-charged and
multi-component records, other elements, below 150 Da, above 1,250 Da; none of it fits the hidden test's range and
adducts. Cost figures for the existing tier, at 2.55 ms per fully fingerprinted candidate (figure of the task): the
second pass now scores min(window, 5,000) rows, mean 3,448 (DEV) to 4,386 (timsTOF) per molecule, i.e. 59 to 75 min
of one process or 15 to 19 min on four workers for 400 molecules; without the shortlist 4,098 (panel) to 6,632
(timsTOF) more rows per molecule, **+70 to +113 min of one process, +17 to +28 min on four** [F]. Disk: the tier is a
mounted input and does not count against the 20 GB working disk. Public Kaggle datasets with PubChem structures
(`kaggle datasets list -s pubchem`, three spellings; nothing downloaded):

| ref | size | licence field | content |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `ahmedberatozer/casmi26-pubchem-tier` | 1.20 GB listed | other | the tier we use |
| `dmitriigluzdov/casmi26-attributed-v4g-reproduction-assets` | 3.58 GB | not read | mirror of pool and tier |
| `dmitriigluzdov/casmi26-pubchem-popularity-prior` | 1.29 GB | not read | counts and keys per tier row (used) |
| `m0delcrafter/pubchem-50m` | 21.6 GB | DbCL-1.0 | PubChem SDF blocks of 500,000 CIDs, "50 M compounds": fewer than the tier |
| `sabetm/smiles-pubchem` | 321 MB | unknown | no description |
| `yuanyuan98/casmi26-pubchem-part` | 213 MB | other | "PubChem part of the pool, n_pmid ≥ 1": a subset |
| `ngdminh31/casmi26-pubchem-tier2-fp-public` | 6.9 GB | CC0 | 7.23 M rows of 56 depositors: a subset [C `availability`] |

None holds more than our tier.

**(b) Other natural-product databases: no gain** [C `extra-db-2026-10-04.md`, + M]. Six catalogues were downloaded and
counted on 10-04: 455,794 eligible structures in their union, 3,146 not pool rows, **826 in none of our sources**;
they add at most 0.9 points over COCONUT on any training library. Here: 0 of the 75 "nowhere" answers is in any of
them. Licences as recorded there: COCONUT CC0, LOTUS CC0 (Zenodo record CC BY 4.0), NPAtlas CC BY-NC 4.0 (the task
names CC BY; not re-read today), NP-MRD CC BY-NC 4.0, NPASS and CMAUP without a licence text. Not reachable then:
Super Natural 3 (server silent; its COCONUT import of 83,037 eligible structures is in the pool), UNPD (no primary
source; 28,668 through COCONUT). No web-API request was spent on these: the local catalogue files answer the question
more completely than a sample would.

**(c) A wider join with the current selector** [M + C]. Recall among record answers: R@100 0.798, R@200 0.842 (+53
answers); by raw order on other instruments 0.81 / 0.87 / 0.90 / 0.94 at 200 / 500 / 1,000 / 5,000; on timsTOF nothing
lies between rank 200 and the shortlist. In molecules: N 200 adds 4.4% × C share 0.05 to 0.22 = 0.2 to 1.0 points of
candidates, placed at about 0.33 [C `budget`]: at most +0.003 [F]; the leaderboard paid 0.025 for it. N 500 would add
about 6 more points of record answers on other instruments and none on timsTOF. **What the joint model must deliver**
is therefore unchanged from `budget` 4.1 (R@25 ≥ 0.75 and R@100 ≥ 0.88 at the exposure of today's 100 rows) with one
addition from this study: it has to score the **whole window**, because on timsTOF the shortlist caps R at 0.844.

**(d) The gate** [M]. It closes the join for 27.8% of record answers (284 of the 970 selected). Per-candidate
protection instead would lift gate-closed lists from 48% / 68% in the final 25 toward 65% / 76%: 4.8 / 2.7 points of
class C, times C share = **0.1 to 1.1 points of molecules**; its larger effect is rank (0.06 to 0.08 MRR per class-C
answer [C `budget`]). Code only.

**(e) Generation for answers that are nowhere** [C `genjoin`, F]. Block: N-like 0.29 to 0.52. Reach of the existing
analog generator into its first 30 products with relatives above Tanimoto 0.7 purged: 4.4% (record-less timsTOF) and
13% (record-less natural-product-library structures); 38% on the panel, whose answers are common compounds. Placement
of a generated answer 0.36 to 0.67. So 0.29 to 0.52 × 0.044 to 0.13 = **1.3 to 6.8 points of molecules listed**, about
+0.005 to +0.045 MRR before costs [C `budget` 3.5]; the cost that sank `genjoin` was −0.010 to −0.014 on
PubChem-supplied answers. Reach is under 10% when no library relative above 0.6 exists.

## 6. Not verified

- The hidden mix. "Nowhere 30 to 43 points" is a residual under local class profiles; a tier row that our selector
  never lists is indistinguishable from it in every score we have.
- That local class-C reach transfers. Every local answer is a library compound; recall is flat across the strata
  measured (library neighbours, instrument, catalogue membership, window size apart), which is evidence, not proof.
- The C-share cap (0.05 to 0.22) rests on the join gain (one submission pair, noise about ±0.02), a foreign probe from
  the old scoring regime and local gains per answer.
- Tier completeness was sampled on 30 formulas of training structures (5,164 CIDs), not on PubChem as a whole; rows
  were matched by InChIKey first block. Tier snapshot date: not stated on the card (`extra-db`: recent).
- "Not in PubChem" = no hit of `fastidentity` at connectivity level (134 structures) and at tautomer level (40 of the
  misses); one request returned a server error. Tier membership by key undercounts tautomer forms (3 such cases found).
- Pool and tier build notebooks were not located; the cards are one sentence each. Licence fields of two mirror
  datasets were not read. Kaggle's dataset size limit was not looked up (the 7.2 GB tier is mounted today).
- NPAtlas's licence (CC BY-NC 4.0 in `extra-db`, CC BY in the task) was not re-read; Super Natural 3 and UNPD were not
  reached on 10-04 and not tried again; no catalogue API was queried.
- The 2.55 ms per candidate is the task's figure, not re-timed; the generator's Kaggle run time was not re-timed.
- Water-loss adducts: 33 Enveda spectra in train. Adduct mislabels of the hidden test cannot be measured.
- `enveda-180` is synthetic; the only Enveda natural-product set in train is the 250-molecule panel of common compounds.
- The fused-chain figures are v33b on 606 local molecules (`budget`, section 6 caveats apply). Nothing was replayed,
  implemented or submitted here.
