## 0. Bottom line

1. **There is no second public pipeline to ensemble with.** Every public notebook at 0.40 or more runs the same components (ahmedberatozer's
   v4n engine with `fpnet_full1` and the v4b ranker, prvsiyan / megayak engine 2, the PubChem tier channel, ICEBERG / GLACIER); they differ in
   ordering rules [V code, C landscape files]. Locally the v80 / obstacledeveloper line (guhongbin's base) has the same rank-1 row as stack1 in
   97-98% of lists. The two notebooks named "ensemble" (kingdamon 0.436, haideptry 0.433) are the v80 / e006 code [V pulled today].
2. **The only locally runnable pipeline that is not stack1's code is ours** (fusion-v4n v59a design: PubChem join, fitted ranker, fragment
   term). It agrees with stack1 on rank 1 in 62% (mixed) / 72% (panel) of lists. Its oracle with stack1 is large (+0.096 masked, +0.057 real;
   pool-answer lists alone +0.036), so the two lists are complementary, but no rule that uses it passes.
3. **All five pre-registered rules fail** (section 4). The two rank fusions are the closest: RRF w 0.3 +0.0129 / +0.0060 (masked / real),
   pool-answer lists +0.0034 [+0.0013, +0.0059]; RRF w 1.0 +0.0426 / +0.0105, pool +0.0108 [+0.0055, +0.0166], but in the real arm 37 rank-1
   answers lost and a worst case of 2.8 public answers. Both are below the size bar (+0.0133 in the weaker arm), and most of their gain is on
   dA / dB, where answers were removed from the pool on purpose (non-transferable, `finalrank-2026-10-17.md`).
4. **Why it would not behave differently from v65c / v67**: it is the same partner. v65c (v59a + e006, RRF k 3, w 0.3) predicted level /
   +0.008 locally and scored 0.430, below both of its inputs (v59a 0.434, e006r 0.443) [C `docs/experiments.md` 48, 51]. The harness
   overrates our pipeline against stack1's: v59a alone minus stack1 is +0.065 (masked) / −0.010 (real) locally against −0.017 on the
   leaderboard [V, C]. Nothing in this study changes those two facts.
5. **Recommendation: no ensemble submission.** A coverage change (PubChemLite in engine 2, `update-2026-10-17.md` 7) and the reproducible
   chain remain the open items; neither is an ensemble.

## 5. Why an ensemble would not behave differently this time

- **Same partner, same mechanism.** The only different pipeline is v59a, already fused with this lineage (v65c: w 0.3, k 3, i.e. R1 with the
  roles swapped) and fed into it (v67a / b / d / e). The fused list scored below both inputs; feeding our base into e006 cost −0.032 to
  −0.045 [C `docs/experiments.md` 48, 52-55; `diag67-2026-10-12.md`: real chain loss, not a fallback].
- **The harness's bias points the same way as the rules.** Locally v59a beats stack1 by +0.065 (masked) and loses by 0.010 (real); on the
  leaderboard it is 0.017 below. Every rule here moves weight toward v59a, so the harness rewards it by construction, and the largest gains are
  on dA / dB, where pool-removed answers make other sources' rows artificially right (coordinator's warning, `finalrank-2026-10-17.md`).
- **No public second lineage exists** that could bring independent errors: A2 / A3 agree with stack1 on rank 1 in 97-98% of lists; D and E
  are not usable or were killed; F is 0.10 below.
- What would make an ensemble behave differently is a **new candidate source** (coverage), not a vote over the same candidates: e.g.
  PubChemLite in engine 2 (A2's only positive single change, +0.006 on another base), or class-3 generation.
