Marks: **[V]** read in code or computed here (file named); **[C]** cited from a named review; **[I]** inference. MRR = MRR@25 on the

## 0. Bottom line

- **The leaderboard gains of the last two weeks were all in small cells.** The N1 challenger (+0.008) acts only on molecules that
  have a [M+NH4]+ / water-loss spectrum and a [M+Na]+ spectrum but no [M+H]+. That is 1.2% of the Enveda NP panel, about 1.5 public
  molecules, so the gain is one answer. The E006 ICEBERG fallback (+0.004) acts on [M+Na]+-only molecules, also about 1%.
  PubChemLite (+0.004 to +0.006) adds a second source for some PubChem-only answers. Each of these gains is roughly one public
  answer from a cell of 1 to 10% of the molecules [V section 2, I].
- **The three largest cells where a new source could act** (hidden-like shares, section 4):
  1. **Answer in no database** (class N): **29-50%** of molecules [C coverage2]. No scorer helps here. Only a generator can act.
     The allowed source is a chemical language model pretrained on PubChem. Our generators passed locally but scored 0.000 on the
     leaderboard.
  2. **PubChem-only answer with a single source** (only the PubChem channel lists it): about **0-14%**. Another **0-8%** are listed
     by no source at all. With one source the local MRR is 0.17 (popularity masked) / 0.44 (real popularity). With a second
     source it is 0.43 / 0.48 (dB) and 0.63 / 0.74 (dA). The allowed source is engine 2 run over the PubChem channel's rows. This is
     the PubChemLite mechanism, applied to each molecule's own PubChem window.
  3. **Database answer without simulator evidence** (negative-only or [M+Na]+-only, MetFrag-lite at most): **4-10%**, 5-12 public
     molecules. The local MRR gap to GLACIER-scored molecules is −0.15 (c2) and −0.22 / −0.24 (dA). FIORA-OS is allowed but was at
     chance on the panel's gap molecules (2026-10-07). MS2DeepScore 2.0, used for analog search across ion modes, is untested. The
     ceiling is 0.25-0.45 public answers [C adductfwd].
- **Already acted on:** proxy-only molecules (3-5%, N1 in s1). **Too small to matter:** a PubChem-only answer behind a closed
  library gate (≤ 1-2%). No source is missing for the 10 test adducts: engine 1, engine 2 and the PubChem channel produce a list for
  every molecule [V].
- Caveat: the local MRRs come from the **stack1-like** replay (stack1err). That chain has no N1 proxies, no PubChemLite and no
  candidate-aware promotion. s1's own replay (s1val) is still unread.

## 5. The three largest cells: allowed source and what we already know

**E1. Answer in no database (29-50%)**
- Allowed source: a chemical language model pretrained on PubChem (751373). It would generate formula-constrained structures
  conditioned on the fold-safe FPNet fingerprint. MSNovelist's own input is CSI:FingerID, which is banned, so FPNet replaces it.
  DiffMS / DualLGD are also allowed with their training code (750252), but the DiffMS MIST encoder has a known bug (#27).
- Our tests:
  - `fpgen-2026-10-12`: our decoder trained from scratch failed its gate. Top-25 was 10.6% on DEV class N against 53% for the
    rule-based generator, and 0% on set F. With the true fingerprint it reached 32.7%, so the bottleneck is the predicted
    fingerprint. A PubChem-pretrained model improves the chemistry prior, not that bottleneck [I].
  - `gen3-2026-10-10`: the analog generator passed locally (A6: class N +0.037 ± 0.004 per molecule) but scored 0.000 on the
    leaderboard (`docs/experiments.md` rows 46-47, 79-80, 88).
  - Public pairs: Data Behind v17 (more generation) −0.008. Tra Crew report +0.003 to +0.004 from class 3. Forum 751300: local
    class-3 gains do not reach the leaderboard.
- Verdict: the largest cell, with the weakest evidence that gains transfer. If tried at all, only as tail rows (rank ≥ 11), where
  pool answers cannot lose a first place.

**E2 / E3. PubChem-only answer with one source or none (together about 0-22%; plausibly 5-12%)**
- Today such an answer has only FPNet A+B+fold0 f·z, the priors, and an ICEBERG / GLACIER re-order inside the channel's 25.
- Allowed source: no new weights are needed. Engine 2 (already in s1) would score the channel's top rows of each molecule.
  - The PubChem structures are allowed (751373).
  - Engine 2 fingerprints training structures at run time (`fp_and_mass`), so 25-100 rows × 400 molecules is cheap.
  - This is the PubChemLite mechanism (+0.006, +0.004, +0.004, +0.005; −0.003 once) on each molecule's own window instead of a
    fixed list of 386k structures.
  - Section 3.2 is the local case for it: a second source lifts dB from 0.17 to 0.43 (m) and from 0.44 to 0.48 (u). This is
    correlational: answers that two sources list may be easier ones.
- Earlier tests:
  - PubChem rows joined into **engine 1**: +0.011 on the old base (v12a, v13a); 200 rows −0.025; grafted onto e006 −0.032 (v67a).
    Adding rows to an engine can push pool answers out of the list, so engine 2's fusion weight 0.6 has to absorb this.
  - New scorers inside the channel did not help: MIST no lift (`mist-2026-10-04`), other FP networks −0.005 to −0.012.
  - More priors score 0.000 (LOTUS, NP, synonyms; patent counts not recommended in update-2026-10-24b).
- Caveat: engine 2's fp models and ranker features have doubtful prize eligibility (745072), the same as s1 today.

**E4. Database answer without a simulator (4-10%, 5-12 public molecules)**
- Allowed sources:
  - FIORA-OS (751373; MIT, MSnLib v7). We ran it locally on 2026-10-07 (`fiora-2026-10-07`):
    - enveda-180 [M−H]- hold fold 0.811 against chance 0.215;
    - unseen negative-mode NP panel molecules 0.160 against 0.053;
    - the 19 panel gap molecules 0.069 against chance 0.061, i.e. at chance;
    - all four gap-term forms fail the replay (best +0.0005 on the panel, losses on c1 / dB in the mixed set).
  - MS2DeepScore 2.0: an analog search from negative-only queries into the mostly positive library. Untested. Engine 2's analog
    search uses plain cosine across polarities. DreaMS as an analog kernel was worse than the v1 kernel (`dreamsim-2026-10-07`).
  - GLACIER retrained on negative adducts with ms-pred: compliant, but days of work and 5-80 GPU-h. Our earlier fine-tunes lost on
    the leaderboard (−0.030, −0.023), and the timsTOF fine-tune was null (`glacierft-2026-10-18`, +0.008 isomer MRR, CI spans 0).
- Other tests on the forward side all failed: the adduct-shift surrogate (`adductfwd-2026-10-17`), CE calibration and spectrum
  cleaning (`tims-2026-10-17`), and the isomer cross-encoder (`isoxe-2026-10-18`, −0.081 against GLACIER).
- Reading:
  - Locally, every forward term for this cell has been null. On the leaderboard, the comparable N1 cell gave one answer from about
    1.5 molecules.
  - A FIORA-OS fallback for segment D is the "winning kind" of change (new evidence where there is none). Expect at most one answer,
    and only if FIORA beats chance on hidden NP spectra, which our panel test did not show.

## 6. Limits

- All MRRs come from the stack1-like chain (no N1, PubChemLite or candidate-aware promotion). For pool answers c1 / c2 this
  matters little. It understates the two-source share of dB in s1.
- Class shares are leaderboard-fit ranges [C coverage2], not measurements. Combining them with segment shares assumes independence.
- Panel cells B to D hold only 2-18 molecules each, so their MRRs carry SEs of about 0.05-0.1.
