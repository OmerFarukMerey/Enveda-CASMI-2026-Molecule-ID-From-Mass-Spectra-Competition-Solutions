## 0. Bottom line

1. **Learning curve: still rising, slowly.** Held-out MRR of the fitted ranker on joined class-2 lists below the gate
   with 10 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 100% of the training molecules (535 to 5,350 lists per fit): 0.796 / 0.829 / 0.838 /
   0.844 / 0.847. The last doubling is worth +0.009 ± 0.003 for the ranker alone and +0.005 ± 0.002 inside the rank
   average of v33b; the fusion passes on about a third of a base-list difference. Joined lists are indispensable
   (without them −0.175 ± 0.010 and 301 instead of 69 exposed lists) but saturated for pool answers: half of them
   give the same result (−0.003 ± 0.004). More data is a small lever.
2. **The premise that the hold-out fold caps the data is wrong, measured:** fold `hold` of the fold-safe FPNet has
   53,422 eligible molecules, of which the studies have used 3,100. **50,322 unseen molecules need no FPNet
   training** (3,349 natural-product catalogue, 1,352 natural-product libraries without a PubChem record, 2,815
   nitrogen-free); the limit is engine time on the CPU. Cross-fitting would add 19,796 more catalogue molecules at
   about 25 GPU hours.
3. **No refit of the ranker beats the production recipe out of fold.** Eleven variants (truncation, regularisation,
   `rank_xendcg`, all 267 inputs, without fingerprint inputs, same-formula specialist, joined lists only, below-gate
   lists only, without the panel, fewer joined or plain lists): nothing is above the reseeding noise of one fit
   (0.003 on 823 lists). Averaging five seeds instead of one gives +0.004 ± 0.002; the production files already hold
   four. **No new ranker was exported.**
4. **Where the ranker loses: isomer order, nothing else.** With a perfect order inside the answer's formula group the
   MRR would be 0.99 (from 0.87 / 0.85); with a perfect choice of formula +0.006. Nitrogen-free molecules are 28% of
   the lists and 58 to 66% of the loss; there the fingerprint alone puts the answer first among its isomers in 20%
   of the lists (10% with joined rows), the analog prior in 30%.
5. **Weight, gate and fade-in of the average: v33b's setting stays.** Gates above 0.7 and no gate lose `dA` / `dB` on
   the panel beyond three standard errors (gate 0.9: −0.059 ± 0.012 and −0.036 ± 0.009); weight 1.0 (v33a) gains
   `dB` on the panel (+0.016 ± 0.007) and loses it on the regime (−0.019 ± 0.007); the fade-ins lose `dA`.
6. **One lever passes, on both sets and all four types: no forward-model re-rank of the base list in stage A**
   (`FWD_A_SCALE` [0, 0]; the PubChem list and stage C keep weights 0.75). Against v33b: panel `c1` +0.012 ± 0.006
   (11 / 1), `c2` +0.021 ± 0.008 (20 / 3), `dA` +0.010 ± 0.007 (31 / 10), `dB` +0.019 ± 0.008 (37 / 14); regime
   +0.001 ± 0.002, +0.013 ± 0.005 (26 / 11), +0.015 ± 0.005 (31 / 12), +0.017 ± 0.006 (34 / 14). Exposure 4 / 2
   against 4 / 3 (panel) and 20 / 2 against 23 / 5 (regime). Sign-flip null over the 24 chains tried: family-wise
   p 0.002 and < 0.001. The dose is monotone (factor 2/3, 1/3, 0). It came out of the fitted stage-A study, which
   chose forward weights a third of the hand-set ones in every fold; the fitted models themselves add nothing.
7. **Recommendation:** v34a (`configs/v34a-v33b-fwda0.json`) first; v34b (factor 1/3) only as the reading aid
   described in section 8.

## 1. Data, folds, what "out of fold" means here

`r3_data.py`: one table of every fold-safe list of the earlier studies (arm (c) of `reviews/rankbank-2026-10-06.md`:
fold-safe FPNet in the engine bank and the three `model_views` slots): the 12 sets of the production ranker (6,687
lists) + 145 joined class-1 lists of the trainplan molecules; 6,832 lists, 1,011,656 rows, 267 columns, 3,350
molecules (3,100 hold-out + 250 panel). Five folds grouped by generic Murcko scaffold over all molecules; every list
of a molecule is in one fold. **An out-of-fold score comes from boosters that have seen none of the molecule's lists**
and 4/5 of the other molecules, panel and regime included; that is one step closer to the production ranker than
the split model of the fsranker review (fitted without regime and panel). The public ranker in the average is the
public ranker on the same fold-safe features in the notebook's order (class-2 emulation on `c2` lists): its fit has
seen the molecules, its inputs have not.

List groups (lists whose answer is a candidate; "below" = `lib_max` < 0.7): `c2J` = joined class-2 lists below the
gate (823: trainplan 405, regime 254, panel 164), the production case for a pool answer; `dropJ` = joined
answer-removed lists below the gate (576; the answer is a joined or a generated row); `c1b` = class-1 lists below
the gate (175); `c2N` = class-2 lists without the join below the gate (2,301). `_nf` = nitrogen-free.

  public FPNet has seen these structures, so the size of this loss is inflated here, but nothing on the positive
  side offsets it (`c2` +0.001 to +0.006). **Closed.** The fade-ins move `c1` by at most +0.005 and cost `dA` 0.006
  to 0.040.
- **Stage-A forward weights.** Every type gains or is level in both sets, with the pure public chain as well as
  with v33b, the wins-to-losses ratio is 2 to 1 or better in every cell, the dose is monotone, exposure does not
  rise (regime 23 → 20). By library gate (× 0 against v33b): regime below 0.7 `c1` +0.018 ± 0.012, `c2`

## 8. Recommendation (task 5)

   The only variant of this study that gains on all four types in both sets, with a mechanism (the same evidence
   applied twice to the same rows; the first application is worth nothing on the v1 side) and against the
   direction the local leak pushes.
   - Above 0.442 by more than two answers (≥ 0.450): the base list should reach the fusion as the rankers ordered
     it; keep it, and the next step on this axis is the fused list (stage C), where the forward terms still act
     on joined and generated rows.
   - Within ±0.004: not readable. The lever is then neither confirmed nor refuted; do not spend the second
     submission on it.
   - Below 0.438: as with the global line search, the local gain on pool answers from weaker forward terms does not
     exist on the hidden test; the reason would be that the hidden molecules' forward scores carry information at
     the base-list stage that the local, seen molecules get from the ranker already.
2. **Second submission, only after the first is read.** If v34a is clearly up: nothing further here; the other
   open candidate of the team (`v36a`, deterministic adduct ties) is independent and can be stacked. If v34a is
   clearly down: **v34b** (factor 1/3) is *not* a rescue, it is the same lever at two thirds of the dose
   (panel +0.011 / +0.018 / +0.005 / +0.015, regime 0.000 / +0.014 / +0.011 / +0.012); it would only tell whether
   the response is monotone, and one submission cannot tell that. In that case do not submit it.

## 9. Honesty and limits

- **Who has seen what.** Fitted ranker: nothing of the test molecule (out of fold); 4/5 of the other panel / regime
  molecules, which makes the reference chain slightly better informed than a ranker facing new chemistry. Public
  ranker, public FPNets (`pub` and the public half of every average), engine 2, ICEBERG, GLACIER: everything.
  `fragnet`: 9.4% of the hold-out answers, none of the panel's.
- **One seed** in the replay and in every variant; the production ranker has four. The reseeding noise (0.003 on
  `c2J`) is not in the standard errors. The replay's chains share one fit, so their differences are free of it.
- **The reference is an emulation of v33b**, not the submitted files (those have seen every local molecule).
- **`FWD_A_SCALE` was found in this study and tested on the same two sets** that suggested it (the stage-A study
  ran on the panel and the regime). The null covers the chains tried, not that choice; the panel and the regime
  agree, and the public chain shows the same, but there is no third set.
- **Panel `dA` / `dB` are not answer-outside lists in two of five cases** (the generator re-creates the answer,
  `reviews/panel2-2026-10-05.md`).
- **CPU-scored GLACIER values** (847 panel candidates) were not compared with GPU values for the same candidates;
  same package and options, another device.
- **Exposure** is after the fusion in section 5 and at ranker level in section 3.
- **Not done**: the engine runs of the second sample; extra join depths as training lists; several refit variants
  named in section 3; a Kaggle commit run; `EXPERIMENTS.md` / `INDEX.md`; a dataset upload (there is none to upload).
- **Failed or corrected on the way**: a shell here-document broke on quoting (files written with the file tool);
  a process-kill pattern matched its own shell once (rerun); two queued fits I had tried to skip were taken over
  when the claiming process ended (kept: they became seeds 2 and 3 of the noise estimate); `rank_xendcg` was run
  once and not tuned; the first GPU runner waited an hour-scale for a game and was replaced by a CPU run until the
  GPU was free; one fit crashed on Windows while removing its part files after its scores were saved; the machine
  was shut down mid-study and three fits were resumed from their folds.
