# Joint spectrum-structure model, phase 1: fast ablations and the gate (2026-10-10)

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed, committed, uploaded or downloaded; no `kaggle` command; no package installed;
the visible `test.parquet` was not read; no NIST data. Pre-registration with four dated addenda: `scratch/joint1/PREREG.md`
(written before the first run). **Every number below was measured in this study.** No leaderboard estimate is made.

GPU: **11.6 h of wall time** (budget 15 h), 18 training runs (budget 24), one trainer at a time through `gpushare` (duty 0.8,
8 GB cap); no game ran, no yield, no out-of-memory skip. CPU: at most 4 worker processes or threads of mine. New data on D::
`joint/bench/candfp_final.npz` (257 MB) and two refit caches (16 MB); D: has 3.0 GB free. Everything else is in
`scratch/joint1/` on C: (2.4 GB: run folders, score files, stored ranks, selector fingerprints, the two confirmation
checkpoints; every other checkpoint was deleted). New tracked files: `train/joint/eval_feature.py`, `run_queue.py`,
`selbench.py`, `selwin.py`; changed: `train/joint/model.py`, `data.py`, `train_joint.py`, `README.md` (new flags, defaults =
phase 0; the two test files pass). `INDEX.md` not regenerated, `EXPERIMENTS.md` not edited, notebook code untouched.

"±" = one standard error. **Primary metric** = gain in MRR@25 on DEV when the model's score is added to our ranker (the
165 production inputs + 4 features of the score, LightGBM seeds 0 and 4 averaged, out of fold by scaffold), mean of the c2J
gain and the dropJ gain, SE by bootstrap over molecules. A run = 24 minutes of GPU-busy time (31.5 min of wall time at duty
0.8, about 14,000 steps of batch 256 = 16 passes over the 220,443 training structures).

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The gate for phase 2 is failed on DEV.** The chosen design is the reference (no change was adopted). At three times the
   ablation length, two seeds: primary gain **+0.0132** (seeds +0.0118 / +0.0146), SE 0.0041, **gain minus two SE +0.005**
   against the required +0.015. The weaker reading (gain ≥ 0.015 and above two SE) fails too. c2N: +0.0076 ± 0.0029 (no loss).
2. **FINAL, evaluated once, is higher**: +0.0264 (seeds +0.0250 / +0.0279), SE 0.0049, minus two SE +0.0166. By the
   pre-registration FINAL does not change the verdict; it is above DEV by about two SE of the difference. The two halves
   pooled: +0.020. Inside the rank average with the public ranker (v33b's base list) the gain is the same on both halves:
   **+0.010 ± 0.003 (DEV) and +0.009 ± 0.003 (FINAL)**.
3. **A plateau, not a design problem.** Twelve designs with isomer negatives give +0.009 to +0.017; the reference's own
   reseed noise is 0.005. Three times the length gives the same number. The mean score of eight runs gives +0.0195 ± 0.0042.
4. **What matters is one thing: PubChem-window negatives.** In-batch negatives only: −0.001. Pool isomers only: +0.001.
   PubChem negatives only: +0.014. Everything else (K, mined hard negatives, loss, temperature, dropout, augmentation,
   pooling, auxiliary fingerprint head) is inside the noise; removing the metadata token and the short GNN run lose.
5. **Where the gain sits**: answers that are PubChem-only (dropJ +0.020 DEV, +0.032 FINAL), lists above `lib_max` 0.7,
   molecules with four or more spectra. **On Enveda spectra it is zero or negative** (DEV −0.008 / −0.016 / −0.004 on
   c2J / dropJ / c2N; Enveda NP panel −0.017 / −0.024 / −0.013; FINAL +0.006 / +0.011 / +0.001 and +0.001 / −0.014 /
   −0.004). The model alone ranks the panel at MRR 0.11 to 0.13 on c2J against 0.40 to 0.43 overall. The hidden test is
   Enveda timsTOF natural products.
6. **New information, partly**: within-list rank correlation with the fold-safe FPNet's f.z is 0.47 (c2J), 0.50 (dropJ),
   0.61 (c2N); no ranker input correlates above 0.62. A redundant score (f.z itself through the same refit) gives −0.003.

   Re-ranking the stored 200 rows: R@25 0.73 / R@100 0.83 (current 0.643 / 0.798; ceiling 0.842); eight-model mean 0.77 /
   0.83. Whole windows, 150 molecules: 0.69 / 0.82 single, 0.74 / 0.87 eight-model mean, against 0.61 / 0.75 for the current

   met). Pool answers on gate-open c2J stay at 0.713 / 0.797 (floors 0.711 / 0.794, met).
8. **Phase 2 is not started.** Section 6 says what it would take to believe otherwise.

## 1. Method

- Benchmark, lists, leak-safety: phase 0 (`reviews/joint0-2026-10-09.md`). `data.assert_leak_safe` ran at the start of all
  18 training processes and printed the same counts each time: 220,443 training positives, 55,367 held-out structures,
  **0 shared identifiers, 0 shared with the 5,350 benchmark answers, 0 benchmark spectra of training structures**
  (`scratch/joint1/runs/<tag>/train.log`).
- Queue: `run_queue.py` (detached, resumable from the run folders, ledger `scratch/joint1/ledger.jsonl`); each run is
  trained, scored on DEV (3 s of GPU) and evaluated (`eval_feature.py`, 13 to 18 min of CPU on 2 threads next to the
  next training run). The rank of every answer under every order is stored per run (`ranks_dev.npz`).
- Reseed control: reference seeds 0 and 1: primary +0.0118 and +0.0172, **reseed noise 0.0054** (paired SE of the
  difference 0.0042); stand-alone MRR (mean of c2J and dropJ) 0.392 and 0.383, noise 0.008.
- Null control: f.z through the same four-feature refit: **−0.0030 ± 0.0020** (c2J −0.0035, dropJ −0.0024, c2N −0.0009).
- Adoption rule (pre-registered): better than the reference mean (+0.0145) by more than the reseed noise and above one
  paired SE. **No run met it.** The provisional rule (stand-alone +0.02 and three times its noise) was met by none either.
- Deviations, each logged in `PREREG.md` before the runs concerned: a regularisation axis was inserted (the reference
  ranks the true structure first for 0.90 of the training structures among their real candidates, 0.27 on DEV c2J); the
  3x run moved forward; `merged`, `no_nl`, `mzbin`, `wide`, `l3`, margin loss, Morgan counts were not run (reasons: the
  plateau; no count fingerprints on disk for the PubChem negatives); the GNN run was half length.

## 6. Verdict and what it would take

**Gate failed; phase 2 is not planned in detail.** Best numbers reached on DEV: +0.0172 (one reference seed), +0.0195 ±
0.0042 (mean score of eight runs), +0.0132 for the pre-registered confirmation; the gate needs about +0.025 at this SE.

Reasons to stop here:
1. The gain is flat across every design axis tried and across three times the training time: more ablations of this
   model family will not move it.
2. Where the hidden test lives (Enveda spectra, natural-product standards) the gain is zero or negative on both halves.
3. In the form v33b uses the ranker (rank average with the public ranker) the gain is +0.010 at base-list level, before
   popularity, forward models, engine 2 and the fusion; what survives the fusion cannot be measured with this benchmark.

What it would take to believe otherwise:
- **A second look at FINAL's +0.026** without touching the gate: the same refit protocol on both halves (fit on FINAL,
  apply to DEV) costs 20 CPU-minutes and no GPU. If DEV rises to FINAL's level under the one-fit protocol, the difference
  is the protocol (more ranker training molecules) and the honest estimate is about +0.02 to +0.025; if not, it is the
  molecules. Not run: FINAL was to be evaluated once.
- **A gain on Enveda spectra.** A panel-internal split (train on 200 standards, score the other 50, five folds; 5 runs of
  10 busy minutes from the reference checkpoint) would say whether the model, like the FPNet, learns that library from
  200 examples. Without it a full-data model's value on the hidden test is unknown in both directions.
- **A different scoring function.** The embedding model is a relative of the FPNet (correlation 0.5 to 0.6). The
  cross-encoder over peaks and candidate substructures (stage 8) was not started because the first stage did not pass.
  It is the one design left that could add isomer-level information; it is a new phase with its own gate, not a
  continuation.
- Measured costs, should a later decision need them: hold-out-safe model 31.5 min of wall time at duty 0.8 (24 busy
  minutes; longer is useless), full-data model about 40 min by the same arithmetic (275,810 structures, not run); eight
  models 4.2 h. Inference: 9,307 spectra in 1.2 s and 371,698 candidate fingerprints in about 1 s on this GPU (T4 not
  measured); fingerprints 2.55 ms per PubChem-only candidate on one core, i.e. 400 molecules × 100 joined rows = 1.7
  CPU-minutes, 400 whole windows of 7,300 rows = 2.1 CPU-hours (outside the notebook's budget unless a cheaper structure
  input is trained: the GNN at half length was far behind, 0.19 against 0.40).

## 7. Not verified

- Nothing on the leaderboard; no fused-list replay (the benchmark is at ranker level); no T4 timing.
- Each ablation has one seed; with reseed noise 0.005 a true effect below about ±0.006 is invisible, in either direction.
- The LightGBM refit uses two seeds; its own reseed noise was not measured separately.
- `mzbin`, `merged`, `no_nl`, `wide`, `l3`, margin loss, Morgan counts, a full-length GNN, the cross-encoder: not run.
- The GNN run had half the busy time and a quarter of the molecule-steps; it is a short-run screen, not a comparison.
- Mined negatives came from one fixed model (ref_s0) and trained a new model from scratch; no iterated mining.
- Whole-window recall: 150 molecules, answer rows identified by InChIKey first block, pool rows left in the window.
- Selector exposure and placement with the model's own 100 rows; class-C numbers are on the current rows.
- Game mode, the yield path (exit code 75) and resume after a shutdown were not exercised (no game ran, no shutdown
  happened); the STOP path was used once to end the queue.
- Section 3's sentence on the two protocols: DEV fits use four fifths of DEV, the FINAL fit all of DEV; no test of
  whether that explains the difference.
