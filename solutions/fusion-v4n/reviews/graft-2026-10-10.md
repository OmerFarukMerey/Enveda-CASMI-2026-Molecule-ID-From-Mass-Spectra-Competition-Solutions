## 0. Bottom line

1. **Who wins which answers** [V]. On pool answers the two pipelines trade the same number of first places (mixed
   27 against 29, panel 19 against 15): order of pool rows, nothing structural. Outside the pool the picture is two
   rules: **ours wins with the joined PubChem row placed by the fitted ranker** (116 and 65 first places e006u does
   not have in arm M, 90 and 23 in arm R; in 114 of the 181 and 90 of the 113 the answer sits in e006u's own
   popularity list, in a slot at rank 4 or lower), **e006u wins with fame**: its promotion (S > 6 and popularity ≥ 5) and the popularity
   rows its fusion lifts hold 3 answers first in arm M and 28 + 43 in arm R (mixed, panel) that ours has lower; 37
   of those 71 are joined rows of ours ranked lower, 3 are not among our rows at all.
2. **Promotion precision** [V]. The rule has two branches. **S branch** (S > 6 and popularity ≥ 5): fired 182 times
   in arm R and the promoted row was the answer 182 times; in arm M and on pool-answer lists it fired **0 times in
   1,751 lists** below the gate (a 95% upper bound of about 0.2% to 0.4% on the false-firing rate: the lists are not
   independent, dA and dB share their molecules). **rel branch** (`rel` > 250,
   locally only where the pool window is empty): 12 firings, 0 right. Nothing inside the fired lists separates right
   from wrong (popularity, S, margin, gaps, `lib_max`: precision 0.94 to 1.00 in every third, the 0.94 being the rel
   branch); what separates them is the branch and the unknown itself, whether the answer is famous. **e006 wastes its
   own promotions**: on the panel's dB lists 78 right promotions leave 55 first rows after its engine fusion and 35
   after its key vote (pool rows collect both engines' votes).
3. **e006's deficits** (grafts onto e006u, section 2) [V]: adding **our join** to it gains in both arms (+0.073 ±
   0.008 / +0.027 ± 0.008 masked, +0.050 ± 0.007 / +0.002 ± 0.005 real); replacing its PubChem merge by the join
   loses what its fame rows gave (panel, real: −0.081 ± 0.008); the **rank average** on top is +0.012 (c2) and +0.027

   (their choice to leave it out is right for their list).

### 3.3 The condition under which each graft gains on the hidden set (inference from the tables above)

  more, i.e. hundreds of substance records and PubMed ids (two thirds of the fired rows are above 13.9), and (iii)

  hidden answer is that famous or if the join already ranks the famous ones first.**
- **v66b (joined-row popularity 0.10)** gains if joined answers are more documented than the joined rows around

- **v66c** = the sum; the two act on different lists (the promotion on 13 + 78, the term on every joined list).
- **Not presets**: the free form (Oa2, Oa3: joined rows compete with pool rows that carry 0.25 × their own
  popularity, so an undocumented joined answer sinks: −0.02 to −0.04 in arm M) and the fitted model (Ob3, Od3: needs
  3 to 5% famous answers outside the pool but pays on pool answers and exposure; only worth building if v66a shows

## 4. What the leaderboard constrains

Facts used [C `docs/experiments.md`, `docs/landscape/pubdiff-2026-10-09.md`, `canqiang-e006-2026-10-10.md`]: v59a
0.434 (first row only 0.377, first two rows 0.413); e006 0.440, flexonafft 0.436, v18 0.433 (new regime); old regime:
lszlst b416 (promotion, ICEBERG broken) 0.417 against base415 (no promotion, ICEBERG broken) 0.374 and b419

against 0.409; the join 0.409 → 0.431; `POP_MU` 0.35 against 0.25: 0.414 against 0.421 on a fork. Every one is a
single submission: ±0.02 each, about ±0.028 on a difference.

1. **The tie of the pipelines** (e006 − ours = +0.006 ± 0.028). Let w be the hidden share of PubChem-only answers
   and p the share of them that behave as in arm R. With the local dB differences e006u − ours (mixed −0.185 M,
   −0.081 R; panel −0.135 M, +0.086 R) and pool classes level: panel profile, e006 − ours = w (−0.135 + 0.221 p). At
   w = 0.13 (the join-equation mix of `budget-2026-10-10.md` [C]) every p from 0 to 1 gives −0.018 to +0.011, inside
   one standard error of the observed difference: **no constraint**. At w = 0.38 to 0.45 (its "N = 0.24" mixes)
   p = 0 gives −0.051 to −0.061 (two standard errors below the observation) and the observation itself needs p ≈
   0.67; the one-standard-error range is p from 0.39 to 0.95. With the mixed-regime profile e006u is below ours in
   both arms (its rule fires on 15 of 281 lists there), so the tie cannot be produced at all for w above about
   0.15: either the hidden PubChem-only answers are fewer than that, or they are more famous than the mixed
   regime's, or the local class profiles are off. **I cannot tell which.**
2. **The public line's own promotion contrast**: +0.043 (b416 − base415) or +0.024 (b416 − b419, ICEBERG differs).
   A right promotion moves an answer from slot 4 to rank 1 (+0.75): 4 to 7 of 124 public answers, **if** the
   comments' scores are taken at face value and wrong promotions cost nothing. That is 3 to 6% of the public part
   famous enough for the S rule, in a chain without the join.
3. **Our probes**: 46.7 answers at rank 1, 8.9 at rank 2, reciprocal-rank mass 2.6 at ranks 3 to 25, about 45% not
   in the list. A promotion can only move a famous PubChem-only answer that is not first already: the 4 to 7 of
   point 2 minus those the join ranks first (locally 29 to 76% of the fired lists). That leaves **0 to about 5
   answers, 0.000 to about +0.03**, as the range a promotion in our chain could show on the public part; the lower
   end is as compatible with every number above as the upper.
4. **v5c** (−0.004 ± 0.028) and `POP_MU` 0.35 (−0.007, pool rows) do not separate the arms: v5c's expected value is
   +0.004 to +0.01 if 4 to 7 answers moved from slots 8 to 12 to slot 4, and negative in arm M.

Plainly: the constraints are too weak to give a range for p. The only statement the data supports is the
conditional one: **p = 0 is hard to hold together with a PubChem-only share above about a fifth**, and the public
line's history says a handful of public answers (order 5 of 124) are famous enough for the S rule. Whether our join
already has that handful first is exactly what v66a measures.
