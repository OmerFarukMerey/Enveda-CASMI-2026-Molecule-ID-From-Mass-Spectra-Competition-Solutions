## 7. Honesty and limits

- **Who has seen what.** `pub`: everything (FPNet A, `fe_A/B/D`, public ranker; on the panel the structures, not the
  spectra). `fs` / `avg` below the gate: the v1 side nothing (fold-safe FPNet, ranker fitted without these
  molecules); `fragnet` has 27 of the 380 regime answers and none of the panel's in its roster, the `derivation`
  tables' roster is unknown. Engine 2 and ICEBERG / GLACIER have seen the molecules in every chain. So `fs` − `pub`
  on `c2` / `dA` / `dB` is a lower bound of the hidden-test difference only for the v1 side; after the fusion the
  leaked engine 2 dilutes both.
- **The public chain's standing on unseen molecules is still unmeasured.** Section 2 is a stratification by exposure,
  not an unseen evaluation; every stratum has been seen.
- **FPNet A stands in for `fpnet_full1`**, which is not local. full1 has seen the panel's spectra as well, so the
  production chain would be more leaked on the panel than `pub` is here.
- **The joined candidates are the fold-safe selection's in both chains.** In production they are the public A+B
  selection's; the fitted ranker reads the PubChem rank (`j__pcrank`), whose distribution may differ.
- **The class-2 tie emulation uses one random draw**; with other draws the panel's `c2` baseline moved by 0.02 in the
  bank review. It affects `pub` and `avg`, not `fs` below the gate.
- **The hash-seed dependence** (bottom line 8) is in the stored rows of both chains with unknown seeds (my runs: seed
  0); it adds noise to both sides, not a bias. It also means two Kaggle runs of the same notebook can differ.
- **The standard errors do not contain the reseeding noise of the fit** (0.002 to 0.003 at ranker level, rankbank
  review) nor the choice of the 380 / 250 molecules as a population.
- **Exposure counts** are after the fusion, as in the replays of the other reviews.
- **Not done**: a replay with the production model (it has seen both sets); other gates or weights of the average
  (not tuned: 0.5); the conf-gate; a Kaggle commit run; the dataset upload; `EXPERIMENTS.md` / `INDEX.md`.
- **Failed or corrected on the way**: the dataset cards could not be fetched; the first regime replay crashed on a
  run in which none of three molecules had candidates (loader made tolerant); two parity mismatches were traced to
  the hash seed, not to the wiring; the test's first version expected the wrong exception type from a commit run
  (the cell's final assert stops it); the agent's run was interrupted once by an API limit and resumed from the
  process list, no job was started twice.
