# Can the full replay order the join submissions like the leaderboard, and a lower-exposure variant (2026-10-05)

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed or committed, no `kaggle` command was run, no model was trained, the
visible `test.parquet` was not read, no NIST data. GPU: inference only, one runner process at a time (ICEBERG at most
1.63 GB, GLACIER 0.42 GB, `gpushare` duty 0.8, no game detected; 717 s and 213 s). At most two CPU processes of mine,

caches of `D:\casmi-data\fwd\` were read, not written).

**Every number below was computed in this study** unless a file is named. MRR = MRR@25 on the metric key; "±" is one
standard error of a paired per-molecule difference; (wins / losses) in brackets. Setting: the mixed regime of
`reviews/mixed-regime-2026-10-02.md` and the replay of `reviews/fwd-replay-2026-10-05.md`: 380 hold-out molecules,
types `c1` (150), `c2` (380), `dA` / `dB` (the 325 with a PubChem record). "Exposure" is measured on the final list of a
pool-answer type: **joined first** = lists whose first row entered only through the join; **in the first 25** = such
rows per list; **answer lost first** = the first row is joined and the answer is the first row that is not.
**No leaderboard estimate is given for anything.**

## 0. Bottom line

1. **All eleven configurations were replayed with real forward scores; nine fully, two with stated stand-ins** (v15b:
   45 of 271 joined lists re-run with the 20,000 shortlist, no `c1` run; v15a: the local blend ranker). 5,545 candidates
   were missing from the cached scores and were scored: GLACIER 5,545 of 5,545, ICEBERG 5,542 of 5,545. Nothing was
   zero-filled. v13a, v22a and v4b reproduce the fwd-replay numbers to the fourth decimal.
2. **No pre-specified composite orders the eleven like the leaderboard.** Single types: Spearman −0.09 to +0.45,
   26 to 35 of 55 pairs. Exposure alone: −0.50 to +0.03, 19 to 28 pairs (chance is 27.5). The best class mix on the
   grid is one point, 0.5 `c1` + 0.5 `c2` (Spearman +0.68, 42 of 55); held out one submission at a time it ranks worse
   than a constant guess (mean rank error 3.14 against 2.73) and gets 36.5 of 55 held-out pairs. Real forward scores
   do not change this: the zero-forward replay reaches 38 of 55 at a different mix.
3. **There is no local go / no-go score.** Against v13a (better / equal / worse within ±0.003) the best composite is
   right for 6 of 10, the exposure-plus-gain rule for 7 of 10, and "always worse" is also 7 of 10: the rule vetoes all
   seven losers and all three submissions that tied v13a. What survives is a one-sided reading among the seven
   configurations that join every list below the gate with N ≥ 100: the three at 0.431 have 25 to 26 `c2` lists with a
   joined row first, the four losers 26 to 40 (15.5 of 21 pairs; post hoc). v14b (0.416) is not separable from the
   ties (26 lists).
4. **Part 2: 24 variants, one passes the stated test on v13a, narrowly.** Stage A with the forward terms of joined rows
   at weight 0.25 (`JOIN_FWD_A`): `c2` joined first 25 → 22, answer lost first 8 → 5, `c2` +0.0041 ± 0.0023 (7 / 2),
   `c1` unchanged, `dA` +0.0030 ± 0.0051, `dB` −0.0077 ± 0.0086 (20 / 21). On the v22a lists the same direction
   (31 → 26, `c2` +0.0058 ± 0.0027) and the `dB` condition fails by a small margin (−0.0111 ± 0.0074). Implemented behind a
   default-off flag with a test and a preset. Everything that acts after the fusion fails: it lowers the exposure and
   costs `dB` 0.02 to 0.11.
5. **What this does not support**: any claim that the preset scores above v13a. It is the variant with the least

Reading.

- **The fitted mix is weak evidence and does not hold up.** It is a single grid point; the region within two pairs is
  four points, all with zero weight on both answer-removed types. That part is consistent across both replays and with
  the lbcal fit (`dB` weight 0.065): the leaderboard order of join variants is not carried by the answer-removed gain.
  But what replaces it is `c1` + `c2` MRR at about equal weights, which held out is worse than no model, and which
  cannot tell v12a, v14b and v15a (all below v13a by 0.011 to 0.015) from v13a (composite differences −0.0006 to
  +0.0004 ± 0.002 to 0.004).
- **Exposure alone is at chance over the eleven**, because the two configurations with the least exposure, v4b and
  v19a, score 0.409. Exposure is a cost; the eleven also differ in the gain.
- **Post hoc, not a test** (`cb_check_flag.py`): among the seven configurations that join every list below the gate

  exposure and is not the lowest). "Answer lost first" is worse (12 of 21).

lost every time, 3 of 3. Below that the replay is blind (v14b), and it has nothing to say about a variant that lowers

Per group.

- **Reduced joined weight after the fusion: no.** It removes 5 to 6 of the 25 exposed lists and costs `dB` 0.021 to
  0.061. A joined answer reaches its place through the post-fusion re-rank (fwd-replay section 6: +0.056 per joined
  answer); taking the forward terms from joined rows there takes that back. Weight 0 for joined rows only is worse on
  `dB` than weight 0 for every row (−0.061 against −0.044), because pool rows keep their forward terms and overtake.
- **Reduced joined weight in stage A: the one group that passes.** In stage A a joined row is compared with joined
  and generated rows only (the `|gen` group), so the weight decides which joined row gets the first joined slot, not
  whether it stands above a pool row. At full weight the forward scores are counted twice for a joined row: they
  choose the row that enters the fusion at the top of the joined slots, and the post-fusion re-rank then lifts the same
  row past pool rows. At 0.25: 9 `c2` lists change, 3 pool answers regain the first place, none loses it, a joined
  row tops 1 instead of 4 of the nine; on `dB` 41 lists change, all with a joined answer, 9 first places gained and 11
  lost (`cb_check_flag.py`). Dose: `dB` −0.0018, −0.0077, −0.0160 at 0.5, 0.25, 0; `c2` joined first 24, 22, 23.
- **Caps: no.** Cap 0 removes nearly all exposure (25 → 4; why four lists stay was not looked into) and
  costs 0.11 to 0.12 on the answer-removed types; with the exemption it still costs `dB` 0.039. Caps 1 to 3 change no
  first place, as the lbcal review found on the pre-fusion list, and cost `dB` 0.001 to 0.010 without any win.
- **"First only if first for the ranker and the forward models": no.** 15 to 16 exposed lists remain, `dA` −0.047 to
  −0.066, `dB` −0.045 to −0.048. In the answer-removed lists the joined answer is often first without leading both.
- **Engine-2 fusion.** The RRF already keeps the joined rows in their pre-fusion order relative to each other (each
  has only its v1-side term, which is monotone in its rank), so there is nothing to restore inside the RRF without
  moving them against pool rows, which is what `FUSE_INJ_CREDIT` did. The two versions that exist are in the re-rank
  after it. Keeping the fusion's slots (the emulated "post split" of the fwd-replay review, here as a rule) lowers the
  exposure to 17 lists and costs `dB` 0.036. Keeping the re-rank's slots and restoring the pre-fusion order is exposure
  neutral by construction (`c2` and `c1` identical) and gains on `dB` (+0.0109 ± 0.0057, 23 / 20) but loses on `dA`
  (−0.0423 ± 0.0087, 5 / 45): where engine-2 lists the answer, the fusion had raised it above the other joined rows.
- **Extra 1** changes no `c2` or `c1` list of v13a: a row that both engines put first has an RRF lead the forward terms
  do not overturn. Nothing to implement. **Extra 2** removes 2 exposed lists and fails on `dB` (−0.0084 ± 0.0051).

## 6. Honesty and limits

- **The 380 molecules are leaked for the public models** (`mixed-regime` sections 2 and 7; `fwd-replay` section 8).
  ICEBERG and GLACIER are MassSpecGym models and have very likely seen every answer; engine-2's fingerprint models
  have memorised them. Consequences here:
  - every `dA` / `dB` level and every gain attributed to forward terms on joined answers is an upper bound; the `dB`
    cost of `JOIN_FWD_A` (−0.008) is measured with forward scores that favour the answer more than they will on the
    hidden test, so it is more likely overstated than understated. That is an argument, not a measurement;
  - on `c2` / `c1` the pool answer is helped by the same leak and by engine-2's memory and still loses the first place
    in 8 lists, so the exposure counts are more likely understated. The ranking of configurations by exposure can
    differ on the hidden test, where engine-2 repairs less (v14a, `EXPERIMENTS.md`);
  - Part 1's failure is partly this: the regime's `c2` and `c1` lists are easier than the hidden ones, so its
    differences between join variants on pool answers are a few thousandths, inside their own standard errors.
- **Eleven points, three of them tied and two reproduced with stand-ins.** Any fitted mix is weak evidence; the
  leave-one-out result says the best one is not better than nothing. Differences of 0.001 on the leaderboard are
  below its resolution and were still counted as ordered pairs.
- **Local stand-ins**: fold-safe FPNet instead of the public A+B models (v1 engine, PubChem list); the blend ranker
  of v15a; v15b for 45 of 271 lists; `p_present` taken from the confgate study's file; forward scores equal to
  Kaggle's up to a small tail (r 0.997 to 0.998 on one run, fwd-replay section 1).
- **Composition**: 11% timsTOF and 81% of molecules covered by the forward models (fwd-replay section 8); 74
  molecules contribute nothing to any forward-weight difference, including `JOIN_FWD_A`.
- **Multiple testing in Part 2**: 24 variants, one test set, thresholds of one standard error. The surviving variant
  would not survive a correction; its support is the mechanism and the dose pattern, not the p-value.
- **Not verified**: the flag on Kaggle (no notebook was built); the ICEBERG time budget (unchanged: the flag alters
  no candidate set); `TOPN` above 60.
- **Failed or not reproduced**: v15b's `c1` run does not exist locally (`c1` equals v13a); 226 of its 271 joined lists
  are v13a's; ICEBERG returned no score for 3 of 40,117 candidates. One first attempt to write the scripts through a
  shell here-document failed on quoting and was redone with the file tool; an edit of `fusion_core.py` and
  `00_config.py` first wrote CRLF line endings and was converted back to LF (`git ls-files --eol`: `w/lf`). Nothing
  else failed.
