Why it is not proposed: (1) it was found after seeing M1's split, the forking path the pre-registration forbids; (2) it fires only on
one local class (answer in engine 2's ChEBI / LIPID MAPS set and in PubChem, removed from the pool), whose hidden share was never
measured; (3) size: firing rate in dA 2.5% (m) to 4.9% (u); even if 30% of public answers were of that class, 124 × 0.3 × 0.05 × 0.5 RR
≈ 0.9 RR ≈ **+0.007, below one answer** [I, arithmetic]. Its downside looks near zero (no firing on pool answers locally or at
Kaggle scale), so it is the one lead worth a fresh pre-registration on new dA-type molecules if the team wants a near-free probe.

## 6. Recommendation

**No single-switch patch on stack1 is recommended from this study.** The measured loss is isomer order inside the right
composition (rank 2-25: 58-69% of local loss) and answers outside every source (the hidden test's largest block); none of the cheap
re-orderings of what stack1 already holds moves an answer per 124 molecules in both arms. The PubChem-selector order (v73a, built in
`v71diag`) remains the only built candidate touching the 8-20% PubChem-only bucket.
