# FPNet fine-tuned with PubChem mass-window negatives (2026-10-05)

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed or committed and no Kaggle notebook was run. One fine-tune of 20,000
steps on the shared GPU (`ft_hn`); the second arm (PubChem negatives replacing pool negatives) was not needed and not
run. Scripts: `train/mine_pc_negatives.py`, `train/train_fpnet.py --pc_neg` (defaults unchanged), `scratch/hardneg/`
(log in `LOG.md`). Outputs: `D:\casmi-data\hardneg\` (about 20 GB). "±" is one standard error of a paired
per-molecule difference. **[C]** = count that does not pass through the ranker; **[R]** = local engine MRR, the kind
of number that has not predicted the leaderboard.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The selection improves by 7 to 12 points at every depth, on every set and split [C].** Answer among the first
   25 / 50 / 100 / 200 PubChem-only structures, 1,014 unseen molecules with a PubChem record: control 60.3 / 67.1 /
   71.8 / 75.9%, fine-tune 72.6 / 76.7 / 79.5 / 82.2% (+0.123 ± 0.011, +0.097 ± 0.010, +0.077 ± 0.009,
   +0.063 ± 0.008). The fine-tune has the answer in its first 25 as often as the control in its first 100.
2. **On Enveda's own natural-product panel (250 molecules, timsTOF, never seen): 70.4 / 74.8 / 83.6 / 86.4% →
   80.4 / 86.8 / 90.4 / 92.8% [C].**
3. **Nothing is lost on the pool side; it gains.** Hold-out top-1 0.669 → 0.685 (+0.016 ± 0.006), np panel
   0.345 → 0.403 (+0.060 ± 0.022). More training and natural-product weighting had moved neither.
4. **Most of what was learned is a prior, not better reading of spectra.** Two thirds of the change of the logits
   is one constant vector; added to the control's logits it reproduces +0.06 of the gain at every depth. It says
   which PubChem rows look like structures that get measured. It was learned from the training libraries only; that
   it holds on the Enveda panel is the best local evidence that it carries to the hidden test, and no more than that.
5. **Secondary, local engine with 100 joined candidates, public ranker, gate 0.7, before the fusion [R]:** answer
   removed from the pool +0.028 ± 0.009 (44 wins / 16 losses), class-1-like −0.002 ± 0.001 (1 / 4), answer a pool row
   without spectra +0.001 ± 0.004 (13 / 20). The exposure does not change (joined rows in the first 25 and on top: the same).
6. **Recommendation: yes, run the production fine-tune** (the same recipe on public models A and B, section 7;
   about 1 h of mining and 1 h of GPU at full duty) and spend submissions on N 100 and N 50 with it.

## 1. The negatives [C]

`train/mine_pc_negatives.py`, 2 workers, 2.6 h. Training structures only: 219,155 pool entries of structures whose
fold is neither `hold` nor `np`, in 45,591 formulas. Window: **±10 ppm** around the exact mass (the window of the
channel and of the pool loss; 5 ppm would hold 1,192 / 6,665 / 16,340 rows at the 10 / 50 / 90% point).

| | |
|---|---|
| PubChem rows in the window, per structure | 2,180 / 11,300 / 21,000 (10 / 50 / 90%), mean 11,785; empty for 0.7% |
| scanned per formula | at most 1,024 random window rows, one per InChIKey14 |
| hard negatives | 16 per structure: highest ECFP4 Tanimoto to the answer in the scanned sample |
| their similarity | hardest 0.28 / 0.41 / 0.69 (10 / 50 / 90%); mean of the 16: 0.22 / 0.29 / 0.40 |
| random negatives | 32 per formula, shared by its structures; 16 drawn per training sample |
| same formula as the answer | hard 80.7%, random 43.1% |
| not the structure itself | rows with its InChIKey14s or an identical ECFP4 dropped; metric key checked on 11,641 structures: 25 of 186,234 hard negatives carry the answer's key (0.013%) |
| stored | 3,377,070 unique rows, selected-bit fingerprints packed: `D:\casmi-data\hardneg\w10\fp.npy` 4.0 GB + index tables |

Hardness is by similarity, not by the model's own score, and from a sample of the window: these are semi-hard
negatives. They are hard enough: at the first step of the fine-tune the model, on structures it was trained on,
ranks a PubChem negative above the answer for 24% of the samples (7% against pool negatives alone).

## 2. The fine-tune

`ft_hn`: from `fpnet_fold0.pt`, batch 64, lr 1e-4, warmup 500, 20,000 steps, ranking loss over the answer + 63 pool
negatives + 32 PubChem negatives (16 hard, 16 random). Control `ft_ctrl`: the same without `--pc_neg`. The GPU share
saw a "game" all night (WidgetBoard.exe is not in the desktop list of `gpushare.py`; the other agent's job held more
than 5 GB at the start), so the run took 2.3 h at duty 0.25. Training accuracy 0.76 → 0.87, ranking loss 1.61 →
0.47; BCE on the bits 0.41 → 0.50 (the logits are less calibrated as probabilities; f.z ranks better).

Leak check: 0 of the 219,155 structures with negatives are held-out structures; no key is in both a held-out and a
training fold; the training spectra are the control's (1,982,405); all evaluation molecules are in `hold` or `np`.
The other direction exists and works against the fine-tune: 86 of 380 regime answers, 166 of 900 of the second set
and 105 of 250 panel answers were among the negatives of some training structure.

## 5. What the model learned: a prior for measured structures [C]

`bias_check.py`, regime. b = mean over molecules of (logits of `ft_hn` − logits of `ft_ctrl`), computed on one half
of the molecules and applied to the other. The two half-vectors have cosine 0.999, and b carries 66% of the squared
change of the logits: most of the fine-tune is the same shift for every spectrum. Raw rank of the answer among the
shortlist rows (duplicates and pool members counted, hence lower levels than section 3):

| Scorer | rank ≤ 25 | ≤ 50 | ≤ 100 | ≤ 200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| control | 0.532 | 0.597 | 0.665 | 0.708 |
| control + b | 0.594 (+0.062 ± 0.022) | 0.658 (+0.062 ± 0.018) | 0.729 (+0.065 ± 0.017) | 0.766 (+0.058 ± 0.017) |
| fine-tune | 0.612 (+0.080 ± 0.020) | 0.686 (+0.089 ± 0.018) | 0.729 (+0.065 ± 0.017) | 0.785 (+0.077 ± 0.016) |
| fine-tune − b | 0.338 | 0.449 | 0.535 | 0.612 |
| b alone (no spectrum) | 0.040 | 0.062 | 0.089 | 0.163 |

Reading: the answer is a structure somebody measured; most PubChem rows of its mass are not that kind of structure,
and a model that only ever saw pool decoys (training structures and COCONUT) had no reason to learn the difference.
Three quarters or more of the gain is that prior; the rest depends on the spectrum. Consequences:

- It is the same kind of information as the popularity prior and the curated-database flags, which looked good on
  hold-outs and did not all hold on the leaderboard (`docs/competition/rules-and-pitfalls.md`, pitfall 7). The
  difference: it is a property of the structure, not a membership list, and it holds on the np panel, whose
  structures (Enveda natural products) are in no training fold. Not run on the panel: this decomposition.
- A hidden answer that is unlike anything in the training libraries gets no help and can lose places to
  library-like decoys. 86 molecules of 1,014 lost rank locally.
- There is a no-training variant: add b (a vector of 10,226 numbers) to the A+B logits inside the PubChem channel.
  It was not built; the fine-tune is the tested object.

## 7. Production: what a Kaggle run needs (not started)

The channel on Kaggle averages public models A and B (`ahmedberatozer/casmi26-v3-models`, `fpnet_0.pt`,
`fpnet_1.pt`; `nb/embed/pc/pc_runner.py` asserts exactly two `fpnet_*.pt` in its model directory). `ft_hn` itself
is fine-tuned from the fold-safe model and is weaker than A+B on molecules they have seen; it is the evidence, not
the artefact. Production = the same fine-tune applied to A and to B, on all structures:

```bash
P=.venv/Scripts/python; T=solutions/fusion-v4n/train; H=D:/casmi-data/hardneg
M="--work D:/casmi-data/directions/run/work --code D:/casmi-data/directions/code --tier D:/casmi-data/pubchem_study/tier --ik14 D:/casmi-data/pubchem_study/popds/pc_ik14.npy"
# 1. negatives of the 55,367 held-out structures (the training ones exist in $H/w10): about 40 min on 2 workers
$P $T/mine_pc_negatives.py $M --only hold,np --out $H/w10_hold && $P $T/mine_pc_negatives.py $M --only hold,np --out $H/w10_hold --assemble
# 2. model B is not on this machine
$P solutions/fusion-v4n/tools/download_data.py ahmedberatozer/casmi26-v3-models
# 3. the fine-tune, once per model, nothing held out (26 min each at duty 0.8, 2.3 h each while gpushare sees a game)
for m in 0 1; do $P $T/guard_run.py --free_gb 12 --checks 2 --every 20 -- python $T/train_fpnet.py \
    --code 'D:\casmi-data\directions\code' --run 'D:\casmi-data\directions\run' --init <v3-models>/fpnet_$m.pt --holdout none \
    --pc_neg $H/w10,$H/w10_hold --pc_k 32 --pc_hard 16 --bs 64 --lr 1e-4 --warmup 500 --max_steps 20000 \
    --duty 0.8 --game_duty 0.25 --yield_gb 11 --resume --tag prod_hn_$m; done
```

Then: `prod_hn_0_final.pt` and `prod_hn_1_final.pt` renamed `fpnet_0.pt` / `fpnet_1.pt` in a new private Kaggle
dataset (provenance: public A / B, Apache-2.0 pipeline, fine-tuned on `train.parquet` with PubChem structures as
negatives; no NIST), and one new config key in `nb/01_setup.py` / `nb/04_pubchem.py` that points the PubChem
channel's model directory at it (the engine keeps A+B). Run time on Kaggle does not change. Candidate submissions:
v13a with the new selection at N 100, and at N 50 (the fine-tune's first 50 hold the answer more often than the
current first 100, with half the joined rows).

Caveats. (a) `--holdout none` trains on every fold; the hold-out table printed by that run is then not a hold-out.
(b) A and B have seen every training molecule, so nothing local can verify the production checkpoints on unseen
molecules: check that their selection on the regime molecules is not worse than A+B's and let the leaderboard
decide. (c) `--only` and the comma list of `--pc_neg` were added after the fine-tune and are checked on CPU only
(two directories return the same rows as one). (d) Add `WidgetBoard` to `gpushare.DESKTOP` first, or the runs take
five times as long.

## 8. Limits of this study

- One fine-tune, one seed, one choice of negatives (10 ppm, 16 hard by similarity + 16 random, sample of 1,024).
  Nothing was tuned; the second arm was not run because the first is not ambiguous.
- All three sets are molecules of libraries the training data also comes from. The panel is the nearest thing to
  the hidden test and it is 250 molecules.
- The tier lacks the answer's row for 9% of the record molecules; windows are the uncorrected ±10 ppm.
- Machine: the mining job and the fine-tune were each stopped once by the 2 h limit of background jobs and resumed;
  for about ten minutes three CPU workers ran instead of two (a kill that did not take).
