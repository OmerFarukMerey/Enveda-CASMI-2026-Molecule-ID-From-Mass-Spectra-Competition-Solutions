## 1. What was done, and what was fixed first

`scratch/dreamsim/PREREG.md`, written before the first retrieval number.

- **Encoder.** `ssl_model.ckpt` (Zenodo 10997887, CC BY 4.0, compliance in `reviews/dreams-2026-10-06.md`), loaded with
  `scratch/dreams/dr_lib.py`; the embedding is the precursor token of the last layer, 1,024-d, L2-normalised.
- **What the model was trained on** (paper, `scratch/dreams/paper/dreams.txt`): GeMS spectra, "only spectra acquired in
  positive ionization mode", singly charged, precursor m/z ≤ 1,000; its input is the 100 most intense peaks plus a precursor
  token (m/z, intensity 1.1). It has **no adduct and no polarity input**, and its charge feature is 1. Handling here: every
  spectrum of every adduct and both polarities is embedded as it is (precursor m/z as measured, charge 1). Negative mode
  (639,968 library spectra, 389 of the 1,184 panel query spectra) and non-protonated adducts are therefore off-label; they
  are reported separately. Spectra with a precursor above 1,000 or no peak at or below 1,000 get no embedding (32,888
  library spectra; 26 of 1,184 panel and 44 of 1,423 mixed query spectra).
- **Library.** The v1 cache: 2,539,608 spectra of 275,810 structures, peaks as `casmi.build` cleans them (cut above
  precursor + 2 Da, 0.1% floor, at most 512 peaks). Query spectra are cleaned the same way (embedding of cleaned against
  raw query peaks: median cosine 1.0000). Unit of retrieval: the metric key; a key's similarity is the maximum over its
  spectra. **The answer's own key is removed with all its spectra from every library.**
- **Queries.** The Enveda panel (250 timsTOF natural products = all of `enveda-np-examples`, 1,184 spectra) and the mixed
  380 (1,423 spectra), which holds 40 Enveda-180 molecules as the drug-like timsTOF contrast (a larger Enveda-180 draw was
  not run). 620 of 630 molecules have a valid DreaMS query spectrum (246 + 374); all methods are compared on those.
- **Methods.**
  - `a` the v1 engine's kernel as used: one representative per structure, polarity and library, query merged per
    polarity, `casmi.spectra.search_shift` (hybrid entropy similarity), same polarity, ± 200 Da.
  - `b` engine 2's kernel: one representative per structure (timsTOF first), every query spectrum, maximum of direct and
    shifted entropy similarity, ± 200 Da, set "all".
  - Both are the re-implementation of `reviews/enveda180z-2026-10-06.md` (`scratch/zen180/s6_analog.py`), read from its
    cache `D:\casmi-data\zen180\analog_*\` (first 400 representatives per molecule).
  - `c_max` / `c_mean` DreaMS cosine, query spectra aggregated by maximum / mean, all masses and polarities; `c_win` =
    `c_max` inside ± 200 Da of the answer's mass; `c_winpol` = `c_win` with same-polarity pairs only.
  - `ra_ac`, `ra_bc` rank average of `a` (`b`) and `c_win` over the union of both first 400 (a missing rank counts 401);
    `ra_ab` the two kernels averaged, as the control for "averaging anything helps".
- **Gate for step 3, fixed first**: share of panel molecules with T ≥ 0.7 among the first five; DreaMS or a rank average
  must beat both kernels by more than two standard errors there and not be worse at twenty.
- **Who has seen what.** The kernels are not learned. DreaMS: self-supervised on unannotated MassIVE / GNPS spectra; the
  public library spectra of these compounds are probably among them (245 of the 250 panel structures have gnps / mona /
  massbank spectra), the Enveda timsTOF spectra unknown; no structure labels in any case. So DreaMS is tested here on
  library spectra of the kind it was trained on, which favours it, and still does not win. Engine 2 in section 4: its
  FPNets have not seen the panel (regime audit) and its rankers are cross-fitted on it; on the mixed 380 its FPNets have
  seen the 42% with collision energy and the rankers are the public ones.
- **Not computed**: maximum common substructure (not cheap for 620 × 9 lists × 20; the two fingerprints agree in sign
  everywhere).

Reading.
- **The premise does not hold for the v1 kernel.** On timsTOF natural-product queries against a library that is 55%
  non-timsTOF, the hybrid entropy kernel finds structurally closer analogs than the DreaMS embedding. The cross-instrument
  argument is not visible either: on the panel 59% of the v1 kernel's first analogs are structures that only have

- **The rank average is a real but shallow gain**: it puts more close relatives into the first twenty (and one at the

- **Against the ceiling** both are far away: 72% of the panel has a library relative with T ≥ 0.7 inside the window, the

### 4.1 Reading

- **Engine 2 did not improve with DreaMS neighbours; it got worse in every arm that contains them**, on both sets, beyond
  two standard errors for `c` and (mixed) `bc`. Adding DreaMS to the v1 kernel's list (`ac` against `a`) costs 0.048 on the
  panel and 0.055 on the mixed set: the structural gain of the rank average at depth 20 does not reach the list.
- **Where it sits**: the loss is in the lists whose answer starts first (panel: 0 helped, 21 to 46 hurt; −0.10 to −0.24
  per list), the gain in those where it starts at 2 to 40 (+0.03 to +0.10, wins about equal to losses at 2 to 10). The
  strata are selected on the label, so some of this is regression to the mean; the total is what counts and it is
  negative.
- **Why a better depth-20 list still loses**: engine 2's analog features are maxima of T(candidate, analog) × similarity³
  (and ⁶), so the first few analogs decide; there the rank average is not better (first five: +3.3 ± 2.5 against `a`,
  −2.4 ± 2.6 for `ra_bc` against `a`), and DreaMS's first analogs are class-level neighbours with lower T (top-1 mean
  0.357 against 0.432). The rankers were also fitted to the kernel's lists.
- **Limit of this integration**: positions carry the kernel's similarity values; a learned weight for a separate DreaMS
  analog feature (a new feature block, rankers re-fitted) was not tried. It would need new ranker rows, which is not the
  "smallest integration" and not justified by section 2.

### 4.2 By-product: engine 2 with the v1 kernel's analogs

Not the question of this task, and not a recommendation. Facts: `a` finds closer analogs than `b` on the same library
(+11.0 ± 2.7 on the panel, +3.7 ± 1.8 on the mixed set, first five); engine 2 re-scored with that list is +0.036 ± 0.019
(panel, 1.9 standard errors; +0.046 ± 0.019 against the matched control `b2`) and +0.030 ± 0.012 (mixed). Against it: it
is a trade that loses correct first places (0 / 17 and 0 / 16), the pattern that has lost on the leaderboard before; it
makes engine 2's evidence a copy of the v1 engine's, and engine 2's worth in the fusion is that it differs; `b` here is the
re-implementation of engine 2's "all" set and the position-mapped similarities are an artefact of this harness; nothing
was run through the fusion. If someone wants to test it, the honest version is `Engine`-style representatives (per
structure, polarity and library, query merged per polarity) inside `casmi_engine.analog_search`, then engine 2's own
lists with cross-fitted rankers, then the fusion with exposure.

## 6. Decision

Nothing wired: no flag, no dataset, no preset, no test. The idea as stated (DreaMS neighbours are structurally closer than
the kernels' neighbours on timsTOF queries) is measured and false for the v1 kernel; for engine 2's kernel DreaMS is level
alone, better in a rank average by structure, and worse in engine 2's list. The stage-level metric and the engine-level
check agree in sign for DreaMS alone and disagree for the rank averages, which is itself worth keeping: **a structurally
better analog list at depth 20 did not make a better engine-2 list.**

## 7. Failed, not done, not verified

- **The gate fixed in advance was not met** (first five: +3.3 ± 2.5) and step 3 was run all the same; its arms and the
  position-mapped similarities were chosen after seeing section 2, before any engine-2 number.
- **Kernels `a` and `b` are re-implementations** (cache of the 10-06 Zenodo review): the answer's exact mass stands in for
  the engines' target mass, engine 2's representatives are read from the v1 cache, only its "all" set is computed (not the
  per-polarity lists), and only the first 400 representatives per molecule are kept. `b2` shows the cost of that
  difference inside engine 2: −0.009 ± 0.005 on the panel, +0.001 ± 0.003 on the mixed set.
- **Enveda-180 contrast**: the 40 Enveda-180 molecules of the mixed set only; no larger draw.
- **Maximum common substructure**: not computed.
- **fp16**: NaN embeddings under autocast; cause not looked for.
- **T4**: no timing; scaled.
- **Negative mode and other adducts** are embedded off-label; no adduct-specific treatment (e.g. shifting the precursor
  to the [M+H]+ mass) was tried. Spectra with a precursor above 1,000 have no embedding (10 of 630 molecules dropped from
  the comparison).
- **GeMS overlap** with the panel compounds or with Enveda's spectra: not checked (as on 10-06).
- **Redundancy with `fe_D.pt`'s fingerprint features**, the generator's parents with DreaMS, the v1 engine with a
  replaced analog list, a separate DreaMS analog feature with re-fitted rankers, the fusion and exposure: not run.
- `embedding_model.ckpt` (the contrastive fine-tune of the paper, trained on MoNA) is the checkpoint the paper uses for
  similarity; it is not local and was not used. The SSL checkpoint's precursor token is what was measured.
- One seed of the cross-fitted engine-2 rankers; reseeding noise is not in the standard errors.
- The solution's tests were not run (no solution file changed). `INDEX.md` not regenerated: run `python tools/gen_index.py`
  when this file is committed.
