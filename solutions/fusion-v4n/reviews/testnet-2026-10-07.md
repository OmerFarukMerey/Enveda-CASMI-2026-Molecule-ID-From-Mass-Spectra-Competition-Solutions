# The test set as a network: do neighbouring unknowns constrain each other's candidate lists? (2026-10-07)

**The family structure is too sparse, and the rule's ceiling too low, to matter: on the Enveda panel 32% of molecules have a
spectral neighbour at 0.7 but only 19% have one that is a structural analog, and the propagation rule fed the neighbours'
TRUE structures gains +0.004 ± 0.002 MRR over all lists. Nothing was wired.**

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed, committed or uploaded; no `kaggle` command; no model trained; no GPU used; no
NIST data. CPU: at most 3 numba threads / one process of mine at below-normal priority. Scripts: `scratch/testnet/`
(git-ignored). Outputs: `D:\casmi-data\testnet\` (new; every other cache was read only). **No file of the solution was
changed except this review**; no preset, no test, no notebook build (section 6 says which condition failed). `INDEX.md` and
`EXPERIMENTS.md` not edited.

**Every number below was computed in this study** (`D:\casmi-data\testnet\net_stats.json`, `report.md`, `cluster.md`). MRR =
MRR@25 on the metric key; "±" = one standard error of a paired difference, per molecule in the type tables and

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Rules.** `docs/competition/overview.md` and `rules-and-pitfalls.md` contain nothing that forbids using relations among
   the test molecules: the stated limits are notebook-only, 9 h, no internet, free public data and models, no NIST. The idea
   uses the test spectra and our own lists inside the run only, which is also what pitfall 2 asks for. The full Kaggle rules
   page is not in the repo and was not read here (not verified). `docs/landscape/literature-2026-10-05.md` item 8 lists
   self-training on the test spectra as not recommended; no public notebook in our landscape notes builds a test network.
2. **Network, kernel as the engines preprocess spectra: unusable in its shifted form.** The precursor peak is kept, so a
   shifted kernel matches precursor to precursor: on the mixed 380 the modified-cosine edges at ≥ 0.7 (2,391 of them) have
   mean T 0.11, the value of random pairs (0.10). With peaks at or above precursor − 1.5 removed (changed after seeing this,
   before any rule number; `PREREG.md` addendum 1) the shifted entropy kernel is clean enough to use, and is the rule's kernel.
3. **Network, rule kernel, Enveda panel (250 = all of `enveda-np-examples`; there is no wider Enveda NP set in train):**
   molecules with a neighbour at 0.6 / 0.7 / 0.8: **46% / 32% / 13%**. Edges at ≥ 0.7: 66, mean T 0.42, **39% with T ≥ 0.5**;
   at ≥ 0.8: 19 edges, mean T 0.62, 79% with T ≥ 0.5. Molecules whose best neighbour is a real analog (T ≥ 0.5): 48 of 250
   (19%) at 0.7, 28 (11%) at 0.8. Formula difference of the answers on the 66 edges: 3 isomers, 22 one common
   biotransformation (CH2 5, hexose 4, O 2, H2 2, acetyl 2, C2H4 2, ...), **41 (62%) neither**.
4. **Size.** The panel cannot be resampled up to 400. Subsampled, the share at 0.7 rises 10% (50) → 17% (100) → 23% (150) →
   28% (200) → 32% (250), so a 400-molecule draw of the same population would be at or above 32% (extrapolation, not
   measured). 400-molecule draws from other parts of train: gnps 7.9% (sd 2.2, edges as good as the panel's: T 0.41), riken
   30% (edges are NOT analogs: T 0.16, 4% with T ≥ 0.5), enveda-180 14.5% (T 0.19, 1%), mixed 380 7.4%. The hidden set's
   family structure is unknown and could be denser (few extracts) or sparser than the panel.
5. **The rule on the panel as a test set, final re-rank, W 0.5, SIM 0.7 (primary):** leaked lists (v33b's chain) composite
   +0.0032 ± 0.0021; fold-safe lists (U chain) **+0.0011 ± 0.0015**. Where the answer starts at rank 2 to 25: leaked
   +0.0155 ± 0.0063 (30 / 15), **fold-safe +0.0070 ± 0.0041 (28 / 12; 1.7 SE)**. Where it starts first: −0.0024 ± 0.0014
   (0 / 3) and −0.0019 ± 0.0013 (0 / 2). Before the forward re-rank (point A): nothing (+0.0003 ± 0.0018, +0.0006 ± 0.0008).
6. **Oracle ceiling** (neighbours' true structures instead of their lists, same rule): composite +0.0048 ± 0.0024 leaked,
   **+0.0042 ± 0.0022 fold-safe**; rank 2 to 25: +0.0224 ± 0.0071 and +0.0167 ± 0.0064. The lists realise a quarter (fold-safe) to two thirds
   (leaked) of a ceiling that is itself about one answer in 250 molecules. The limit is not the neighbours' lists: knowing the neighbour
   exactly does not separate the answer from the same-scaffold isomers that fill its own list.
7. **Failure mode.** A correct first place demoted: 3 lists (leaked) and 2 (fold-safe) of the panel's rank-1 lists, 0 in the
   mixed 380. Only 1 of the 5 was caused by a neighbour's wrong candidate; 4 were supported by the neighbour's true
   structure, and the oracle demotes as many (4 and 2). The "two wrong neighbours reinforce each other" case is rare; the
   term itself prefers the more analog-like isomer. At W 1.0 it demotes 24 and 16 first places. The confidence condition
   (propagate only from neighbours with `lib_max` ≥ 0.7) halves the gain and does not remove the demotions.
8. **Wiring conditions**: network share ≥ 20% is met nominally on the panel at 0.7 (not at 0.8, not for gnps or the mixed
   set); the gain beyond two SE on not-first lists on fold-safe lists is **not met** (1.7 SE on the panel, 1.3 on the mixed
   set); no type loses beyond one SE (met); exposure rises by one list on the fold-safe panel, 4 → 5 (**not met**).

## 1. What was fixed in advance

`scratch/testnet/PREREG.md`, written before the first number; one addendum (kernel, item 2 above), written after the
network statistics and before any rule number.

- **Network.** Spectra cleaned as the engines do (`pv._clean`: floor 0.002, 256 peaks, entropy weighting). Kernels: the
  engines' `entropy_sim` (plain) and `entropy_sim_shift` (reference shifted by the precursor m/z difference: "mod"); classic
  cosine and GNPS-style modified cosine (sqrt intensity, greedy matching; in the precursor-removed variant at least 4 matched
  peaks). Tolerance 0.01. Same ionisation mode only, same adduct and across adducts; molecule pair = best spectrum pair.
  Neutral mass = median over a molecule's spectra.
- **Rule.** For A with at most 5 neighbours B (s_AB ≥ SIM): support(c) = Σ_B s_AB · max over d in B's top 5 of
  T(c, d) / √rank(d), counted only if mass(formula c) − mass(formula d) equals nm_A − nm_B within max(0.002, 10 ppm · (nm_A +
  nm_B)), and 0 when c and d share the metric key (test molecules are distinct). All supports are computed from the lists
  before propagation, so molecule order does not matter; a molecule without neighbours or without support spread is returned
  untouched. Point B: final 25, z(1 / (3 + rank)) + W · z(support), stable sort. Point A: base list, score ← z(score) +
  W · z(support), rows left in place for the popularity prior and stage A to consume. W 0.5 (primary) and 1.0; one round
  (two reported); confidence variant and oracle as in item 6 and 7.
- **Decision rule**: on point B W 0.5 and point A W 0.5 only; any other variant that passes is post hoc and not wired.

## 5. Why the ceiling is low

- Spectral neighbours are analogs only at the top of the similarity scale. At 0.7 three edges in five join molecules with
  T < 0.5, and 62% of the edges have a formula difference that is no single biotransformation.
- Where the neighbour IS an analog, the answer's competitors are its isomers on the same scaffold. All of them satisfy the
  mass condition and all are similar to the neighbour, so the support term has little spread across the rows that matter.
- The answer must already be in the list, below first: 86 of 904 fold-safe panel lists (117 if "absent" counted, which the
  rule cannot reach).
- The panel is the favourable case: 44% of its molecules have a true analog (T ≥ 0.5) inside the set and its library
  coverage is high. Whether the hidden test's families are denser is unknown; nothing here measures it.

**Nothing was wired**: no `TEST_NET` keys, no function under `nb/embed/`, no test, no preset `v39a`, no notebook build. The
failed conditions are the second and the fourth, and the oracle ceiling says a better-tuned version of this rule would not
change the answer.

What the idea would still need to become a candidate, none of it run: a set with the hidden test's kind of difficulty and
unknown neighbours absent from the library (the panel's neighbours are library compounds); a support term that separates
isomers on a shared scaffold (e.g. the forward models scoring the pair's shifted fragments, not a fingerprint similarity);
and a log line on a real hidden run to learn how dense its network is. The last is the only cheap one: a default-off,
read-only statistic (molecules with a neighbour at 0.7 and 0.8, edges, with the precursor region removed) would cost one
submission's log and change no list. It was not added here because the task tied any wiring to the conditions above.

## 7. Honesty and limits

- **Kernel changed once after seeing data** (precursor region removed), before any rule number; both preprocessings are
  reported. The SIM 0.8 result is post hoc.
- **The 400-molecule figure for Enveda NPs is an extrapolation** from subsamples of 250; a larger Enveda NP set does not
  exist in train. The riken and enveda-180 draws show that a high neighbour share can carry no structural meaning.
- **Seen models**: section 3. The forward models are leaked in both chains. The library holds the batch's other molecules.
- **Start-rank strata are selected on the label**; they describe the rule and cannot validate a change to the base list.
- **One seed** of the out-of-fold ranker; its reseeding noise is not in the standard errors.
- **Fold-safe panel = 226 molecules**, leaked panel = 250: the two panel rows are not on identical molecules.
- **Run time was not measured separately.** The network of 250 to 400 molecules (1,200 to 3,000 spectra, four kernels) and
  all seventeen variants of one type ran in seconds to tens of seconds on 3 threads; no notebook-scale timing exists.
