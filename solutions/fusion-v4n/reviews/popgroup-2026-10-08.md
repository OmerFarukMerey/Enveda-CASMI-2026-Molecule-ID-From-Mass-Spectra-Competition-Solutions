Reading.
- **A confident first place is not what gets broken.** The broken first places on the mixed regime have a larger margin
  (median 0.71) than the fixed ones on either set (0.59, 0.25); "margin below 1" keeps 16 of 20 panel fixes and 20 of 36
  mixed breaks.
- **A large popularity margin is the wrong condition.** Where the most popular isomer leads the current first place by
  10×, the panel gains 4 / 0 and the mixed regime loses 0 / 12: a tenfold more documented isomer is exactly the trap for
  an obscure answer. Requiring the two most popular members to differ by 10× leaves almost nothing (98 panel lists, +1.75
  of +13.59).
- **Absolute fame helps a little** (max `lsid` ≥ 6: +7.12 against −3.00) but mostly by cutting volume.
- **`lib_max` separates.** At 0.9 and above the term is pure loss on the mixed regime (2 / 22) and nothing on the panel
  (0 / 0): the ranker has the library spectrum and is right; the famous isomer is a different compound. Below 0.7 the
  panel keeps 11 of its 20 fixes with no break and the mixed loss shrinks from −15.38 to −3.42.
- **Natural-product-likeness of the list.** COCONUT share ≥ 0.5 holds in 417 of 438 panel lists, 351 of 373 mixed
  natural-product lists and 44 of 111 lists that are not natural products. It removes 40% of the non-natural-product
  loss (−4.51 of −11.04) and nothing else. On the hidden test (natural products only) such a condition would be on
  nearly always. **So the losses on the 120 non-natural products are a harm check that says little about the hidden
  test; the mixed regime's natural products, and their less famous half, are the rows that count.**
- **`dA` / `dB` gain on both sets** (+8.52 and +7.20). Section 4 shows that this is not a popularity effect.

## 8. The two-sided arithmetic

**The raw form (v46x).**
- The panel says: +0.0212 ± 0.0040 composite; `c1` +0.019, `c2` +0.036 (18 first places gained, 1 lost on 452 lists),
  `dA` +0.009, `dB` +0.021.
- The mixed natural products say: −0.0034 ± 0.0051 composite, made of `c1` −0.017 ± 0.014 and `c2` −0.020 ± 0.010 (8
  gained, 21 lost on 401 lists) against `dA` +0.013 and `dB` +0.010; their less famous half −0.0221 ± 0.0102 with `c1`
  −0.059 and `c2` −0.052 (3 gained, 19 lost).
- The `dA` / `dB` gains are in both sets and are mostly the fusion effect (section 4). On pool answers alone the two
  sets disagree in sign: panel (`c1` + `c2`) / 2 = +0.028, mixed natural products −0.018, less famous half −0.055.
- If a share p of the hidden answers behaved like the panel and the rest like the mixed natural products, the local
  composite breaks even at p = 0.14; with the rest like the less famous half, at p = 0.51. This is arithmetic on local
  numbers whose sign agreement with the leaderboard is 5 to 7 of 11; it is not an estimate.

**The gated form (v46b / v46c).** Panel +0.0120 ± 0.0030, mixed natural products +0.0017 ± 0.0024, less famous half
−0.0025 ± 0.0044; break-even against the less famous half at p = 0.17. It gives up half of the panel gain to make the
other side close to zero. Its popularity-specific part is small and one-sided on the panel (`c2` +0.023 ± 0.007, 15
wins / 0 losses; first places 9 / 0) and slightly negative within noise elsewhere (mixed `c1` + `c2` first places 3 / 5,
less famous 2 / 5).
