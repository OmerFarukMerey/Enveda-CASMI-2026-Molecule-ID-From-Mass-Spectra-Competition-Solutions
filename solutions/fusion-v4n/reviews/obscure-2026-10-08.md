# Is the fame of the answer the one variable that separates local validation from the hidden test? (2026-10-08)

**First line: no.** A local regime restricted to obscure answers does not reproduce the leaderboard's signs better than
the full local sets. Pre-stated primary definition (bottom tercile of substance records): **6 of 14 decidable signs in
the leaked chain against 8 on the full set, 5 of 14 in the unseen chain against 5**; its correlation intervals include
zero. No validator was packaged. Fame does move the three popularity-dependent contrasts toward the leaderboard, weakly;
it moves the forward-weight and engine-fusion contrasts away from it and leaves the join contrasts wrong.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Verdict: the obscure regime is not a better validator.** The rule was at least two more decidable signs than the
   full sets in both chains and a correlation interval excluding zero. T3 (162 molecules, median 28 records): leaked
   chain 6 of 14 (full set 8), unseen chain 5 of 14 (full set 5); Spearman +0.13 (−0.17 to +0.35) and −0.03 (−0.23 to
   +0.23). "Always worse" scores 13 of 14.
2. **No other real-data definition passes either.** Bottom decile, the PubMed-inclusive forms, "at most 3 records", "no
   record", the union and the three reweightings give 6 to 9 of 14 in the leaked chain and 5 to 9 in the unseen chain;
   every Spearman interval includes zero; the definitions with 9 to 50 molecules are thin in every cell.
3. **The fame-masked construction meets the letter of the rule and is not accepted.** 10 of 14 and 7 of 14 (+2 in both
   chains), Spearman +0.19 (+0.01 to +0.27) and +0.14 (+0.02 to +0.23). Its two extra signs are the two contrasts whose
   loss the mask was built to reproduce in the poplow study; its correlation is not above the full sets' (+0.17 in
   both); with leaderboard noise both intervals include zero; and on the two earlier popularity submissions it has the
   wrong sign by −0.028 and −0.015 where the leaderboard gained +0.005 each. It was pre-registered as one of ten
   definitions, not as the primary.
4. **What fame does and does not explain.** With falling fame the in-group prior (contrast 13) turns negative in both
   chains (T3 about 0, decile −0.011 ± 0.007 and −0.007 ± 0.005, reweighted to 2 records −0.010 ± 0.005 and

   positive in the leaked chain at every real fame level and reaches −0.003 ± 0.003 only in the unseen chain's decile.
   The forward-weight contrasts 2 and 3 become wrong (+0.003 to +0.007), `FUSE_ALPHA` 0.3 becomes wrong in the unseen
   chain, v34a and the three join contrasts stay wrong. **Seven of the fourteen decidable contrasts have no fame
   mechanism that helps.**
5. **Obscure local answers are not hard lists.** v33b's composite is 0.57 to 0.58 on T3 and 0.56 to 0.58 on the decile

   −0.010 for the earlier step 0.15 → 0.25, which scored +0.005; with that point included the implied effect is +0.004
   (−0.001 to +0.009). **v47a is supported by a construction calibrated on the two points it explains, and by no real
   local stratum.**
