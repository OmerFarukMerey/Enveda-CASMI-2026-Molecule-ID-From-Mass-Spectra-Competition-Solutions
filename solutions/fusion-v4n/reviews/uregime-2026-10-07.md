# The U regime: does a base list that has seen nothing reproduce the leaderboard's signs? (2026-10-07)

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed, committed or uploaded; no `kaggle` command was run; no network was trained; the
visible `test.parquet` was not read; no NIST data. GPU: inference only through the existing runner (GLACIER at most 0.43 GB,
ICEBERG at most 1.54 GB, one at a time, shared mode; the other processes held 1.7 GB when it started). CPU: at most two
processes of mine plus the runner's child, below-normal priority (another agent ran six engine workers throughout). Scripts:
`scratch/uregime/` (git-ignored). Outputs: `D:\casmi-data\uregime\` (new; every other cache was read, not written).
**No file of the solution was changed; no validator script and no test were added** (section 4 says why). `INDEX.md` and
`EXPERIMENTS.md` not edited.

**Every number below was computed in this study** unless a file is named. MRR = MRR@25 on the metric key; "±" = one standard
error of a paired per-molecule (or per-list, where said) difference; (wins / losses). **No leaderboard estimate is given.**
"Seen" = the molecule's structure was in the model's training data. Leaderboard differences are single submissions on about
130 molecules; one answer between place 1 and 2 is 0.0038 (`reviews/determ-2026-10-07.md`).

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The U regime does not track the leaderboard. Verdict: no.** Signs right: **U 5 of 11**, U with the gate 4 of 11, the
   leaked regime of the calib review **7 of 11**. A rule that says "worse" every time scores 10 of 11, because ten of the
   eleven leaderboard differences are negative. Spearman of local against leaderboard differences: U +0.27 (molecule
   bootstrap, 95%: +0.12 to +0.42), leaked +0.28 (+0.08 to +0.41); with one answer of leaderboard noise per submission
   added, both intervals include zero (−0.01 to +0.65, −0.05 to +0.64). The pass rule was 9 of 11, an interval excluding
   zero, and more signs than the leaked regime. The count is not met, and neither the count nor the correlation separates U
   from the leaked regime.
2. **The working hypothesis is refuted in the form it was stated, and survives in a narrower form.**
   - Refuted: "take memorisation out of the base list and the base list becomes weak". The answer starts at rank 1 in 53%
     of all lists with the leaked public chain, 57% with v33b's chain and **51% with the fully unseen chain** (pool-answer
     lists: 73%, 75%, 72%). Switching the stage-A forward terms off gains in U as it does everywhere else: +0.010 ± 0.003
     (composite), wrong sign against the leaderboard's −0.011, in both sets.
   - Survives, at list level: the stage-A forward terms hurt when the answer starts first (−0.019 to −0.023 per list, 3 to 8
     helped against 52 to 69 hurt) and help on average when it starts at rank 4 to 25 (+0.002 to +0.035 over all lists, never beyond 2.5
     standard errors, and carried by answer-removed lists: on pool answers at rank 4 to 10 the sign is mixed). The local gain of v34a is the rank-1 lists; they are half of every local set whoever built the base list.
3. **Which contrasts break it.** Wrong in U beyond one standard error: v34a against v33b (+0.010 ± 0.003), N 200 against 100
   (+0.005 ± 0.002), join gate 0.8 against 0.7 (+0.011 ± 0.003), v22a against v13a (+0.008 ± 0.003). Wrong inside one
   standard error: forward weights 0.6 and 0.5 against 0.75. The first is wrong in every regime and set tried (U, gate
   variant, leaked, public chain; +0.009 to +0.015, two to five standard errors). The three join contrasts are wrong in
   every regime too: locally a wider or different join gains on answers the base list does not hold and costs pool answers
   nothing; on the leaderboard it lost 0.008 to 0.025.
4. **No re-weighting of the four list types gives all eleven signs** (linear programme: infeasible in U and in the leaked
   regime). With cells split by where the answer starts, a mixture exists in U, with a worst-case margin of 0.002 to 0.005
   and weights on cells of 58 to 144 lists; fitted to the eleven differences and held out one at a time it predicts worse
   than the mean of the other ten (RMS 0.018 to 0.022 against 0.011). **There is still no local score that orders our
   submissions**, the third study to find this (`calib`, `panel2`), now without the leaked base chain as the excuse.
5. **Best account of the hidden test** (section 5; an account, not a measurement): among the molecules we score at all, the
   answer is usually in the candidate lists but **not** a confident first of the base list, engine 2 holds it more often
   than the join alone does, and joined candidates are stronger competitors than they are locally. In local lists of that
   kind (answer at rank 4 to 10 of the base list, 189 lists) all five fusion-stage contrasts have the leaderboard's sign;
   in rank-1 lists one of five. Removing memorised models does not produce such lists; the local molecules are easy for a
   reason that is not the leak.
6. **Shelf candidates on these chains, as description only** (the regime failed, so there is no go / no-go): forward
   weights above 0.75 lose on pool answers at every dose and stage; `FUSE_KRR` 1 (v35a) gains, and **its gain has v34a's
   signature**: it comes from rank-1 lists (+0.023 ± 0.003) and "answer absent" lists and it loses where the answer starts
   at rank 2 to 25 (−0.015 ± 0.005). That is a warning for v35a, not a verdict.

## 4. Verdict, and why no validator was built

The condition was at least 9 of 11 signs and a correlation interval excluding zero, better than the leaked regime. U has 5
of 11 against 7; its interval excludes zero only while the leaderboard numbers are treated as exact, and the leaked regime's
does the same. **The U regime is not a validator, and packaging it as a go / no-go tool would give a number that has been
shown not to order our submissions.** Nothing was added under `tools/` or `prototypes/`. The replay itself
(`scratch/uregime/u_replay.py`, any config on any of eight base chains, resumable) stays as a measuring device with the
caches listed in section 8.

**5.3 The account.** For all eleven results to hold at once the hidden test, among the molecules that score at all, has to
look like this. Each line names the results that require it and the local stratum that shows the same sign.

1. **The answer is in the lists and is not a confident first of the base list.** Required by v34a (−0.011), 0.6 and 0.5
   (−0.007, −0.004) and `FUSE_ALPHA` 0.3 (−0.031): every stage that adds a second opinion to the base list is worth more
   than at home. Local counterpart: starts at rank 4 to 25. Locally these are 12% of the lists in U (9% + 3%); the
   leaderboard behaves as if they were the majority of what is scored. A level check that needs no fit: the public
   leaderboard is 0.44 where every local chain has 0.78 to 0.91 on pool answers.
2. **An interior optimum of the forward weights near 0.75 at both stages**: 1.0 loses (−0.006), 0.6 and 0.5 lose, zero in
   stage A loses. Local rank 4 to 10 lists show exactly this shape (1.0: −0.007, 0.6: −0.004, 0.5: −0.017, stage A off:
   −0.007), each inside two standard errors except 0.5.
3. **Engine 2 holds the answer far more often than the join alone does.** `FUSE_ALPHA` 0.3 gains +0.044 ± 0.005 on `dB`
   (answer reachable through the join only) and loses −0.047 ± 0.006 on `dA` (engine 2 has it); a loss of 0.031 on the
   leaderboard needs `dA`-like molecules to outweigh `dB`-like ones several times over, or engine 2's rank of the answer
   to be much better relative to the v1 list than it is locally. `dA` alone is the one single-type composite that follows
   the leaderboard in both regimes (7 of 11, Spearman +0.36 and +0.26).
4. **Joined candidates are stronger competitors than at home, and deep joined answers are rare.** Required by N 200
   (−0.025), gate 0.8 (−0.015) and v22a (−0.008) together with N 50 (−0.011) and no join (−0.022): the first hundred
   candidates below the gate bring answers, anything added beyond them displaces more answers than it brings. Locally the
   second hundred brings answers (+0.018 on "absent" lists) and displaces nothing. This is the part with no local
   counterpart in any chain, set or stratum, and it is the same finding as the calib review's exposure veto: a join
   variant that adds exposed lists has lost every time.
5. **The fitted ranker helps where the public one is unsure** (v33b +0.005): local rank 2 to 25 starts, +0.05 to +0.09.

What this says about the working hypothesis: the local sets are too easy, but **not because the public models memorised
them**. A chain that has seen nothing still puts 67% of class-2-like and 81% of class-1-like answers first. The local molecules are library and catalogue
compounds: the analog prior alone is 36% of our ranker's gain (`reviews/fsranker2-2026-10-07.md`), popularity is high, the
generator re-creates 40% of removed panel answers (`reviews/panel2-2026-10-05.md`). Those are properties of the molecules,
not of a model's training set, and no cross-fitting removes them. Not tested here: which of them is the one that matters.

**5.4 What would test the account** (not run): the same replay on molecules chosen for a hard start without using the
label, e.g. no analog above a Tanimoto threshold in the library and low popularity. The other agent's second hold-fold
sample (`D:\casmi-data\rankbank4\`, engine runs in progress during this study) could supply several hundred such lists
once engine 2, the PubChem lists and forward scores exist for it. The rank-4-to-10 stratum above is selected on the
label and cannot serve as a validator for anything that changes the base list.

## 7. Honesty and limits

- **The regime's construction was fixed first and was not changed.** Everything in sections 5 and 6 that is split by the
  answer's start rank is post hoc and is labelled so; five bins and twelve to twenty cells were looked at.
- **What U has still seen** is in the table of section 1: the forward models (both sets), engine 2's FPNets on `mx`, part
  of `fragnet`'s roster, and our ranker's fit holds 4/5 of the other molecules of the same two sets.
- **Contrast 10 is a bridge**, not a U contrast; tallies are given with and without it. Contrasts 4 and 10 in the leaked
  column come from chain P, the other nine from L.
- **The contrasts keep the submitted fusion config but not the submitted base chain**: on the leaderboard the join and
  forward-weight contrasts ran on the public ranker, v34a and `FUSE_ALPHA` on v33b's or v24a's chain. U replaces all of
  them by one chain. That is the regime's definition; it also means a U contrast is not an emulation of the submission.
- **Leaked column on the panel uses all 250 molecules and the in-sample engine 2; U uses 226 and the cross-fitted one.**
  The comparison of regimes is therefore not on identical molecules on the panel (it is on `mx`: 5 against 5 signs).
- **One seed** of the out-of-fold ranker ensemble (four boosters per fold); its reseeding noise (0.003 at ranker level,
  fsranker2 review) is not in the standard errors. The class-2 tie emulation of the public ranker uses one random draw.
- **Composite weights are equal by decree**; the hidden class mix is unknown. Secondary composites are in section 3.
- **Leaderboard differences are single submissions.** Three of the eleven are within one and a half answers. The
  bootstrap resamples molecules, not submissions; the last column of the summary table adds leaderboard noise.
- **The MetFrag cache differs from the calib study's** (fuller); `dA` of L moves by 0.004 (section 1).
- **Exposure counts** are after the fusion.
- **Not done**: the trainplan / hold-fold molecules (no engine-2, PubChem-fusion or forward caches); megayak's leak-free
  FPNets for engine 2 (not on this machine); a public-FPNet version of the N 50 / N 200 / gate / `ft_hn` lists (rows do not
  exist, so chain P covers six contrasts); the validator, its test, and any change to `tools/`; `INDEX.md`,
  `EXPERIMENTS.md`; a hard-start set chosen without the label (section 5.4).
- **Failed or corrected on the way**: the first report run stopped on a missing `tabulate` package (table written as
  text instead); a `sed` edit broke one line of the report script (repaired by hand); the first check of the MetFrag
  cache printed nothing because the variant was already stored (flag made to bypass the store); a `tail` call and a
  foreground wait were rejected by the shell tool and redone. No job was run twice; no process of mine is left running.
