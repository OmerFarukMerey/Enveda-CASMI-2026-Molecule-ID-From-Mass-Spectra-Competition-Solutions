## 0. Bottom line

**Does more training data improve our ranker on unseen lists beyond two standard errors? No.**

1. With 2,000 further unseen molecules (1,852 with usable lists; training set 6,687 → 13,092 lists, 3,350 → 5,202
   molecules) the refit **alone** moves the production case, joined class-2 lists below the gate (`c2J`, 823 lists), by
   **+0.0075 ± 0.0042** (81 / 65; 1.8 standard errors), nitrogen-free +0.015 ± 0.012, class-2 lists without the join
   +0.001 ± 0.002, joined answer-removed lists +0.002 ± 0.006, class-1 lists below the gate +0.006 ± 0.011.
2. **Inside the rank average with the public ranker, which is what v33b ships, there is nothing: `c2J` +0.0003 ± 0.0034**
   (64 / 63), nitrogen-free −0.008 ± 0.008, panel `c2J` −0.015 ± 0.009 (12 / 20), plain class-2 lists −0.001 ± 0.002
   (nitrogen-free −0.004 ± 0.004), answer-removed +0.002 ± 0.004, class-1 −0.003 ± 0.009.
3. The curve is not monotone inside the average (+500: −0.003, +1,000: +0.002, +2,000: 0.000 on `c2J`), i.e. it is the
   reseeding noise of two-seed fits (0.003 per single fit, `fsranker2`). The +0.005 ± 0.002 per doubling that the
   sub-sampling curve of `fsranker2` promised inside the average did not appear when the data were actually doubled.
4. One thing did move in the right direction: `c2J` lists with a joined row first fall from 66 to 59.5 (alone) and from
   52.5 to 42.5 (in the average) of 823. It does not show in the MRR.
5. Consequently, as instructed by the coordinator mid-task: **no ranker was exported, `casmi26-ours-ranker-fs2` does not
   exist, the preset v37a was not kept, no full replay was run, the further batches were not started, the queue is
   stopped.** The folder behind 0.442 was not touched.
6. **Not tested: the sampling.** The batch with a nitrogen-free, isomer-rich half (`hold3`, 1,000 molecules) was drawn but
   never run. hold2 itself is richer in such molecules than the old set (section 1), and the nitrogen-free test lists
   show no gain inside the average, so there is no sign that a targeted batch would change the answer; it is not proven.

Readings.
- **Alone, the refit is 1.8 standard errors up on the one group where the fsranker2 curve expected a gain, and the gain
  comes with the joined lists** (plain lists only: −0.001 ± 0.003; both: +0.0075). It is the size the sub-sampling curve
  predicted (+0.009 per doubling), so that curve was right about the ranker alone. It is still below two standard
  errors, and the reseeding noise of a two-seed mean (about 0.002) is not in the error.
- **In the average the gain is absent.** The public half has seen every molecule; where the fitted ranker improves, the
  rank average does not pass it on (the two orders already agree there, or the public order overrides it). The one
  cell beyond 1.5 standard errors points down (panel `c2J` −0.015 ± 0.009, `c2N` nitrogen-free −0.004 ± 0.004).
- **Nitrogen-free and isomer-rich lists, the reason for the targeted sampling, do not respond inside the average**
  (−0.008 ± 0.008; > 20 isomers 0.000 ± 0.004), although hold2 added 770 nitrogen-free molecules (555 with ≥ 20 pool
  isomers) to 1,085. The error anatomy of `fsranker2` said the ranker has no input that separates such isomers; more
  rows of the same inputs do not create one.

## 3. Why nothing was exported, and what was not done

- The coordinator's rule for this task, received before the refit was evaluated: export and wire only if more data
  improves the ranker beyond two standard errors on the held-out lists. It does not (largest cell 1.8, and zero in the
  shipped design). **No `casmi26-ours-ranker-fs2`, no preset `v37a`, no full replay with forward scores, no GPU use.**
- A preset `configs/v37a-v33b-ranker-fs2.json` and a test for it were written while the engine ran and **removed again**
  (a preset pointing at a dataset that does not exist is a trap). `RANKER_FS_DS` is already wired
  (`nb/05_v1_engine.py`, `tools/build_notebook.py`); a build with that preset attached
  `huseyinemreaksoy/casmi26-ours-ranker-fs2` as the 16th dataset (10 cells), so the day a refit is worth shipping only
  the preset and `r4_train.py` / `r4_readme.py` (written, never run) are needed.
- All thirteen `tests/test_*.py` passed (exit code 0 each) when run directly with the venv python at 12:00 on the tree as it is now.
- The replay machinery was checked only: `r4_replay.py` reproduces the `pub` chain of `fsranker2` on the mixed regime
  exactly (0.8475 / 0.7568 / 0.5453 / 0.3514) and gives 0.8465 / 0.7616 / 0.5506 / 0.3547 for v33b with two seeds
  (5 candidates without forward scores; that run was deleted).
- **Not done**: the replay of the refit; hold3 and later batches; a refit with only the nitrogen-free or only the
  natural-product part of hold2; more than two seeds per point; `EXPERIMENTS.md` / `INDEX.md`.
- **Failed or corrected on the way**: two shell here-documents broke on quoting (files written with the file tool); the
  first supervisor started a second fit worker again and again for a job another worker held (harmless; fixed); joined
  engine jobs that follow the PubChem lists were throttled so that slots stood idle for part of the batch (the PubChem
  step was the bottleneck anyway); the supervisor ended by design when the batch was finished and had to be started
  again for the fits; D: has 12 GB free, which would have capped the growth near 10,000 molecules.

## 4. Recommendation

Stop buying training molecules for this ranker. Its limit on unseen molecules is not the number of lists but the
inputs (isomer order in nitrogen-free molecules) and the rank average that dilutes it; v33b's ranker files stay.
Should the fitted ranker ever be used alone below the gate (v33a's design, which lost `dB` and `c1` on the mixed regime
in `fsranker2`), the refit with these 2,000 molecules is the better one by about +0.008 on joined class-2 lists and has
6 fewer exposed lists per 823; that alone does not justify a submission.
