## 0. Bottom line

- **No collision-energy miscalibration worth fixing was found.** On the development folds, the truth's similarity to its own
  prediction peaks at **e_eff ≈ 0.8 × stated eV** (MEAN and MERGE, both folds; +0.01 to +0.02 similarity over identity). Isomer
  order is best with **MERGE(a 0.9, b +5)**, meaning stepped spectra are matched against merged predictions at roughly their own
  energies. That is msfwd's R3 rule again, not a rescaling. A fixed energy (FIX) is worse than the stated energies (FIX ≥ 60 eV
  collapses). The stated timsTOF eV therefore carry real information and are close to GLACIER's scale. The Orbitrap token is
  about equal to the QTOF token.
- **Cleaning:** the development folds selected top-k 100 and precursor removal in both folds. The other steps were selected in
  only one fold or not at all: noise floor 5%, sqrt intensity and top-50 (fold 0); merging split centroids, removing isotope
  peaks, cutting low m/z, merging duplicate spectra and the ABOVE-precursor cut (never).
- **All three pre-registered rules fail on the cross-fitted replay** (T-CE, T-CL, T-BOTH). On the panel's pool-answer lists,
  the effects are +0.0009 (13 / 8), −0.0070 (12 / 16) and −0.0026 (12 / 11). Composite CIs span 0 in every arm, and the size
  criterion (+0.0133) is missed by an order of magnitude.
- **The isomer gains seen on the development folds do not generalise.** Development isomer MRR rose by +0.03 to +0.08. On the
  held-out fold the gains are +0.008 ± 0.018 (T-CE), +0.006 ± 0.013 (T-CL) and +0.017 ± 0.017 (T-BOTH). None of this reaches
  the final lists.
- **No patch.** This is consistent with joint1 and msfwd: the timsTOF "domain gap" for GLACIER is not in the CE scale or the
  peak hygiene that the runner already applies.

## 1. Setup [V]

- **Molecules.** 230 molecules in stack1's GLACIER branch with timsTOF [M+H]+ spectra (msfwd items): panel 194 (8,123
  candidates, 515 spectra) and Enveda-180 36 (1,513, 122). No molecule mixes instruments. Energies on the panel: [40], [60],
  [20 50], [35], [80], [20], [20 40 60 80], [20 40 60], [0 20 40 60 80].
- **Predictions.** GLACIER (package runner unchanged; wrapper `tims_runner.py`) predicted every candidate on a CE grid: QTOF
  token at 5-120 eV in 5 eV steps (stack1's token for timsTOF) and the Orbitrap token at 10-100 eV in 10 eV steps. That is
  about 327k predictions, 0 failed. Runs:
  - GPU: 23:09-00:35 through `guard_run.py` with `GPU_LOCK` held and released. A game was running, so duty was 0.25; ≤ 0.28 GB;
    225-285 predictions/s while active.
  - CPU (low priority, 3 threads): the mixed set and 85 panel molecules, run while audit1017 held the lock.
- **Base B0.** The identity rule computed from these predictions, which equals the runner's own definition. Per-spectrum
  agreement with msfwd's similarities: mean |Δ| 0.003 (median 3e-8 for GPU-predicted panel molecules; CPU-predicted ones carry
  float noise). B0 vs msfwd's B': 969 / 1,552 lists identical, composite +0.0006 / +0.0014, 14 / 13 wins / losses. That is the
  noise floor of this harness.
- **Replay.** Cell 34's GLACIER branch was rebuilt for 1,340 / 1,340 lists, all of which reproduced F; 0 candidates fell back
  to a captured score.
- **Folds.** Split by md5(truth identity) mod 2. Fold 0: 126 molecules; fold 1: 102.
- **Development objective.** Isomer RR of the truth inside its formula group, using the GLACIER column (mean over timsTOF
  spectra).
- **Selection** (pre-registered, threshold +0.005):
  - T-CE: the best CE rule (430 rules).
  - T-CL: greedy cleaning over 8 families, at most 4 steps.
  - T-BOTH: T-CE's rule followed by greedy cleaning.

Readings [I]:

- The rules re-order hundreds of lists but win about as often as they lose. That is the signature of the forward score's noise
  floor: B0 vs B' alone moves 380 lists.
- Whatever isomer signal survives is diluted by cell 34's fusion with engine 2 and by E2R2.
- The intensity transforms (power 0.25, entropy weighting off) were clearly harmful on the dev folds. The runner's
  entropy-weighted, power-1 similarity is already a good choice for timsTOF.
- Leakage: GLACIER saw MassSpecGym structures, so the isomer signal everywhere is optimistic. Even so, none of it survives.

## 4. Runtime

The T-CE rules are MERGE rules. They need one extra prediction per stepped component, as msfwd's R3 did: about +21% GLACIER
predictions, ≈ +2.5 min on a T4 at 83.9 predictions/s for ~430 molecules [C msfwd]. The Orbitrap token and MEAN remaps cost
nothing. Cleaning runs on the CPU only (seconds). Runtime was never the constraint.

## 5. Recommendation

Do not build a patch:

- CE rescaling: the best similarity is at ≈ 0.8× the stated eV, but that does not improve isomer order out of fold.
- Stepped-spectrum merging: already tested as msfwd R3 and neutral.
- timsTOF cleaning: dev-fold gains vanish on the held-out fold and hurt the panel's pool lists (T-CL −0.007).

Closing the domain gap would need a forward model fine-tuned on timsTOF spectra from permitted data, scored on held-out Enveda
molecules. This study shows that re-parameterising the existing checkpoint at inference time does not do it.
