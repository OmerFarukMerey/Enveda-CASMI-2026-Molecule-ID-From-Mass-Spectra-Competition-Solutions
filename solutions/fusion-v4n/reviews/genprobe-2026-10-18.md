## 4. Reading [I]

On database-answer molecules the rule fires rarely (3%) and its candidate was never right; its rank-3 floor costs about 0.0003 per
molecule there. Its value rests entirely on how many hidden molecules are class-N-like with the answer generated in engine 1's top 3
but not already in stack1's top 3: that is what G-current / G-first measure.
