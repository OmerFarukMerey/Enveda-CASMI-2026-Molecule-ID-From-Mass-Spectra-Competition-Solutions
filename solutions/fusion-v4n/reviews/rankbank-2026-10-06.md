# A ranker fitted to the improved fingerprint bank (2026-10-06)

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed, committed, uploaded or downloaded; no `kaggle` command was run; no
fingerprint network was trained; the visible `test.parquet` was not read; no NIST data. No tracked file was changed
except this review (`INDEX.md` not regenerated: other agents were adding files). GPU: one inference job (`ft_hn`
logits for the 3,100 hold-out molecules, 44 s, 250 MiB). CPU: three v1-engine workers plus one other process at
below-normal priority (four engine workers and no other process for 12 minutes); LightGBM with one thread.
Scripts: `scratch/rankbank/` (git-ignored). Outputs: `D:\casmi-data\rankbank\` (3.6 GB, all new; every other cache
was read, not written).

**Every number below was computed in this study** unless a file is named. MRR = MRR@25 of the answer in the
key-deduplicated candidate list ordered by the ranker (**ranker level: before popularity, forward models, engine 2
and the fusion**); "±" = one standard error of a paired per-molecule difference; (wins / losses) in brackets.
**No leaderboard estimate is given.** "Seen" = the molecule's structure was in the model's training data.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The question of the task has a clean negative answer: (d) − (c) is zero.** A ranker fitted to the fold-safe +
   `ft_hn` bank is not better than a ranker fitted to the fold-safe bank, on molecules neither has seen:
   +0.001 ± 0.002 (hold-out, grouped CV, 3,100 class-2 lists), −0.000 ± 0.005 (the 380 regime molecules, outside the
   fit), +0.000 ± 0.010 (Enveda panel, 250). Nitrogen-free molecules: +0.008 ± 0.005, −0.008 ± 0.012, +0.003 ± 0.014.
   No isomer-count bin differs beyond one standard error in any set. The largest cell (nitrogen-free, CV, 1.6
   standard errors) is the size of the reseeding noise of the fit (−0.002 ± 0.001 between two seed pairs of the same
   ranker).
2. **The embedding is better at its own level and the gain stops at the ranker**: the bank's `f.z` alone gains
   +0.018 ± 0.002 (hold-out), +0.010 ± 0.006 (regime), +0.033 ± 0.009 (panel) over the fold-safe model's. Swapping
   the bank under the public ranker gives +0.001 ± 0.001 / −0.002 ± 0.004 / 0.000 ± 0.004 (the fpbank review's
   finding, reproduced); fitting the ranker to the bank gives the numbers of point 1.
3. **Why: the ranker hardly uses the fingerprint.** A ranker fitted without any of the 32 fingerprint-derived inputs
   (of 160) loses 0.013 ± 0.003 on the hold-out (nitrogen-free 0.031 ± 0.006), 0.023 ± 0.008 on the regime and
   0.017 ± 0.013 on the panel. That is the whole budget of this route. An embedding that is 0.02 to 0.03 better at
   its own level moves a fraction of it.
4. **(c) − (b) is large and not new**: fitting the ranker to fold-safe features gains +0.038 ± 0.003 (hold-out),
   +0.049 ± 0.010 (regime), +0.057 ± 0.015 (panel) over the public ranker with order-free ties on the same features,
   and +0.083 / +0.105 / +0.187 over the public ranker in the order a hidden class-2 answer has. New here: with
   fold-safe features it **does** transfer to the Enveda panel (the ranker study had tested it there on features of
   the public models, −0.009 ± 0.018). This is the gain the leaderboard did not pay for (0.405 to 0.408 against
   0.409 without the join, 0.417 against 0.431 with it, `EXPERIMENTS.md`).
5. **Join configuration** (100 joined rows, gate 0.7; rankers fitted with joined lists and five injection
   features): (d) − (c) is −0.011 ± 0.009 (regime `c2`) and 0.000 ± 0.014 (panel `c2`), `c1` +0.002 ± 0.004 and
   +0.002 ± 0.006. Exposure (pool-answer lists with a joined row first) is equal in (c) and (d): 30 and 30 of 380
   (`c2`, regime; the public ranker 35), 14 and 14 of 250 (panel; public 12), `c1` 6 / 6 and 5 / 6.
6. **The c1 / c2 trade**: a fitted ranker used on every list costs class-1 lists on the panel 0.133 ± 0.019 against
   the public ranker on the same fold-safe features and 0.241 ± 0.021 against the public bank + public ranker.
   Behind the gate (public ranker at `lib_max` ≥ 0.7) the cost is −0.008 ± 0.006.
7. **Nothing was exported, wired or replayed.** The condition of the task ((d) − (c) beyond two standard errors on
   the unseen molecules) is not met in any set. No dataset folder, no `RANKER` / `FP_BANK` value, no preset
   `v31a`, no full replay. The "better embeddings" route is closed for this pipeline at the ranker stage.

## 4. What could ship: the trade, and the decision

- **What a fold-safe ranker throws away, on the type where the public models' memory is legitimate (`c1`: the
  molecule is in train).** Panel, the cleanest emulation (model A has seen the structures through other libraries,
  not the panel's spectra): the public bank + public ranker scores 0.954 on class-1 lists; the fitted rankers 0.713
  and 0.715 (**−0.241 ± 0.021**, 102 losses to 5 wins; nitrogen-free −0.329 ± 0.032); in the join configuration
  0.937 against 0.669 / 0.695. Three things are lost together: the public FPNets' knowledge of the structure
  (0.954 → 0.846 with the public ranker kept), the pool-order prior, which is correct for class 1, and the public
  ranker's fit to these molecules (0.846 → 0.713). The hold-out does not show this (+0.014 ± 0.005 for the fitted
  ranker): its class-1 lists have library scores near 1 (median `lib_max` 0.99 against 0.86 on the panel,
  `reviews/ranker-2026-10-02.md`).
- **`c2`**: the fitted rankers are above the public chain in the order a hidden class-2 answer has (panel

## 5. Honesty and limits

- **Ranker level only.** Every table is before popularity, forward models, engine 2 and the fusion. The ranker
  study measured that the fusion absorbs base-list gains four times larger than anything in the (d) − (c) row; a
  zero before the fusion cannot become a gain after it.
- **Who has seen what** is in section 1. In (b) the public ranker has seen every test molecule; it still loses to
  (c), so (c) − (b) is a lower bound of what the fit is worth on these features, not an inflated one.
- **The answer is always a pool row** (or, in the join's training lists, an injected row): the defect of every local
  set; it does not bear on (d) − (c), which compares two arms on the same lists.
- **One definition of (d)** was run: the 0.5 / 0.5 temperature-matched bank with `ft_hn` in two of the three
  `model_views` slots. Not run: `ft_hn` alone, other weights, the bank change without the `model_views` change (its
  test rows exist in `D:\casmi-data\fpbank\join`, its training rows do not).
- **No control arm with `ft_ctrl`** (it would separate "a second model in the views" from "a better model"). It is
  only needed to attribute a positive difference; there is none. The scripts take `--arm k` for it.
- **Reseeding noise** of the fits (section 3.3) is not in the standard errors; two seeds per fold in the CV, four in
  the split models.
- **Hyper-parameters and features were not tuned**: the public ranker's own, as the brief asked. The 250-feature set
  of our packaged ranker was not run for (d); megayak's rows were not used (other layout).
- **The hold-out is library chemistry** (16% timsTOF, all synthetic `enveda-180`); the panel is the test's instrument
  with famous answers. The three sets agree on (d) − (c), which is what matters here.
- **(a) on the regime does not exist** (no public-model run of the 380); (a) on the hold-out is 100 molecules.
- **The exposure counts are at ranker level** (first row of the engine list), not after the fusion as in the
  replays of the other reviews; they are comparable between the arms of this review only.
- **Failed or corrected on the way**: three shell here-documents broke on quoting (files were then written with
  the file tool or a script file); a queue edit turned `mx` into `m` (fixed before any worker used it); the
  feature build for (d) crashed once on a run directory without chunks (rerun with explicit sets); the fourth
  engine worker was stopped once to free a process slot and its molecules were rerun; the first class-2-only
  rankers were fitted before the class-1 failure was seen (kept as a negative control, section 2); twice the
  agent's run was interrupted by an API error and resumed from the logs, no job was started twice.
- **Not done**: the solution's tests (no file of the solution changed); `EXPERIMENTS.md` / `INDEX.md` entries.
