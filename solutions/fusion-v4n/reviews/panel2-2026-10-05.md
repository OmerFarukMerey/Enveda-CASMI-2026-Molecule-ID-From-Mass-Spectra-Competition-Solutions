# Panel 2: a validation regime from Enveda timsTOF spectra only, and whether it tracks the leaderboard (2026-10-05)

Offline only. Nothing was submitted, pushed or committed; no `kaggle` command was run; nothing was downloaded; no model
was trained; the visible `test.parquet` was not read; no NIST data; no file of the solution tree was changed except
this review (`INDEX.md` was not regenerated, `EXPERIMENTS.md` has no entry). GPU: inference only through the existing
runner, one process at a time (GLACIER at most 0.44 GB, ICEBERG at most 1.9 GB, `gpushare` duty 0.8). CPU: at most
three v1-engine workers plus one other process of mine, below-normal priority. Scripts: `scratch/panel2/`
(git-ignored). Outputs: `D:\casmi-data\panel2\` (1.4 GB, all new; other caches were read or copied, never written).

**Every number below was computed in this study** unless a file is named. MRR = MRR@25 on the metric key; "±" = one
standard error of a paired per-molecule difference; (wins / losses) in brackets. **No leaderboard estimate is given
for anything.**

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The regime exists and is complete for what it can hold**: all 250 structures of `enveda-np-examples` (1,184
   timsTOF spectra, collision energy on every spectrum, the test's ten adducts), each run as `c1`, `c2`, `dA` and `dB`.
   All eleven configurations were replayed with real ICEBERG / GLACIER scores (36,487 candidates; GLACIER scored all,
   ICEBERG all but 2; no candidate was left unattempted, nothing was filled). v15b and v15a are better reproduced than
   in the mixed regime (v15b: all 117 changed lists re-run, with `c1`; v15a: still the stand-in ranker).
2. **It is not a better predictor of the leaderboard than the mixed regime.** With the leaked engine-2 arm (the mixed
   regime's convention) it is worse: best class mix 34 of 55 pairs (mixed regime 42), leave-one-out rank error 4.77
   (3.14; a constant guess 2.73). With the leak-free engine-2 arm it is equal: 42 of 55 at one mix, leave-one-out 2.86
   and 38.2 of 55 held-out pairs (mixed: 3.14 and 36.5). Neither beats the constant guess, the two arms of the same
   panel disagree with each other, and every difference between join variants on pool answers is under one to two
   standard errors (0.002 to 0.009).
3. **The exposure veto does not carry over.** Scaled to 250 lists (3.3 more `c2` lists topped by a joined row) it
   fires on v15a only (+7; a loser). It misses v14a and v22a, which the mixed regime's veto caught: on the panel their
   exposure is *below* v13a's (2 and 1 lists against 4). The panel has too few exposed lists to carry a veto: 4 of 250
   `c2` lists for v13a with the fold-safe models (25 of 380 in the mixed regime).
4. **Why it fails where it should help**: the panel's answers are famous natural products with a median of 170
   spectra in other libraries. Pool answers are easy (`c2` 0.82, `c1` 0.91 against 0.73 and 0.82 in the mixed
   regime), the analog generator re-creates 40% of the removed answers, and the forward models have very probably
   seen 245 of the 250 structures. Same instrument and chemistry as the test, opposite difficulty.
5. **Leak size, measured**: with the public FPNet A and public `fe` models instead of the fold-safe model every type
   is higher (`c2` +0.001 to +0.032, `dA` +0.07 to +0.10, `dB` +0.06 to +0.10) and **the sign of the join on pool
   answers flips**: `c2` v13a − v4b is +0.005 ± 0.009 fold-safe and −0.020 ± 0.009 with the public models, with 11
   instead of 4 `c2` lists topped by a joined row. The public-model arm is the only one in which the join costs pool
   answers the way the leaderboard history says it does.
6. **Today's candidates on the panel** (section 6): forward weights 0.75 gain on pool answers in every arm (`c1`
   +0.007 to +0.027, `c2` +0.008 to +0.018, beyond two standard errors in most arms) and cost `dB` 0.004 to 0.009
   (within two); exposure does not rise. Weights 0.5 gain more on pool answers and lose `dA` / `dB` beyond two standard
   errors. `JOIN_FWD_A` 0.25 does not reproduce its mixed-regime pass: no `c2` gain with the fold-safe models, a `dB`
   cost of 0.007 to 0.024. `ENG_EXTRA_FPNET`: nothing beyond two standard errors after the fusion, and the soup was
   tuned on these very spectra.

**Verdict: equal at best, worse in the comparable arm.**

- In the arm built like the mixed regime (`ctrl | pub`) the panel is worse on every line: no single type above 32
  pairs, a best mix of 34, a leave-one-out error far above the constant guess.
- With engine 2's fingerprint features off, `c2` MRR alone reaches 38 pairs and the best mix 42 with a leave-one-out
  result marginally better than the mixed regime's (2.86 against 3.14; 38.2 against 36.5 pairs). That is still not
  better than the constant guess, it is one arm of two, it was not predicted, and it rests on `c2` differences of
  −0.006 to +0.007 with standard errors of 0.003 to 0.009. It is not a score to decide submissions with.
- **Exposure is anti-correlated with the leaderboard on the panel** (−0.34 to −0.60), because here the join variants
  with the fewest exposed lists are v4b, v19a, v14a and v22a, four losers.
- The go / no-go composite calls v12a, v14b, v14c and v15b "better" than v13a; none is.
