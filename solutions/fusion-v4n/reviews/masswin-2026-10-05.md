## 0. Bottom line

1. **There is almost nothing to prune where it would count.** On the panel 92% of the first 25 rows of the v1 list have
   the answer's formula. A corrected ±5 ppm window removes 0.39 of the first 25 rows (1.6%) and loses the answer for 2 of
   250 molecules (0.8%); of the 3.9 rows ranked above a pool answer, 0.024 per list (6 rows, all in one of 247 lists)
   lie outside ±5 ppm and none outside ±7. ±4 ppm: 0.77 rows (3.1%), 6 answers (2.4%). ±3 ppm: 1.94 rows, 16 answers
   (6.4%). Deeper in the lists a window removes more and still nothing that is scored: 20% of the 98 joined rows and
   15% of the pool rows are outside ±5 ppm.
2. **Formula evidence without a model gives the same picture.** Cutting the first 25 rows to the first row's formula
   removes 2.2 rows per list on the panel and drops the answer's formula for 2.0% (`c2`) to 4.4% (`dB`) of the lists; to
   the majority formula 1.7 rows and 3.6%.
3. **The data has no MS1 and no isotope-pattern column** (`train.parquet`, `test.parquet`: MS/MS peaks, base peak
   intensity, adduct, mode, instrument, precursor m/z, collision energy). 47% of the Enveda MS/MS spectra do hold a
   peak at the precursor + 1.0034 (median 0.17 of the precursor peak): a weak carbon-count signal exists. It was not
   modelled, because it can only separate formulas and the decoys are isomers.
4. **Several adducts do pin the mass.** With two or more adducts none of 158 panel molecules is outside ±4 ppm (one
   adduct: 6 of 92). A per-molecule tolerance (`clip(2 + spread, 3, 5)` with two or more adducts, else 5; chosen on
   enveda-180) has a mean of 4.3 ppm on the panel and loses the same 2 answers as ±5.
5. **End to end no pruning variant passes** (one type beyond +2 SE, no type beyond −1 SE, no exposure increase), on
   either set, hard or soft, fixed or per-molecule. On the panel every pruning difference is within 1.1 SE. On the
   regime the variants that touch the pool or engine 2 lose on `c2` (−0.006 to −0.011) and raise the exposure (25 → 26
   to 32 lists topped by a joined row).
6. **The one thing that moves is not pruning.** The existing flag `PC_PPM` / `MASS_OFFSET_BY_ADDUCT` (back-filled channel
   list, preset `v21a`) gains `dB` +0.027 ± 0.011 on the regime, most of it on the 39 timsTOF (enveda-180) molecules
   (+0.133 ± 0.051, 7 / 0; other instruments +0.013 ± 0.010): the coverage effect of the masscal review. On the panel it does not reproduce: `dB` +0.007 ±
   0.006, `c2` −0.009 ± 0.005 (2 / 10), pool-answer lists topped by a joined row 14 → 19. It fails the test on both sets.
7. **Nothing was wired**: no `PPM_WIN`, `POOL_MASS_OFFSET` or `ENG_POOL_PPM` key, no test, no
   `configs/v27a-v13a-masswin.json`.

## 4. Verdict

- **No variant removes decoys that matter.** What a mass window or a formula cut can remove is already at the bottom
  of the lists; the rows above the answer are isomers (panel: 0.6% of them outside ±5 ppm).
- **No pruning variant passes the test, so nothing was wired.** `nb/00_config.py`, `nb/05`, `nb/06`, engine 2 and the tests
  are as they were; `configs/v27a-v13a-masswin.json` was not written; the `tests/test_*.py` were not run, since no code
  changed.
- **The flag that already exists** (`PC_PPM=5`, `MASS_OFFSET_BY_ADDUCT='enveda'`, preset `v21a`) is a coverage lever for
  molecules with very large PubChem windows. It raises the exposure on the panel (14 → 19) and on the regime (25 → 28,
  `c1` 6 → 9). By the rule of the 5 October log (no join variant with exposure above v13a's) it is not a candidate.
- **Pool and engine-2 windows stay at 10 ppm**: `b5` `c2` −0.008 ± 0.004 (5 / 20) with 25 → 32 lists topped by a joined
  row; `c5` `c2` −0.006 ± 0.004; `d5` `c2` −0.011 ± 0.006. On the panel `b5`, `d5` and `e` are noise around zero.

## 5. Honesty and limits

- **The panel has no engine 2.** Its replay stops after the slot merge. Variant (c) and the engine-2 part of (d), (e)
  and `fall2` exist on the regime only, where 40 molecules are timsTOF. The panel's selection model is `ft_ctrl`, a
  stand-in for the fold-safe model.
- **The panel's molecules are training structures of the public models** (forward models, engine-2 is absent, the
  public ranker very likely); levels are
  optimistic, paired differences less so. The regime's leaks are those listed in the fwd-replay review.
- **Scope**: 4 ppm, `e` and `tc` on the regime ran on the timsTOF molecules only. Under a plain 5 ppm window the
  other-instrument molecules lose 9 answers in engine 2 and 3 to 4 elsewhere to instrument error the test does not
  have; section 3.4 is therefore pessimistic for the hidden test, and section 3.3 rests on 39 to 40 molecules.
- **Approximations**: (i) the soft variants are post-hoc re-orderings of ranked lists, by construction; (ii) the
  PubChem slot list of `aP` / `d` / `e` is the cached 200-row list filtered, not a channel re-run; (iii) the `aF` lists
  come from cached windows, checked against one real run at 5 ppm and not at 4; (iv) MetFrag scores do not exist for
  rows that are new to a list (they act only in molecules without forward coverage); (v) `best_pool_fz` of the
  PubChem dict is the 10 ppm pool's in every variant; (vi) the engine has two states for a few molecules
  (`EXPERIMENTS.md`, ranker study), which adds noise to every pair of engine runs.
- **Exposure counts have no error bar**; "exposure up" is one or two lists for several rows.
- **The per-molecule rule**: its panel loss depends on how the spread is computed (2 or 4 of 250).
- **Not done**: engine 2 on the panel; 3 ppm and 7 ppm engine runs; a formula cut end to end (section 1.5 already
  shows 2 to 4% of the answers lost for 2 rows removed); an isotope-ratio model; `aF` together with `PC_N1=20000`.
- **Machine and failures.** The first session ended mid-run and every background job was killed; all runs were
  resumable and were restarted. Three times a process-cleanup command of mine matched its own shell and killed it
  (the intended targets were also stopped; no process of another agent matched). The queue shells hit the two-hour
  background limit once; the queues kept running and finished. For about 70 minutes four engine workers ran. Several
  of my "wait" commands were stopped by the tool's time limits. No engine error, no forward-runner failure.
