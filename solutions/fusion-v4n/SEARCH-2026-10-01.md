# Fusion-knob search around v2 (2026-10-01)

Offline re-fusion of cached stage outputs only: nothing was submitted, no notebook was run. Harness:
`tools/regime_eval.py`. Raw results, specs and the scripts that generated them: `runs/search/` (git-ignored).
Reference everywhere is **v2** (`POP_MU 0.15, FRAG_LAM 0.5, FRAG_MODE "gap"`, LB 0.404). Every config has `TOPN 60`.

**Bottom line.** The harness does **not** reproduce the leaderboard order, so nothing below is a prediction of the LB.
The one finding that rests on unbiased data is that v2 loses 18 of 400 exact library hits to the ICE/GLACIER re-rank
and that a library gate (`ICE_LAM_LIB` / `GL_LAM_LIB`) recovers 16 to 18 of them at no measurable cost. Everything that
looks better in the class-2 sim (more popularity, more ICE/GL weight) rests on evidence that is biased in a known direction.

## 1. The harness

`tools/regime_eval.py <spec.json>` evaluates each named config in every regime, paired against the reference, and
writes one table (`.json`, `.md`, per-molecule reciprocal ranks as CSV).

| Regime | Run | Mode | What it measures | Known bias |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `class2` | `runs/val1` (250 held-out natural products, library purged) | `sim`: same-formula candidates of the base list shuffled, 6 seeds (12 for the finalists) | can ICE / GLACIER / frag / popularity find the truth when the ranker carries no isomer information | over-rewards every non-ranker term by construction (the ranker is noise inside a formula group); over-rewards popularity (see 3.1); engine-2 has the truth in only 7.6% of lists |
| `library` | `runs/vis` (400 visible-test molecules, lib_max 0.988 to 1.0) | `plain` | "do no harm to exact library hits" | the v1 ranker is right on all 400, so less re-scoring always wins; easier than real class-1 |
| `c2_leaked` (diagnostic) | `runs/val1` | `plain` | the opposite bracket of `class2`: the ranker knows 94% of the answers | every re-scorer looks harmful; channel-balance knobs are meaningless |
| `lib_nogate` (diagnostic) | `runs/vis` with `LIB_TAU 1.01`, `FUSE_SKIP_LIB null` forced | `plain` | a library hit on which the gate does **not** fire | as `library` |

Composite = share × Δ_library + (1 − share) × Δ_class2 for class-1 shares 0.2, 0.35, 0.5. Verdict column: `sim+` =
library Δ ≥ −1 SE and class-2 Δ > +2 SE; `lib+` = library Δ > +2 SE and class-2 Δ ≥ −1 SE. "top-1 Δ" = molecules whose
top-1 answer differs from v2 (mean over seeds in the sim). SE is the paired standard error over molecules.

A third regime is one more entry in the spec, for example `"class1": {"run": "runs/valc1", "mode": "plain"}` plus
`"composite": {"class1": "class1", "class2": "class2"}`. A regime can force its own overrides (`"set"`).
Speed: the first call ever takes about 11 minutes (tautomer keys for the candidate SMILES, then cached in
`runs/search/cache/keys.json`); after that 1 to 3 s per new config (6 seeds, four regimes) and 0 s for a config already in
`runs/search/cache/results.sqlite` (62 configs, two regimes: 175 s the first time, under 1 s cached).

What the table says, knob by knob:

- **POP_MU**: monotone in the sim (0 → -0.041, 0.25 → +0.022, 0.4 → +0.047), flat on library hits (within ±0.002).
  **POP_LIB_OFF** never helps: it costs 3 library hits (-0.0037), the ones popularity rescues from ICE/GL.
- **ICE_LAM / GL_LAM**: the two regimes pull in opposite directions. More weight wins the sim (2/2: +0.020 ± 0.006)
  and loses library hits (2/2: -0.026, 0 wins / 29 losses; the same in `lib_nogate`). Less weight does the reverse
  (0.5/0.5: library +0.020, sim -0.022). GL 1.5 alone is the mildest: sim +0.007 ± 0.003, library -0.005 ± 0.003.
- **ICE_LAM_LIB / GL_LAM_LIB**: +0.008 to +0.0255 on library hits. Their sim effect comes from 8 molecules only (see 4.1).
- **FRAG_LAM (gap)**: 0 → -0.003 ± 0.002, 1.0 → +0.002 ± 0.002 in the sim and -0.008 ± 0.003 in `c2_leaked`. No case
  for moving it. **FRAG_LIB_OFF** changes nothing at 0.25 and 0.5 and one molecule at 1.0.
- **FUSE_SKIP_LIB 0.9**: library +0.019 (16/0). Formally also `sim+` (+0.005 ± 0.002, 8/0), but those 8 wins are the 8
  val1 molecules above the gate, where engine-2 does not have the truth: the channel-balance artefact, not evidence.
- **POST_ICE_LAM / POST_GL_LAM**: same trade as ICE/GL. POST_GL 1.5: sim +0.006 ± 0.003, library -0.003, `lib_nogate` -0.004.
- **FUSE_ENG_K 25**: sim +0.0005 ± 0.0003 (16/1), nothing elsewhere. Too small to matter.
- **FILL_25**: no change in any regime (every list already has 25 entries).
- **FUSE_ALPHA / FUSE_KRR**: α 0.4 +0.039 and KRR 1 +0.039 in the sim, but +0.077 and +0.119 in `c2_leaked`: a gain
  that is 2 to 3 times larger when the ranker's memorised order is left in is the leak signature. KRR 1 also gains
  +0.013 on library hits (12/0), but that overlaps with the library gate: gate alone is 16/0, gate + KRR 1 is 17/0.
  Not recommended, as instructed; α 0.8 and KRR 10 lose in the sim.

### 3.1 Why the popularity gain cannot be trusted

In val1 the true structure is the most popular same-formula candidate for 171 of the 234 molecules whose truth is in
the base list. The median popularity score of a truth is 13.2; the median of the candidate pool is 2.1 and its 75th
percentile is 3.0; the least popular val1 truth (3.3) is still above that. The gain is positive in every stratum
(pop 0.25: +0.027 where the truth is the most popular isomer, +0.018 for 2nd to 3rd, +0.009 for the 12 molecules at
4th or lower), so val1 contains no population on which popularity could lose. The LB is the only evidence on real
compounds: POP_MU 0 → 0.15 (with frag gap) gave +0.005 where the sim promised +0.044.

## 5. Recommendations (at most 3, ranked)

Overrides are relative to the default CFG, in the format of `configs/*.json`.

**1. Half-strength library gate**
```json
{"POP_MU": 0.15, "FRAG_LAM": 0.5, "FRAG_MODE": "gap", "ICE_LAM_LIB": 0.5, "GL_LAM_LIB": 0.5}
```
- Library: 0.9941, +0.0196 ± 0.0050, 16 wins / 0 losses. Class-2 sim: +0.0002 ± 0.0004. Qualifies as `lib+`.
- Top-1 changed vs v2 (`compare_configs.py`): library 14 of 400; val1 0 of 250 (top-25 order changes in 8). In the shuffled sim 2.5 of 250 on average.
- Trustworthy: the library result. Real spectra of real test-like molecules, 16/0, and the mechanism is plain (ICE/GL
  re-orders a list whose top-1 was already right). It only touches molecules with lib_max ≥ 0.9.
- Biased or thin: "no harm to the sim" rests on 8 molecules. Exact hits are easier than hidden class-1 molecules.
- Size if it transfers: share of hidden molecules that are exact-style hits × 0.0196, so +0.004 / +0.007 / +0.010 for
  shares 0.2 / 0.35 / 0.5, which is 2 to 4 LB molecules. If hidden library matches rarely reach 0.9 it is a no-op (0.404).
- Variant: the `v4a` preset (gate 0/0) recovers 2 more exact hits (1.000) but halves the 8 analog hits; see 4.1.

**2. Gate + POP_MU 0.25**

## 6. What the harness cannot tell us

1. **It failed its own calibration.** It does not see why v3b lost 0.014, so it cannot rule out a similar loss for any config here.
2. **How many hidden molecules the library gate touches**, and how many of those are exact hits versus analog hits.
   The only analog hits available are 8 molecules.
3. **Class-1 molecules that are not exact hits.** `runs/vis` has lib_max ≥ 0.988 and a ranker that is right 400 times
   out of 400. `runs/valc1` is the missing regime. It will be leaked like val1 (the v1 ranker saw those spectra), and
   the shuffle would also erase the legitimate library-match order, so plan to read it in both modes as a bracket.
4. **Anything about channel balance** (FUSE_ALPHA, FUSE_KRR, FUSE_ENG_K, FUSE_SKIP_LIB): engine-2 lacks the truth in val1.
5. **The right weight of any re-scorer against the ranker.** In the sim the ranker is noise inside a formula group, in
   `c2_leaked` it is an oracle; the hidden test is somewhere in between and the two brackets disagree in sign for ICE/GL.
6. **Whether popularity helps on uncommon compounds**: val1 has none.
7. **LB-scale effects.** Recommendation 1 is worth 2 to 4 LB molecules at best and the paired LB noise is about 4, so
   a single submission will not confirm or refute it.
