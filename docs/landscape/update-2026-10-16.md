## 8. What to build on, ranked

The public line has nothing left above 0.451; the four teams at 0.48 to 0.495 publish nothing, and MarvinTMB said on
10-09 that no extra data is needed. Candidates for us, best first [C]:

1. **Make the selected submissions reproducible under the 10-14 rule.** Weights without original training code
   (`fpnet_full1`, v3 `fpnet_0/1`, v4b `fe_A/B/D` and ranker; `prvsiyan/casmi26-fp-models-v2`) are now stated "not
   allowed", with checks on "any and all submissions". Our clean chain (`clean-chain-plan-2026-10-10.md`,
   `ownfp-2026-10-10.md`, `pcsel-2026-10-14.md`) is the route; v71b shows the cost of one swap (−0.012). Takes: one
   question to the host on whether published-but-unlicensed datasets *with* training data but without training code
   are acceptable, then own rebuilds of each network (GPU days) and paired validation runs. Protects the score; adds none.
2. **Engine arbitration** where engine 1 and engine 2 disagree on rank 1 (the general form of E2R2). Our budget study
   bounds it at +0.01 to +0.02 realistic; E2R2 took +0.001 to +0.002 of it. Takes: a scorer for the two first rows from
   features neither engine uses (fragment score, simulator agreement, popularity), fitted on the 2,084 stored fused
   lists, then one validation run; CPU only.
3. **Class 3 generation.** Host: class 3 "must be predicted de novo"; participant estimates put it near half the public
   part; our probes leave about 45% of answers in none of our rows (`coverage2-2026-10-10.md`). Our rule-based analogs
   were neutral (v62a/b) and the learned generator did not pass its gate (`fpgen-2026-10-12.md`). Takes: a generator
   that passes its gate, then candidates at fixed late slots. Largest headroom, lowest odds.
4. **Engine-2 seed bagging** (+0.003, one pair): a free CPU change inside the embedded engine; only behind a
   validation run, since it is below one answer.
5. Not recommended: further rel threshold tuning, rank-3 / supported hedges, shields, tails, the five dmitriigluzdov
   refinements, anything from the private ncorp14 dataset.
