## 5. The ensemble question

### 5.1 What v18's final list contains that our engine-2 list does not

Engine 2 is identical, so the question is what else v18's list carries. Read from its cells 6 to 9 [V]:

- **its v1 stage**: the public ranker's list without the join, popularity 0.15, no forward re-rank. In our pipeline
  this is one of the two chains of the rank average below `lib_max` 0.7 (with joined rows in it) and the whole list
  above 0.7: present, with another weight;
- **its PubChem stage**: the popularity-ordered list in fixed slots, and the promotion. The popularity-ordered rows
  are candidates our list does not have; the join takes the best 100 by f·z instead;
- **its forward stage**: GLACIER alone, [M+H]+ only, after the fusion: the same scores with other weights;
- **its priors**: the same popularity table at 0.15.
