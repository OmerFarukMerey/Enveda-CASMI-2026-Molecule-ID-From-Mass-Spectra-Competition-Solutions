- **Notebook that uses it: none.** `kernels list --dataset` returns nothing for the heads and nothing for
  `ahmedberatozer/casmi26-dreams-ssl-readiness-v1`; the author's last notebook is `casmi26-v4q-inference` of 09-30;
  no pulled notebook contains the slug. **Leaderboard score: none.** The author's team is at 0.399 with one more
  submission (10-05 15:07) and no change. [V]
- **Claim, unchanged** [C, card]: NP209 MRR@25 0.282 → 0.367 for the specialist against the "general hard ensemble"
  (+0.084, CI 0.046 to 0.124); "General key/scaffold Test2 regressed"; "These are not leaderboard scores"; manifest
  status `SOURCE_AND_OWN_HEADS_READY_NO_LOCAL_INFERENCE`.
- **What the code does** [V, `code/joint_retrieval_worker.py`, `joint_fusion.py`, read not run]: only molecules with
  a measured `[M+H]+` spectrum (at most 3 spectra per molecule, at most 1,000 peaks); embeds them with the frozen
  DreaMS SSL encoder (1,024-d) on the GPU; candidates = every row of `casmi26-v2-pool` (asserts 710,701 rows and a
  file hash) within 10 ppm of the measured neutral mass (cap 4,096) **plus any `candidate_smiles` passed in the
  request**; Morgan-4096 of each candidate through the head; score = mean cosine over spectra and the two seeds;
  returns the best 32 pool keys and a score for every candidate, extras included. Fusion: a third RRF term with
  weight 0.35 and K 3 next to engine 1 (1.0) and engine 2 (0.6); strong library hits are left alone.
- **What wiring it into `solutions/fusion-v4n` would take.**
  - Inputs: the heads (31 MB) and `ahmedberatozer/casmi26-dreams-ssl-readiness-v1` (**1.27 GB**: official DreaMS SSL
    weights, source pinned at `dbec3a0`, an inference draft). `casmi26-v2-pool` is already attached. Neither was
    downloaded here (the second is above the 200 MB limit of this task).
  - Cells: one new cell after `nb/05_v1_engine.py` that writes the request JSON (molecule ids, optionally the 100
    joined SMILES as `candidate_smiles`) and runs the worker in its own process, as `nb/03_engine2.py` and
    `nb/04_pubchem.py` do; a third term in `fuse_rrf` of `nb/embed/fusion_core.py` behind a new flag (default off);
    the dataset pair in `tools/build_notebook.py`.
  - Run time on a T4: **not documented and not measured by anyone** (the manifest says no local inference was run).
    The work is about 1,200 DreaMS forward passes and two small MLP passes over at most 4,096 candidates per
    molecule; the worker enforces its own deadline [V code]. Whether DreaMS's dependencies install offline on the
    current Kaggle image is undocumented; the readiness dataset ships source, not wheels.
  - Licences. Heads: "Other, not CC0"; "trained on held CASMI26 competition/library data" [C card]. DreaMS weights
    CC BY 4.0, source MIT. **Host, 10-05: "DreaMS weights are allowed" and artefacts trained on `train.parquet` are
    fine** [V forum 745604, 745841], which removes the two licence objections of yesterday. Nothing here is flagged
    non-commercial beyond the "Other" label; nothing is NIST-derived per the card (DreaMS SSL is pretrained on GeMS;
    "External SSL pretraining overlap unknown" is the card's own caveat).
  - **Undocumented**: where the 19,000 "NP-associated" and 5,613 "synthetic-only" training pairs come from (the card
    gives counts, not sources; "library data" could include a non-train library: **until the author says, the NIST
