### Integration sketches for the top three

**1. Depositor-group bit for joined rows (and, second, the tier as engine-2 candidates).**
- *Dataset*: `ngdminh31/casmi26-pubchem-tier2-fp-public`, CC0. Only `tier2_meta.pkl` (619 MB) is needed for the bit;
  the 6.27 GB fingerprint file only for the engine-2 variant. For Kaggle a derived table of ours (InChIKey14 →
  bitmask, a few tens of MB) would be attached instead; provenance goes into the solution README.
- *Cell*: `nb/04_pubchem.py` already produces the PubChem-only list and `nb/embed/pc_join.py` builds the joined
  records. A lookup `key → group bit` there, used (a) as one more term of the join's selection order (next to `f·z`)
  or (b) as a feature for our second ranker pass. (b) needs a re-fit of `casmi26-ours-ranker-fs`: its 165 features
  have no such column, and the public ranker cannot take one.
- *Run time*: a dictionary lookup; nothing measurable.
- *Interaction*: with the join, it changes which 100 rows are joined or how they are ordered among themselves; it
  must not raise the number of pool-answer lists topped by a joined row (our standing rule). With the second ranker
  pass (`RANKER='fs_avg'`), variant (a) leaves the ranker untouched; variant (b) is a new ranker.
- *Cheapest check before a submission*: download `tier2_meta.pkl` (619 MB, above this task's limit: needs approval),
  then on the stage dumps we have (`pc_deep.json` of the mixed-regime and panel replays) count how often the answer,
  when it is a PubChem-only row, carries bit 1 or 2 against the 100 joined decoys of the same molecule, and whether
  the bit still separates them after conditioning on our popularity value. An hour of CPU, no GPU. If the bit adds
  nothing beyond popularity, stop. The engine-2 variant should not be built before that: it is the direction of
  `eng2-join-2026-10-04.md`.

**2. ISDB as a gap-only forward term.**
- *Dataset*: `aminvafaei/casmi26-e045-isdb` (251 MB, CC BY 4.0), or our own build from Zenodo 14887271 if the choice
  of the 148,935 keys matters. The index is already in `scratch/landscape7/files/` (3.4 MB).
- *Cell*: `nb/07_rescore.py`: a third score dict next to `ice.json` / `gl.json` (cosine or entropy similarity of the
  measured spectrum to the stored spectrum of each candidate key, positive or negative table by ionisation mode);
  `nb/embed/fusion_core.py`: a term behind a new flag, default off, applied like `FRAG_MODE='gap'`, i.e. only for
  molecules that ICEBERG / GLACIER cannot score (negative-only molecules, 7.2% of the test per our forward-model
  study). The plan of `forward-models-2026-10-07.md` 4.7 applies unchanged.
- *Run time*: one `np.load` of 475 MB and a few hundred spectrum comparisons per molecule on the CPU: seconds in total.
- *Interaction*: joined PubChem rows are mostly not in LOTUS, so the term scores pool rows and leaves joined rows
  unscored; in gap mode a missing score must mean "no information" (as `FRAG_MODE='gap'` does), otherwise it becomes a
  bonus for pool rows. It does not touch the second ranker pass.
- *Cheapest check*: no download beyond the index. Join the 148,935 keys with the candidate keys of our stage dumps:
  the share of negative-only molecules whose answer **and** at least five same-formula competitors are covered. If
  that is a handful of molecules, the item is closed without a replay. Only if coverage is material: download
  `peaks.npz` (251 MB, approval) and replay the gap term on the negative-mode training molecules (CFM-ID was not
  trained on our libraries' timsTOF data, but METLIN overlaps public structures: the local number is an upper bound).

**3. Full DreaMS backbone as an independent score for the base list.**
- *Datasets*: `ahmedberatozer/casmi26-full-pc-backbones-20261006` (962 MB, licence "Other"; DreaMS weights CC BY 4.0,
  host-approved) plus the DreaMS source of `ahmedberatozer/casmi26-dreams-ssl-readiness-v1` (1.27 GB).
- *Cell*: a worker process after `nb/06_base_lists.py`, as `nb/03_engine2.py` runs engine 2: embed each molecule's
  spectra (101 peaks, precursor token), project, score the base-list candidates (pool and joined rows alike) by
  cosine to the Morgan-4096 projection, mean over spectra and the two seeds (`factorial_inference.py`,
  `alignment_model.py` in the dataset; read, not run). Output a score dict; use it as a feature of our second
  ranker (a re-fit) or as a `z`-term in stage A behind a flag.
- *Run time*: about 1,200 backbone passes of a 116 M-parameter transformer plus small MLPs: minutes on a T4 [C
  estimate]; the offline install of the DreaMS source on the current image is untested by anyone.
- *Interaction*: it is the first public scorer trained against PubChem candidates, i.e. against the decoys the join
  adds; that is the reason to look at it at all. Used as a stage-A term it re-orders joined rows against pool rows,
  which is where we have lost before; as a ranker feature it has to be produced for the 6,687 training lists too,
  and there the model has seen every molecule: the feature would be leaked in training and clean on the test, the
  opposite of what `fs_pass` was built for.
- *Cheapest check*: none that is clean locally, because the checkpoints were trained on all of train. The only
  honest first step is a Kaggle commit run (no submission) on the 12 smoke molecules to learn whether it installs and
  how long it takes; after that it would need its own fold-safe re-training to be usable in our ranker, which is a
  day of T4 per seed by the card. On this evidence it ranks third and is not worth a submission slot now.
