**What the overlay does, exactly** [V cell 31]. It saves v80's `submission.csv`, rebuilds v80's final list per
molecule from the same run's objects, asserts that the rebuild equals v80's output byte for byte, then builds the
treatment: `metric_fusion(pre-fusion list, engine-2 list, limit=window)` (one vote per metric key, α 0.6, offset 3),
then
- `if ice and gl`: GLACIER alone re-ranks same-formula groups of the whole shortlist (v80's branch, unchanged; the
  ICEBERG dict is passed as `{}`);
- `elif ice_fallback and ice and not gl`: `ice_fuse.rerank(..., ice, lam=ICE_LAM)` with `ICE_LAM` 1.0 (new);
- else the fused order;
then the first 25, topped up from the overflow and v80's pre-overlay list. An ICEBERG dict of nulls (molecule
without a covered spectrum) passes the gate and does nothing.

**"ICE fallback" is therefore not a failure handler.** It does not react to an ICEBERG crash, a timeout or an
adduct ICEBERG cannot simulate. It uses ICEBERG where *GLACIER* is absent, and GLACIER is absent by the line's own
choice (`gl_items = ... if s.get('adduct') == '[M+H]+'`, wangpenghua v29). Both runners cover the same spectra:
positive mode, [M+H]+ or [M+Na]+ (`COV` in `ice_runner.py` and `gl_runner.py` [V]).

## 4. How often our forward stage has no score, and what we do then

**Rules in our code** [V `nb/07_rescore.py`, `nb/embed/fusion_core.py`, `fuse.py`, `gl_fuse.py`]:

| Situation | What happens in v59a |
|---|---|
| Molecule has no positive [M+H]+ / [M+Na]+ spectrum (negative mode, [M+NH4]+, [M+K]+, …) | both runners return nulls. Stage A and stage C skip the forward re-rank; the fragment 'gap' term (`FRAG_LAM` 0.5, MetFrag-style parsimony) re-ranks same-formula groups instead. E006: nothing at all |
| Candidate without a same-formula partner | not sent to the models (by design); it cannot move |
| Candidate unscored inside a scored group (prediction failed, > 100 heavy atoms) | z = 0 in its group = neutral; a group needs two scored members to be re-ranked |
| Library hit (`lib_max` ≥ 0.9) | forward weights 0 (`ICE_LAM_LIB`, `GL_LAM_LIB`) |
| GLACIER has no score, ICEBERG has (GLACIER crashed or stopped at `GL_BUDGET`) | ICEBERG alone, all three re-ranks. This is E006's fallback; we always had it |
| **ICEBERG has no score, GLACIER has** (ICEBERG crashed after its null shell was written, or stopped at `ICE_BUDGET`) | stage A: skipped (the gate is "ICEBERG has a non-null score"), fragment term instead. PubChem list and stage C: GLACIER alone is computed, then **overwritten by the fragment term** (its gate is "ICEBERG does not cover"). Net: GLACIER unused. With `FWD_GL_ALONE`: GLACIER alone in all three, no fragment term |
| ICEBERG package cannot be imported or its site install fails | both models off (they share the site); `FAILS` records it, the run continues unless `STRICT` |
