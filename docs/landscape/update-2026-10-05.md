### 3.1 FRIGID (`kvswim/frigidckpts-casmi26`, `frigidsrc-casmi26`, `frigiddeps-casmi26`, `-v2`)

- **What it is.** A de novo structure generator: a masked diffusion language model ("DLM", BERT-896, 249.5 M
  parameters) that writes SAFE strings conditioned, by cross-attention, on a 4096-bit radius-2 Morgan fingerprint
  predicted from the spectrum by a MIST encoder and on the precursor formula. Optional inference-time scaling re-noises
  and regenerates under the guidance of an ICEBERG forward model. Paper: Bohde et al., "FRIGID: Scaling Diffusion-Based
  Molecular Generation from Mass Spectra at Training and Inference Time", arXiv 2604.16648, ICML 2026 poster.
  Repository: `github.com/coleygroup/FRIGID`. Checkpoints: Zenodo 19685145 (one 2.8 GB tarball, md5
  `1059c193…`, the same md5 the Kaggle card quotes). [V pages, V card]
- **On Kaggle.** `DLM.ckpt` 2.68 GB, `mist_canopus.pt` 90 MB, `mist_msg.pt` 114 MB; **no ICEBERG checkpoints**, so only
  FRIGID-base can run. Source: `dlm/` (20 files) and `mist/` (18 files), Apache-2.0 headers (NVIDIA). Wheels are cp313;
  the v2 set ships **CPU** torch 2.6.0 because, per its card, the CUDA wheel was "incompatible with Kaggle T4". The card
  calls it the "CASMI 2026 M4 generation channel". [V file lists, cards, source headers]
- **Training data and NIST.** Repository: MIST (canopus) on NPLIB1, MIST (msg) on MassSpecGym, ICEBERG "trained on the
  training split of each benchmark", the decoder on "up to 230 million unlabeled structures" (the paper says up to one
  billion structures from the SAFE corpus, with benchmark test and validation molecules removed). NIST is mentioned
  neither in the repository README, nor in the paper as fetched, nor in the Kaggle source (`grep -i nist`: 0 hits).
  **NIST: no, per the paper and repository** [V pages]; the Zenodo record itself carries no training statement [V].
  `update-2026-10-04.md` had this as unknown.
- **Licence.** Code Apache-2.0; **checkpoints CC BY-NC 4.0** (Zenodo); Kaggle datasets "unknown". Non-commercial weights
  fall under the unanswered forum questions 745072, 745615, 745841. [V]
- **Evidence.** Benchmarks with the **true formula given**: FRIGID-base Top-1 16.09% on MassSpecGym and 19.80% on
  NPLIB1; with scaling 18.29% and 25.03%. No competition score: kvswim's team is at 0.068 after 5 submissions and no
  public notebook imports it. [V pages, V leaderboard]
- **Adoption.** Inputs: spectrum, adduct (MIST has an 8-way adduct one-hot; the samplers default to `[M+H]+`), and the
  formula, which we do not have: it would be taken from the formulas of our top candidates or from a mass
  decomposition, one generation run per formula. MIST needs its own preprocessing (sub-formula assignment of peaks).
  Speed: 6.58 s per spectrum for FRIGID-base on one RTX 6000 Ada, 86 s with scaling (paper); the number of samples
  behind the 6.58 s is not stated; the repository default is 128 candidates per round. On a T4 expect several times
  slower [U], so a budget of 60 to 90 minutes covers a few hundred molecules at one formula each: it must sit behind a
  gate (low `lib_max`, low confidence). Needs GPU torch wheels for the pinned Python 3.12 image (the packaged ones are
  cp313). Integration point: a third candidate source next to the pool and the PubChem join, entering the base list
  with pool id −2 like joined rows.
- **Risks.** (a) Our own result: every change that put more outside candidates above pool candidates lost (0.406,
  0.416, 0.417), and wangpenghua's wider generator lost 0.024; generated structures are more decoys unless they are
  held to tail slots. (b) A local test is leak-prone: the decoder's corpus only excludes benchmark test molecules, so
  our held-out structures are probably in it (pitfall 6). (c) The benchmark numbers assume the true formula.
- **Verdict: later; a local recall study first, no submission yet.** It is the only public route to class 3, and its
  NIST status is now acceptable, but there is no score evidence and the licence question is open.

### 3.2 takumuhata: `casmi26-pc-hardneg`, `casmi26-colab-scripts`, `casmi26-con-v2-seed6100x`, `casmi26-v2ckpt-6100x`

- **The model ("con" = contrastive).** [V `colab_cell_con_v2b.py`] A dual encoder: spectrum side = per-peak MLP on
  Fourier features of m/z, neutral loss and neutral mass plus intensity, 16 learned attention-pooling queries, 256-d
  output; molecule side = a two-layer MLP on a 2048-bit radius-2 Morgan fingerprint. InfoNCE at τ 0.07, batch 512, 45
  epochs, AdamW 1e-3 one-cycle, three seeds. Negatives: in-batch plus `K_HARD` 16 **other training molecules within
  0.02 Da** of the neutral mass. Trained on `takumuhata/casmi26-con-train-ext` ("train + ext": `train.parquet` plus the
  GNPS + MassBank + MoNA merge of `casmi26-extlib`). `con_s6100Xv2.npz` are final weights (8.7 MB), `ckpt_s6100X.npz`
  the resumable intermediate ones. There is no evaluation code or metric in the scripts.
- **The hard-negative pack.** [V arrays] `pc_hardneg_pack.npz`: `mol_key` (406,242 IK14, the train + ext molecules),
  `pc_hard` (406,242 × 16 indices into the PubChem block; 99.4% of rows full), `pc_fp` (5,264,440 × 256 bytes = packed
  2048-bit fingerprints), `pc_key` (5,037,739 distinct IK14), `n_top` = 8, `ppm` = 5. Subtitle: "PubChem same-formula
  isomer hard negatives for contrastive training". No SMILES, no build script; the selection rule behind `n_top` is not
  documented [U: probably the 8 most similar plus 8 others per molecule, inside 5 ppm]. The script that trains on it
  (a v3) is not public.
- **Provenance / NIST.** Train + GNPS + MassBank + MoNA, PubChem structures. **NIST: no** per the subtitles [U: the
  record-level licences of the external library are not documented]. Licence CC0 (labels); scripts "other".
- **Evidence.** Team Takumu Hata: 0.173 (21 submissions). No notebook uses the models. **None.**
- **Adoption.** The weights are a weak retrieval model for us (we already have FPNets trained on more). The pack is a
  ready training set for "answer against PubChem isomers" but only as fingerprints; we can mine the same negatives
  from our own PubChem tier, with SMILES and our features, in an hour.
- **Verdict: no** for the checkpoints. **Later** for the idea: a ranker or FPNet fine-tune whose negatives are PubChem
  same-formula isomers is exactly the missing "tell the answer from PubChem decoys" training signal, but our 10-03
  study found a ranker fitted on joined lists indistinguishable after the fusion, and v15a lost 0.014.

### 3.3 `n10705013/casmi26-union-pool`

- **What it is.** [V card] `union_pool.parquet` (30 MB): 489,408 distinct standardised IK14 from COCONUT (475,971
  rows), LOTUS (150,590) and the MassSpecGym molecule universe (28,923); columns `ik14_std, smiles, formula, mass,
  source`. Built for a "density-vs-membership" supply probe (a preregistration document is cited but not public).
- **The useful part is in the card:** it names the earlier probes "COCONUT LB 0.106 / LOTUS LB 0.118 / MSGym LB 0.093".
  These are pure mass-window submissions [U for the scores and the exact ordering rule]. Read with care: LOTUS, three
  times smaller than COCONUT, scores higher, so the score is driven by how few candidates share the window (density) as
  much as by membership; that is what the union probe is meant to separate. No result for the union yet.
- **NIST: no** (structures only). Licence CC BY 4.0. Team IDEA: 0.357.
- **Adoption.** Nothing to adopt: our pool (710,701) already contains COCONUT and LOTUS almost entirely (the
  natural-product table below finds 98% of LOTUS + NPAtlas in the public pool).
- **Verdict: no as an asset; watch the probe results.** They are the only public measurements of where hidden answers
  live.

## 6. What I would test first

A local, read-only-of-the-LB recall study of FRIGID-base, before any integration: about 200 `[M+H]+` molecules whose
answer is only in PubChem or nowhere in our pool, formula taken from our own top candidates (not from the label), 128
samples per formula on the local GPU within the sharing limits. Measure (a) how often the answer's metric key is among
the samples, (b) where the public ranker places it in a list that already holds the 100 joined PubChem rows, and (c)
how many pool top-1s the added rows displace. Use the Zenodo-only Enveda-180 structures (816, absent from train) as
the cleanest set, and state that the decoder may have seen them as unlabeled structures. Go to a submission only if
(a) is well above what the PubChem join already reaches and (c) is near zero with the rows confined to tail slots.
